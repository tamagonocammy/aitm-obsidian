> [!summary] Millie's summary of this arc (2026-11-12)
> The rapture of the Cohort, the "SAI" era of the AU. First look at the overall state of affairs of Pandora, cameos of the main characters.

> [!info] About this synopsis
> Written 2026-11-12 from the notes already in this vault, with a source after each paragraph. It adds no new canon. Everything the files do not yet say is listed under **Still open**.

## Synopsis

The files's picture of Pandora starts about six months before the Siege. Varang's corruption is spreading through the Mangkwan, and she has allied herself with Quaritch. The Mangkwan occupy Bridgehead, and Cpl. [[Miri Sugo]] defects, runs through the jungle, and gets past an ambush by calling out [[Nika]]'s name before he is sent on to the Resistance. *(Era: Blurryface)*

In the same stretch of months, the Astikasya raid Mining Outpost Delta-7. [[Kobeni]] hides behind the air filters, hears [[Sänrri]] speaking Japanese, and defects. The ikran-mounted crossbow is being developed at this time. *(Era: Blurryface)*

Inside the Mangkwan, [[Syayvi]] and [[Tìmeyo]] shelter among them, watch the heresy unfold, and begin gathering the [[The Scattered Ashes|Scattered Ashes]] from within. *(Era: Blurryface)*

The RDA stays a standing threat. Its mining work pushes toward the Western Frontier, and the notes set the company against everything Na'vi life is built on. *(Hostile forces note)*

Sänrri is the center of the [[The Lost Cohort|Lost Cohort]], a circle that formed around her spiritual training under [['Ipxerey]]. Each member arrived with a different wound and a different reason to stay. Sänrri counts as part of the Cohort by ruling. *(Lost Cohort note; Rulings Log)*

The files do not describe how the taking happens. It says only that Sänrri, the Lost Cohort and [[Txafwe]] are captured by the Mangkwan, and that this captivity is the Scaled and Icy era. *(Rulings Log)*

The captives are held for months. They are made to watch sacred rhythms bent into heresy, and Txafwe sees Varang working alongside Quaritch. *(Era: Scaled and Icy)*

[[Rängay]] is made to take part in corrupted ceremonies while her mate is elsewhere and her children wait at home. Her profile says the capture struck at the core of her soul, because she had to watch Eywa's Eye mocked. *(Rängay's profile)*

[[Pxayu]], cut off from her daughter [[Lawarey]], catches herself copying the Mangkwan ceremonies and is horrified at how well she adapts. [[Pamyaw]] loses her instruments, which her profile describes as Eywa's breath being stolen from her. *(Pxayu's profile; Pamyaw's profile)*

[[Apayì]] keeps the others' spirits up. Her small observations about the ash in the cell and about Rängay's voice work as lifelines, reminding each of them who they still are. *(Apayì's profile)*

[[Roneyu]], Mangkwan-born and easy to overlook, helps Sänrri without being seen. The helper clears a path and then slips away that same night, so Sänrri never learns who it was. The Rulings Log links Roneyu to the Keons of AITM. *(Era: Scaled and Icy; Rulings Log)*

The children stay behind. [[Nìla]] and [[Mawpay]] wait at home with their mother Rängay, and Lawarey waits at the Falls. Apayì's knowledge that Lawarey is waiting gives her fierce determination. The files do not say how any of them are cared for while their parents are held. *(Rängay's profile; Apayì's profile)*

What Sänrri learns from the two Toruk Makto, Tunskxe and Yoko'fya, belongs to the next arc, and her return home belongs to the arc after that. *(Rulings Log)*

## Ruled on 2026-11-12

- The Mangkwan kidnap all of them: Sänrri, the Lost Cohort and Txafwe.
- The "main character" cameos are shots from *Avatar: Fire and Ash*, there to show that AITM and the films' story are happening in the same world at the same time.
- **Approved cameos (2026-11-12):** (1) the Mangkwan war party coming home from burning and looting the Wind Traders' airships; (2) Quaritch showing Varang how guns work, which is the moment Txafwe witnesses.

## Ideas to consider (not canon)

> [!warning] These are Claude's suggestions, offered because Millie asked for them. None of this is canon until she says so.

Two shots are approved and listed under Ruled. Three more that the captives could plausibly witness, still only ideas from inside the Mangkwan camp (film details from the Wikipedia plot summary, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avatar:_Fire_and_Ash, and themoviespoiler.com):

3. **The queue.** In the film Varang subdues Spider through her queue connection. If the Cohort watches that happen to a masked human boy, they see the forced tsaheylu used on someone else before it is used on them, and Sänrri's lesson in the next arc has something to answer.
4. **Other prisoners.** The Mangkwan capture Quaritch, Jake and the Sully children, who then escape when Kiri turns the plants on the guards. A glimpse of that chaos from the cages would be the strongest "same world" shot, and it gives the camp a night of disorder.
5. **The camp empties.** The Mangkwan and the RDA leave together for the Metkayina village. A thinned camp is the opening Kaymi and Tsu'lan would need, which hands this arc cleanly to the rescue.

Check against your own timeline before using any of them: the Siege is set weeks after the film's events, so the captivity has to overlap the film's first half for these to work.
