> [!info] About this synopsis

## Synopsis

Dr. Tomori Takamatsu arrives on Pandora with RDA Sci-Ops, and [[Nika]] helps her into an avatar body. She later defects and follows Jake Sully's path. The Era note gives no dates or order for these steps. *(Era: The Dreamwalker Years)*

Her profile describes a moment when her queue first touched the roots of Eywa and she felt understood, but it does not say when that happened. *([[Sänrri]]'s profile)*

The author's summary names a flashback to 2154 and the Battle of the Hallelujah Mountains. No excerpt in the files describe that flashback or the battle, so this part of the arc has no text behind it yet. *(Author's summary)*

The summary says the Astikasya found her. No excerpt describes that moment. The Sänrri profile says the Cohort found her, and that lost people find their way to her, but it does not show the clan finding her. *(Sänrri's profile)*

Under [['Ipxerey]], Sänrri trains as tsakarem. The [[The Lost Cohort|Lost Cohort]] forms around her, made up of [[Rängay]], [[Pxayu]], [[Apayì]] and [[Pamyaw]]. During this stretch she clashes with Rängay and falls for [[Kaymi]]. *(Era: The Dreamwalker Years; Relationship Lore)*

Under the Tree of Souls she undergoes the consciousness transfer and becomes Sänrri for good. The note names "The Line" as the anchor of that change. The scene is planned but not yet written. *(Era: The Dreamwalker Years; Rulings Log)*

By the Great Games, about fifteen years before the Siege, Sänrri is already bonded with Kaymi. The files call Kaymi the love of her life, but no excerpt describes a mating, and the Era note leaves open whether the bonding came before or after the transfer. *(Era: The Dreamwalker Years; Sänrri's profile)*

[[Lì'may]] is rescued from an RDA facility and adopted by Kaymi and Sänrri. The Era note leaves the date of that adoption open. *(Era: The Dreamwalker Years)*

## Who is in it

Sänrri (Dr. Tomori Takamatsu before the transfer): the arc's centre, moving from dreamwalker to tsakarem through the transfer.

Kaymi: the warrior who falls for Sänrri, is already bonded to her by the Great Games, and adopts Lì'may with her.

'Ipxerey: the tsahìk who trains Sänrri as tsakarem.

Rängay: the disciplined ritualist who clashes with Sänrri in training and with Kaymi over Sänrri.

Pxayu: a Lost Cohort member, in the group that forms around Sänrri's training.

Apayì: a Lost Cohort member, in the group that forms around Sänrri's training.

Pamyaw: a Lost Cohort member, in the group that forms around Sänrri's training.

Nika: helps Tomori into her avatar body at RDA Sci-Ops.

[[Kobeni]]: defects after the Astikasya raid on Mining Outpost Delta-7, where she first meets Sänrri. The files do not date that raid against this arc.

Lì'may: rescued from an RDA facility and adopted by Kaymi and Sänrri.

Jake Sully: the path Tomori follows after she defects. The files give him no profile.

## Ruled on 2026-11-12

- In 2154 Tomori was a scientist with the RDA's Sci-Ops in the Western Frontier, alongside Nika, when Jake Sully rebelled and the Battle of the Hallelujah Mountains was fought.
- She escaped in the middle of it. Her workplace exploded in an attack. The image is "Heavydirtysoul."
- Millie has invited ideas for expanding this; see below.

- **Approved 2026-11-12, from Claude's suggestions:**
  - The 2154 escape and the first memory Luka invades in the final battle (her human life, "Heavydirtysoul") are the same scene. Arc 1 plants it; arc 8 pays it off.
  - The explosion comes as the RDA abandons the Western Frontier after Jake's victory. (Check the *Frontiers of Pandora* opening in-game for details before writing.)
  - The image is a rover or a Samson coming apart and burning on the way out, with Nika beside her.
