**By Millie Caicedo**

## Where it sits

The ninth arc, and the last of the nine. It belongs to the era [[Breach — The Final Battle and the Wild Hunt]] and follows [[Siege of the Falls]], whose final battle opens that era. The Wild Hunt Arc is the first major arc after the Siege of Sheltering Falls. Where the Siege was a story of defense, a clan holding its ground against overwhelming force, the Hunt is a story of pursuit: a vow made in the dark and carried across the Western Frontier until it is fulfilled, and nine riders assembling without knowing they were becoming nine.

In Millie's words (2026-11-12): the vendetta against the lost ANAKT fireteam, the contact to the Anurai, The Flying Troupe take the stage, Tsu'lan against Luka, and Sänrri lost in thought, catatonic, with "The Contract."

**Arc theme.** A love too large to stop. A refusal to let what happened to the people you love go unanswered.

**Arc anthem.** "Ghost Riders in the Sky."

**The number nine.** The Siege was broken by nine Spears. The Hunt rides with nine. Luka could calculate every variable and never solve for nine: for people who show up for each other without being asked, for the specific math of found family that does not follow logic and cannot be predicted.

**Visual reference.** The *Kizumonogatari* animation style, described in full at the end of this note.

## Before the Hunt

**The end of the Siege.** In the Cave of Truth, Tsu'lan faced Luka, the Strangler Fig of the ANAKT Fireteam. The fight was real. Luka escaped wounded, and Tsu'lan let him go. He has carried the weight of that decision every day since. See [[Siege of the Falls]].

Luka's last words before disappearing into the Western Frontier: *"There are many of us. Death is meaningless to us Recombinants. Semper Fidelis."* A warning, not a taunt. The Strangler Fig does not waste breath on taunts.

Tsu'lan made a vow. He told no one he was making it.

**What the Siege left behind.** The clan is rebuilding and arguing over whether to keep the human weapons. Tìmeyo and Yawyay have left for the reef. Syayvi and Nì'syu stay, and Sìlpey is coming. Mizi wanders the mist, guiding lost children home, and belongs to neither side.

**The Flying Troupe arrives.** The Troupe comes to Sheltering Falls on Tsu'lan's word. Roneyu comes with them and faces the tsakarem they saved, who does not know them. Tsu'lan tells them: *"You are here now. That is what matters."* Syayvi sits beside Roneyu at the fire. The Troupe stays longer than planned, and when it leaves it takes a new Anurai song with the sound of mist and falling water woven into it. See [[The Flying Troupe]].

**Sänrri after the Siege.** "The Contract" is Sänrri's song after the Siege. Millie's summary places her lost in thought and catatonic during this arc. The files hold no scene, cause or length for this, so it is listed under **Still open**.

The Hunt assembles through convergence, not planning. Each person arrives at the same point through their own thread of loyalty, knowledge or recognition.

## Arc structure

### Part One: The Vow

Tsu'lan tells no one he is leaving. He does not have to. The Nine Spears know him well enough to see the decision already made in his posture, in the particular way he checks his weapons, in the absence of the restlessness he has carried since the Siege ended.

Kaymi sees it first. Kaymi always sees it first. She cannot stop him and she knows it, and the expression on her face when she understands this is a whole story in one image.

The Nine Spears cannot follow. The Falls still need protecting, the clan is still rebuilding, and someone has to hold what was held. Rawìl stands with Tsu'lan the night before and says nothing useful and everything necessary. Tsa'rawm does not say goodbye. He says *"come back."* This is more honest.

Tsu'lan leaves at first light. Alone.

### Part Two: The Bone Sanctuary and the Anurai

Tsu'lan rides to the Anurai, to the Bone Sanctuary, the valley of bone instruments and ancient clan knowledge at the western edge of the frontier. He comes for Yukeyo, who is Anurai born, trained here, and knows this territory the way Tsu'lan knows combat: completely, intuitively, with no wasted motion.

Yukeyo has been expecting him. Not because she was told, but because she is Yukeyo.

She asks one question before agreeing: *"Is this justice or is this grief?"*

Tsu'lan is quiet for a moment. Then: *"Srane. Both."*

She nods. Both is honest. Both she can follow.

The Flying Troupe is at the Bone Sanctuary. They have been traveling, as they always travel, carrying its music across the Western Frontier. They hear the exchange. They do not discuss it among themselves. The next morning, all nine of them are packed and ready.

Teysaw says: *"You are going the same direction we are."* This is not a lie. It is also not the whole truth. Tsu'lan looks at her for a moment and then accepts what is being offered.

Paysìn, without looking up from whatever she is doing: *"I know where he is. I have known for two days. Nobody asked."*

### Part Three: The Ghost

The route to Luka passes through the Northern Ravines, Tsyokanì's territory. He is the Ghost of the Northern Ravines, the fighter who moves through clan territories like weather, who carries a blade and the quality of someone who has already decided something and is waiting for the right moment, and who has not had a home in a very long time.

Paysìn knew where Tsyokanì was too. She knows where everyone is. She says nothing until the group is close enough that the information becomes useful and not merely impressive.

The group finds Tsyokanì at the edge of the ravines at dusk, the time he is most likely to be seen. He is not hiding. He is present in the landscape the way the rock is present, the way the mist is present. He looks at them. He says: *"Leave."*

Tsu'lan steps forward alone. He states the situation without ornament: a vow, a clan that was hurt, a man who escaped. No performance. No appeal to anything except the plain truth of it.

Tsyokanì says: *"You are going to get yourself killed."*

The spar happens because Tsyokanì needs to see what Tsu'lan is made of at the moment when the comfortable options are gone. He needs to know whether the justice holds when it costs something.

It holds. Of course it holds.

*"No,"* Tsyokanì says finally, when they are both breathing hard and something has been established. *"Not today."*

He joins the Hunt.

### Part Four: The Wild Hunt

Nine ikran. The night sky over the Western Frontier. Polyphemus enormous and amber behind them.

The riders are silhouettes, perfectly still. The only movement is the slow pulse of bioluminescent markings in blue, gold and pale green against the gas giant. The only sound is wings on cold air.

The Troupe's ikran fan out behind Kxatu and Hayu. Each carries a rider who chose to be here. Below, in the ravines, the quarry runs. He is fast. He knows this terrain. He has survived worse.

But the Hunt does not tire.

Below, Luka looks up.

He has calculated every variable. He always calculates every variable. The variable he never had a column for, the one that undid Ivan and broke everything they built, is above him now: nine points of bioluminescent light across a sky that does not slow, does not hesitate, and has already decided.

He runs. The Hunt follows.

### Part Five: The End of the Hunt

Tsyokanì drops from Hayu at the moment of exact precision, the two-second window where Luka's route becomes predictable, and lands between the quarry and the next escape. He does not speak. He draws his blade.

Tsu'lan lands behind Luka a heartbeat later.

The fight is real. Luka was built for this. He does not surrender. But there are nine of them and one of him, and above all there is Tsu'lan, who fought him once and let him go and has spent every day since carrying that weight.

Tsu'lan does not make the same mistake twice.

The Hunt ends in the ravines at the blue hour before dawn, when the bioluminescence is fading and the first pale light finds the edges of things.

Tsu'lan holds the kuru.

Luka's last words, spoken before the end: *"There are many of us. Death is meaningless to us Recombinants. Semper Fidelis."*

The same words as after the Siege. A warning, not a taunt. He dies but he warns. The Hunt ends but the war does not.

### Part Six: Coming Home

Tsyokanì stands at the edge of the ravines. He has lived here a long time, in this borderland between completed things and whatever comes after. The ravines have been his home and his exile at once, and leaving them requires deciding which one they were.

Tsu'lan looks back at him. Nothing in the look makes demands.

After a long time: *"I am finished here."*

*"Srane,"* Tsu'lan says.

Nine riders at dawn. The color coming back into the world, the blue first, then the gold. The Falls in the distance, the mist rising, the sound of water carrying across the morning air.

Tsyokanì among them.

## The Falls thread (ruled 2026-11-15)

The arc cross-cuts between the Hunt and the Falls, where the clan holds what is left. The Siege and the Hunt are only days apart. In six beats that run alongside Parts One to Six: Sänrri's silence ("The Contract"); [[Rängay]] shut out of Sänrri's shelter (*"Her song is gone."*); [[Narayra]], Rängay's older sister and the clan's envoy, arriving just after Tsu'lan leaves (*"You decided to show up, after all of this?"*); Narayra as the critic, answered by Kaymi, Tsu'ong, Ketxe, Syayvi (*"You are not wrong about me."*) and [[Lì'may]] (*"Sa'nu is MY SA'NU!"*); Rängay's "Backslide"; and the Hunt's return, where Yukeyo's question and Narayra's sit side by side. Full beats: `_inbox/2026-11-15 Narayra and the Falls Thread.md`.

## Themes

**The vow as the engine of the arc.** The Hunt does not move because of strategy or necessity. It moves because Tsu'lan made a promise in the dark and will not put it down. Everything else assembles around that single immovable fact.

**Nine as the variable that cannot be solved.** The Siege was broken by nine Spears. The Hunt rides with nine. Luka calculated everything and could not account for found family.

**The Ghost coming home.** Tsyokanì has been the Ghost long enough. The arc is the story of the Hunt finding its last rider, the one who was already in the field and follows not because he was invited but because something in him recognizes the grammar of what Tsu'lan is doing. Love that kept going because it had nowhere to stop.

**The warning that outlasts the hunted.** Luka's final words are not defeat. They are data. There are many of us. The Hunt ends but the war does not. Tsu'lan comes home with a fulfilled vow in one hand and a new weight in the other.

## After the arc

Tsyokanì arrives at Sheltering Falls with the nine riders.

'Ipxerey receives him at the entrance, looks at him for a long moment, and says: *"You do not travel alone."*

It is not a question. The tsahìk names something real that no one else has had the spiritual vocabulary to name directly.

*"No,"* he says. *"I do not."*

She steps aside. He walks in.

He is learning, slowly, that being known is not the same as being in danger.

## Notes on visual language (*Kizumonogatari* style)

- **Color.** Almost entirely drained. Night is grey-black and deep blue. Polyphemus is washed-out amber. The only saturated color is bioluminescence, which makes the riders look made of light.
- **Action.** Never shown in full. A moment, then black, then aftermath. The grammar of something already decided being carried out.
- **Layon's sequence.** Breaks the visual language on purpose: the composition tilts, and the one rider is too close, too bright, in the wrong third of the frame. He is the chaos variable. The animation knows it.
- **Tsyokanì's sequence.** He is not there, then he is somewhere else. The camera moves to find him after the fact. It never catches him moving, only arrived.
- **The confrontation.** Hard cut from motion to absolute stillness. The triangle: Luka at the bottom point, Tsu'lan and Tsyokanì at the upper corners. No music. Only natural light, the first grey wash of dawn. Three figures, hard shadows, stone.
- **The final shot.** Almost entirely sky.

## Ruled

- **Luka dies at the end of the Wild Hunt.** Ruled by Millie in October 2026, confirmed 2026-11-12. The Character Database's "escaped/at large" is superseded.
- **The re-forged Luka is discarded.** The version where Luka is re-forged at Bridgehead to impersonate Tsu'lan is not canon.
- **Tsu'lan is the strongest of the Nine in body.** Tsu'ong, Kaymi or Tsa'rawm could give him a fight.
- **Roneyu** is born Mangkwan, escaped unnoticed, and travels with the Troupe. They are AITM's "Keons."

- **Sänrri's silence** falls between the Siege and the Hunt: lost in thought, catatonic. "The Contract" is her song there (ruled 2026-11-12).
- **"The lost ANAKT fireteam"** is a saying. The Hunt's target is Luka. Mizi lives, blind and hurt, almost a ghost (ruled 2026-11-12).
- **Roneyu rides** with the Hunt, "to mend a life lost to ash" (ruled 2026-11-12).
- The line on Luka's note about studying Tsu'lan to impersonate him is removed; it belonged to the discarded version (ruled 2026-11-12).

- **The Falls thread and [[Narayra]]** (ruled 2026-11-15). See above.
- **The Hunt's "nine" is the Flying Troupe;** the Hunt is the Troupe plus whoever joins: Tsu'lan, Yukeyo and Tsyokanì (ruled 2026-11-15).
- **The Siege and the Hunt are days apart** (ruled 2026-11-15).

## Changed from the original

- The Background called Luka the "last surviving member of the ANAKT Fireteam." Mizi survived the Siege and defected, so the line now says only that he is a member of the Fireteam. Ivan, Sua, Till and Hyuna are dead.
- The original Part Four listed ikran named Skar, Kivä, Folrä and Tsyeri and left out Syäwem. The riders in the table now use the character notes' ikran, and the Part Four ikran list is cut down to "the Troupe's ikran" until the count is settled (see below).
- Added from the Breach era note: the "Before the Hunt" section (the Troupe's arrival at the Falls, Roneyu, Syayvi at the fire). Added from the Rulings Log: the Ruled list.

The original has nine riders (Tsu'lan, Tsyokanì, Yukeyo, and six Troupe members), but Part Two says "all nine of them" of the Troupe pack up, and Part Four lists ikran for Yezì (Folrä) and Pakì (Tsyeri) and none for Yukeyo. The Troupe note says seven ikran arrive at the Falls. The Troupe's members in the vault are Teysaw, Txokän, Layon, Sìltsyu, Seytu, Yezì, Pakì, Paysìn and Roneyu. Who rides, and does Roneyu ride?
- [ ] **Ikran names.** Original arc: Teysaw's is Skar, Seytu's is Kivä. Their character notes: Sìkaya and Kxì've. This note uses the character notes. Which is right?
- [ ] **Order of the Troupe's whereabouts.** The Breach era note and the Troupe note have the Troupe arriving at Sheltering Falls on Tsu'lan's word. The original Part Two finds them at the Bone Sanctuary. Do they come and go, and where does the Falls visit sit relative to the vow?
- [ ] **Luka's flight.** The original has him disappearing into the Western Frontier and running in the ravines. His note says he escaped wounded to RDA lines, hiding among the Mangkwan in red paint. Which is it?
- [ ] **Who ends Luka, and what is the kuru.** The original says "Tsu'lan holds the kuru" and does not say whose kuru it is or who strikes the last blow.
- [ ] **Roneyu and Syayvi.** Whether they knew each other inside the Mangkwan is still undecided (Breach era note, Open Questions).
- [x] **When the Hunt happens.** Answered 2026-11-15: days after the Siege. No file gives how long after the Siege Tsu'lan leaves.

> Tsyokanì stands at the edge of the ravines. He has lived here a long time, in this borderland between completed things and whatever comes after. The ravines have been his home and his exile simultaneously, and leaving them requires deciding which one they were.
>
> Tsu'lan looks back at him. Nothing in the look makes demands.
>
> After a long time: *"I am finished here."*
>
> *"Srane,"* Tsu'lan says.
>
> Nine riders at dawn. The color coming back into the world — the blue first, then the gold. The Falls in the distance, the mist rising, the sound of water carrying across the morning air.
>
> Tsyokanì among them.
>
> ---
>
> ## Themes
>
> **The vow as the engine of the arc.** The Wild Hunt does not move because of strategy or necessity — it moves because Tsu'lan made a promise in the dark and will not put it down. Everything else assembles around that single immovable fact.
>
> **Nine as the variable that cannot be solved.** The Siege was broken by nine Spears. The Hunt rides with nine. Luka calculated everything and could not account for found family. The math of people who show up for each other does not follow logic.
>
> **The Ghost coming home.** Tsyokanì has been the Ghost long enough. The arc is the story of the Hunt finding its last rider — the one who was already in the field, who was already part of this before anyone asked, who follows not because he was invited but because something in him recognizes the grammar of what Tsu'lan is doing. Love that kept going because it had nowhere to stop.
>
> **The warning that outlasts the hunted.** Luka's final words are not defeat. They are data. There are many of us. The Hunt ends but the war does not. Tsu'lan comes home with a fulfilled vow in one hand and a new weight in the other.
>

> ## After the Arc
>
> Tsyokanì arrives at Sheltering Falls with the nine riders.
>
> 'Ipxerey receives him at the entrance, looks at him for a long moment, and says: *"You do not travel alone."*
>
> Not a question. The tsahìk naming something real that no one else has had the spiritual vocabulary to name directly.
>
> *"No,"* he says. *"I do not."*
>
> She steps aside. He walks in.
>
> He is learning, slowly, that being known is not the same as being in danger.

> ## Notes on Visual Language (Kizumonogatari Style)
>
> - **Color:** Almost entirely drained. Night is grey-black and deep blue. Polyphemus is washed-out amber. The only saturated color is bioluminescence. It makes riders look made of light.
> - **Action:** Never shown in full. A moment, then black, then aftermath. The grammar of something already decided being carried out.
> - **Layon's sequence:** Breaks the visual language deliberately. The composition that tilts. The one rider too close, too bright, in the wrong third of the frame. He is the chaos variable. The animation knows it.
> - **Tsyokanì's sequence:** He is not there, then he is somewhere else. The camera moves to find him after the fact. It never catches him moving. Only arrived.
> - **The confrontation:** Hard cut from motion to absolute stillness. The triangle. Luka at the bottom point. Tsu'lan and Tsyokanì at the upper corners. No music. Only natural light — the first grey wash of dawn. Three figures. Hard shadows. Stone.
> - **The final shot:** Almost entirely sky.
