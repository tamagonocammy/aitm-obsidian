> Syayvi's story, from her rise, her friendship with the Cohort, Sänrri, and all, until her exile to the Mangkwan with 'Awll and Tìmeyo.

> [!note] Where this arc sits

> [!info] About this synopsis

## Synopsis

The family tragedy starts with the twins' father, whose neglect leads to the death of their mother, Luey. [[Syayvi]] and [[Tìmeyo]] kill him, and both are exiled. The exile gives Syayvi a new name, Tswa'avä, and cuts her off from Eywa. Tìmeyo loses the tsakarem path and the mating once promised to her with [[Kaymi]]. *(Era: Prologue)*

The files do not show Syayvi's rise inside the clan before the exile. Her profile describes her charisma and emotional intelligence only in the years after the exile, so the climb before it has no scenes behind it yet. *(Syayvi's profile)*

The [[The Lost Cohort|Lost Cohort]] were [[Sänrri]]'s closest companions during her spiritual training, and Syayvi later draws them into her cause. The files do not show how Syayvi came to know Sänrri or the Cohort. The Rulings Log makes Sänrri part of the Lost Cohort, and it places the Mangkwan's capture of Sänrri, the Cohort and [[Txafwe]] in the "Scaled and Icy" era. *(Syayvi's profile, Rulings Log)*

After the exile, the twins find temporary shelter with the Mangkwan. There they discover corruption and heresy against Eywa, and they choose to forge a path of their own under the Mangkwan tsahìk, Varang. *(Syayvi's profile)*

Varang's rituals fracture Tìmeyo's connection to Eywa further. Tìmeyo recoils in spiritual horror, while Kerevi, the alter in the same system, watches with a child's fascination and learns which prayers keep them safe without grasping why they matter. *(Tìmeyo's profile)*

[[Nì'syu|'Awll]] takes part in heretical rituals under the Mangkwan and keeps up the Nì'syu persona all the while. Her profile also has her made to aid Quaritch's forces, which she feels as a final betrayal of her true self. *(Nì'syu's profile)*

Txafwe, a Tayrangi rhythm-keeper from the Eastern Sea, is captured by the Mangkwan and sees the same heresy and the same Quaritch collaboration. Trusted ones are torn away from him, and the capture brings back his old fear of being left alone. *(Txafwe's profile)*

[[Yawyay]], from the Ta'unui coastal clan, seeks out the Mangkwan of her own accord. Quaritch burned her village, and she hoped the Mangkwan's rumored work with the humans would give her a way to strike back. Varang's corrupted practices show her that her hope for vengeance is the same destructive cycle that took her family. *(Yawyay's profile)*

Syayvi uses her charisma and emotional intelligence to draw others to her. Those she gathers include 'Awll, Tìmeyo, Txafwe, Yawyay and the Lost Cohort. Together they form The [[The Scattered Ashes|Scattered Ashes]], called Veykawniraä Txeva in Na'vi, a group of escapees from the Ash People. *(Syayvi's profile)*

The Ashes are an exile's found family, held together by shared exclusion and the need to build something real out of displacement. *(Relationship Lore)*

'Awll took the name Nì'syu herself, and she knows who she is. Through tsaheylu, shared during their mating, Syayvi came to know 'Awll's hidden truth before 'Awll had to say it. *(Rulings Log, Nì'syu's profile)*

The files end the arc at the gathering. Syayvi's return to the Falls is Overcompensate, which the Rulings Log places in Prelude to Fire, so this synopsis does not reach it. *(Rulings Log)*

## Who is in it

- Syayvi: the arc's centre, exiled as Tswa'avä, then the one who gathers the Ashes.
- Tìmeyo: Syayvi's twin, who loses the tsakarem path and the promised mating with Kaymi.
- Kerevi: Tìmeyo's alter in the same DID system, drawn to the Mangkwan rituals.
- 'Awll (Nì'syu): took the name Nì'syu herself, knows who she is, and is Syayvi's mate.
- Txafwe: Tayrangi rhythm-keeper, captured by the Mangkwan, later one of the Ashes.
- Yawyay: Ta'unui drummer who joins the Mangkwan willingly and turns against their rule.
- Sänrri: Syayvi's friend in the summary, captured with the Cohort and Txafwe in the Scaled and Icy era.
- Lost Cohort: Sänrri's closest companions in her spiritual training, drawn into the Ashes.
- Kaymi: Tìmeyo's promised mate, whose calling with Tìmeyo the exile severs.
- Luey: the twins' mother, who dies after the father's neglect.
- Varang: the Mangkwan tsahìk under whom the twins forge their own path.

## Still open

- [ ] Whether 'Awll went into exile with Syayvi and Tìmeyo. The author's summary says yes; the Prologue names only the twins.
- [ ] How Syayvi can befriend Sänrri before an exile that the Prologue places before the main story. The files do not show that friendship.
- [ ] How Txafwe and Yawyay came to be Ashes. Txafwe joined the Astikasya for [[Rängay]] and is not an exile in his own story; Yawyay went to the Mangkwan willingly.
- [ ] What Syayvi's rise before the exile looked like. The summary names it, but the files have no scene or detail for it.
- [ ] Why the group is called The Scattered Ashes. No note explains the name.
- [ ] When the Cohort's capture by the Mangkwan happens in the story. The Rulings Log places it in the Scaled and Icy era, and the files do not say whether this arc shows it.
- [ ] Whether Yawyay's burned village and the Ta'unui reef are one place. Her profile says Quaritch burned her village; the Rulings Log says the reef is her home.
- [ ] Where the arc ends. The author's summary stops at the exile to the Mangkwan, while Syayvi's profile also covers life among them and the gathering; this synopsis follows the profile.
