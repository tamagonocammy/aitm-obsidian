### *Txon a aysamsiyul meuiat holan*
*(Or: How the Astikasya Discovered That the Flying Troupe Plays Games They Were Not Prepared For)*

**By Millie Caicedo** · Na'vi dialogue checked line by line with Reykunyu's parser (October 2026)

---

The celebration had been going for hours.

The occasion was simple enough: the Flying Troupe had arrived at Sheltering Falls seven days ago, stayed three days longer than their route required, and showed no particular signs of leaving. Eyfuwen, who loved nothing more than an excuse to make food for a large number of people, had declared this sufficient reason for a proper feast. The Troupe had contributed what traveling musicians typically contribute when they arrive somewhere with full supply packs and generous hospitality in their immediate future: music, acrobatic demonstrations that made the children lose their minds, and three large gourds of something they had acquired at their last stop along the Western Frontier.

Zangke. Aged in deep stone vessels for a full turning of the seasons. The kind that arrived in your chest before it arrived in your head, which was already too late.

Which is how a crowd of people ended up in one of the smaller chambers of Sheltering Falls, the celebration continuing in full noise somewhere above them, absolutely none of them in a condition to make responsible decisions, while Tìfyaw te Zeswa Anaya'ite set down her empty gourd with the particular satisfaction of someone who has been waiting for exactly this moment.

**"Awnga uvan si."**
*"We are playing a game."*

**"Srane! Awnga uvan si!"** Layon agreed immediately, because Layon would agree to any game at any time, and also he had been trying to explain how extraordinary he was for the last twenty minutes and needed a new format.
*"Yes! We are playing a game!"*

**"Kehe,"** said Teysaw, who was already reaching for another gourd.
*"No."*

**"Frapo plolltxe. Nga snolaytx,"** Tìfyaw told her.
*"Everyone has spoken. You lost."*

**"Kawtu plolltxe!"**
*"No one spoke!"*

**"Frapo plolltxe. Nga snolaytx."** Tìfyaw settled herself with great ceremony. **"Ngal lì'ut a'ipu plltxe. Txo tuteo hivangham, tsakrr po näk. Txo frapo hivangham, tsakrr tute a plolltxe näk."**
*"Everyone spoke. You lost. You say a funny word. If someone laughs, they drink. If everyone laughs, the one who spoke drinks."*

Silence.

**"Fìuvanìri frapo snaytx,"** Mìreya said.
*"In this game, everyone loses."*

**"Fìuvanìri frapo yora',"** Tìfyaw corrected. **"Tsa'u lu teng. Pesu lu 'awve?"**
*"In this game, everyone wins. That is the same thing. Who is first?"*

---

## Round One: Layon, Obviously

Layon te Pxortu Voral'itan stood up before anyone could object, which was his general approach to most situations.

**"Oe lu 'awve, taluna oe lu 'ipu frapoto."**
*"I am first, because I am funnier than everyone."*

**"Trr akinä ayoe tok fìtsenget,"** Teysaw said. **"Ngal frarewon fìlì'ut plltxe."**
*"We have been here seven days. You say this every morning."*

**"Ulte frarewon oe plltxe nìngay."** He spread his hands. **"Peu lu ikran a ke new tswayon?"**
*"And every morning I speak the truth. What is an ikran that does not want to fly?"*

Everyone looked at him with the concentrated effort of people determined not to laugh.

**"Pa'li!"**
*"A direhorse!"*

Dead silence.

Then Pakì made a sound like a bird being startled out of a tree and collapsed sideways into Sìltsyu, laughing so hard she could not make a noise for a full three seconds, just shaking.

**"MA PAKÌ!"** said Yezì.

**"Ke tsun oe ftivang! Fya'o a po plolltxe—"** Pakì wheezed.
*"I cannot stop! The way he said it—"*

**"Tsa'u keng ke lolu sìlronsem,"** Teysaw said, but her jaw was tight in the specific way of someone refusing to do something their face wanted to do without permission.
*"It was not even clever."*

Layon pointed at Pakì. **"Po näk. Frapo alahe lu meyp."** He sat down looking like he had personally achieved something.
*"She drinks. Everyone else is weak."*

---

## Round Two: Teysaw, Against Her Better Judgment

Teysaw stood up with the energy of someone who has decided that if this is happening she is going to win it.

**"Tam."** She crossed her arms. **"Krra tsahìk sänume si nìtxan ulte frapo hahaw, tsa'u lu peu?"**
*"Fine. When the tsahìk teaches for a very long time and everyone sleeps, what is that?"*

A beat.

**"Uniltaron."**
*"A Dream Hunt."*

Kaymi, daughter of the Tsahìk, inhaled zangke.

This was unfortunate.

**"MA KAYMI—"** Mìreya started.

**"Oe zo,"** Kaymi said, completely not fine, eyes watering.
*"I am well."*

Atanvi had gone red from her collarbone to her ears trying not to laugh. Her tail was betraying her completely.

Sänrri had put her face in her hands. Her shoulders were moving.

**"Nga herangham srak?"** Kaymi demanded.
*"Are you laughing?"*

**"Oe ferpìl,"** Sänrri said, muffled.
*"I am thinking."*

**"Nga herangham."**
*"You are laughing."*

**"Oe ferpìl nìtrea—"**
*"I am thinking in spirit—"*

Teysaw sat down with the expression of someone who has won something and does not intend to celebrate, because that would be undignified.

---

## Round Three: Pakì Attempts Structure

Pakì stood up. She was still giggling from the previous round.

**"Tam, tam. Oeru lu 'aw."** She took a breath. Failed to compose herself. Took another breath. **"Pesu— pesu—"** She lost it again.
*"Okay, okay. I have one. Who— who—"*

**"Ma Pakì,"** said Yezì.

**"Srane, srane!"** Pakì wiped her eyes. **"Pesu hahaw nìftue frato?"**
*"Yes, yes! Who sleeps more easily than anyone?"*

**"Pesu?"** Tsa'rawm asked, leaning in. He had arrived twenty minutes ago with Txokän, been handed a gourd, and decided this was his event now.
*"Who?"*

Pakì grinned. **"Txolaw!"**

The groan that swept through the chamber was audible. Somewhere in Sheltering Falls, in the warmest available spot, the Spear-maker was almost certainly asleep.

**"Tsa'u ke lu hangvur. Tsa'u lu ngay,"** Mìreya said.
*"That is not a joke. That is true."*

**"Ngay srak?!"** Pakì asked, delighted. **"Nìngay srak?!"**
*"Is it true?! Is it really?!"*

**"Ngay nìwotx!"** said Tsa'rawm, who was family and therefore an authority.
*"Completely true!"*

**"Po li herangham; tsakrr po näk,"** Rängay observed.
*"She is already laughing, so she drinks."*

This was accurate. Pakì was laughing at her own joke with the unselfconscious enthusiasm of someone who finds herself genuinely funny and sees no reason to pretend otherwise.

She drank. She remained laughing. These were not in conflict.

---

## Round Four: Txokän, With Minimal Investment

Txokän te Ivansì Evar'itan had been sitting against the wall with his legs stretched out and a gourd balanced on his knee, participating in this event in the sense that he was present in the same room as it.

Teysaw looked at him. **"Ngeyä alo."**
*"Your turn."*

**"Kehe."**
*"No."*

**"Srane."**
*"Yes."*

A long pause. He looked at the ceiling. Then back at the assembled group with the expression of someone calculating the exact minimum effort required to satisfy a social obligation.

**"Nantang plltxe fyape krra po lu wesek?"**
*"How does a viperwolf speak when it is being subtle?"*

No one had any idea where this was going.

**"…Ke'u. Po lu nantang. Fo ke plltxe."** He picked up his gourd. **"Ngal pamit stolawm. Nga li lu kerusey."**
*"…Nothing. It is a viperwolf. They do not speak. You heard a sound. You are already dead."*

Total silence.

Then Tsa'rawm started laughing. Really laughing, the deep helpless kind, with no dignity to it at all.

**"TSA'U KE LU HANGVUR!"** Kaymi said.
*"THAT IS NOT A JOKE!"*

**"TSA'U LU OERU 'IPU NÌTXAN!"** Tsa'rawm managed.
*"IT IS VERY FUNNY TO ME!"*

Txokän drank his zangke with the composure of a man who made exactly the contribution he intended to and is satisfied.

**"Po holangham,"** he noted, pointing at Sìltsyu, who had in fact made a very small, very soft sound and was now looking at the floor.
*"She laughed."*

**"Tsa'u ke lolu tìhangham,"** said Sìltsyu.
*"That was not laughter."*

**"Tsa'u lolu 'uo."**
*"It was something."*

Sìltsyu drank, because she was honest.

---

## Round Five: Seytu, With Great Seriousness

Seytu te Anku Kxitx'itan stood up and straightened himself to his full height, which was the kind of thing Seytu did whether standing in front of a celebration or in front of a cliff.

**"Oel 'uot holawl."**
*"I have prepared something."*

**"Ngal holawl srak?"** Mìreya asked.
*"You prepared?"*

**"Oe frakrr si fa tìkan."** He cleared his throat once. **"Pelun 'ekong frakrr lu mune?"**
*"I always act with purpose. Why is a beat always two?"*

Everyone waited.

**"Taluna 'aw ke lu 'änsyem."**
*"Because one is not complete."*

Perfect silence.

**"Tsa'u…"** Kaymi started.

**"Tsa'u ke lu hangvur. Tsa'u lu hafyon,"** Mìreya finished.
*"That is not a joke. That is wisdom."*

**"Ulte rey'eng lu mune,"** Seytu said, sitting back down with total equanimity.
*"And balance is two."*

Pakì burst out laughing again. Not at the joke. At Seytu saying it with complete sincerity.

**"MA PAKÌ—"**

**"Ke tsun oe ftivang! Fya'o a po plolltxe—"**
*"I cannot stop! The way he said it—"*

**"Po näk nìmun,"** Yezì said wearily. **"Po frakrr näk."**
*"She drinks again. She always drinks."*

**"Oe uvan si!"** Pakì protested.
*"I am playing!"*

**"Nga lu uvan,"** said Yezì.
*"You are the game."*

---

## Round Six: Roneyu, With Great Effort

Roneyu te Kxaro Venu'itu had been sitting beside Sìltsyu for the better part of the evening, contributing primarily by existing quietly and occasionally accepting a gourd when it was passed to them. When Sìltsyu gently indicated it was their turn, they looked like someone who had been handed an unusual object and was not certain what to do with it.

They stood up. Sat back down. Stood up again.

**"Oel fya'ot ke omum."**
*"I do not know the way."*

**"Frapol fya'ot ke omum,"** Tìfyaw said. **"Tsa'u lu tìkan."**
*"Nobody knows the way. That is the point."*

Roneyu considered this. **"Peu lu…"** they started, slowly, **"…txep a 'i'a nì'aw?"**
*"What is… a fire that just ends?"*

Everyone waited.

**"…Hasey?"** Roneyu offered.
*"…Done?"*

A pause.

**"Tsa'u lu fra'u srak?"** Layon asked.
*"Is that everything?"*

**"Txep lu hasey,"** Roneyu said. **"Tsa'u lu 'änsyem."**
*"The fire is done. That is complete."*

Something happened in the room. It was not quite laughing. It was softer than that: a ripple of warmth, several people exhaling something that became a smile. Seytu, who had said not one hour before that one alone is never complete, looked at Roneyu as if they had solved something.

Mìreya, who did not smile casually, smiled.

Sìltsyu put a hand briefly on Roneyu's arm.

**"Frapo näk,"** Atanvi said, which was not actually the rule, but nobody argued, and everyone drank, and Roneyu sat back down looking uncertain about what had just happened but not entirely unhappy about it.
*"Everyone drinks."*

---

## Round Seven: Sänrri Explains Things

Sänrri te Tsawtral Tanhì'ite, tsakarem, former scientist, person who had once organized seventeen subspecies of Pandoran moss into taxonomic categories for personal satisfaction, was at the stage of zangke where everything was extremely interesting and required thorough explanation. She set the topic down first, as she always did, like an object on a table.

**"Oeru lu 'aw. Palulukanìri, pelun po txonmì ke taron?"**
*"I have one. As for the palulukan: why does it not hunt at night?"*

Everyone leaned in.

**"Taluna krra po zera'u, smar li ke sìlpey."** She nodded. **"Tsa'u frapor lu sìltsan."**
*"Because when it is coming, the prey has already stopped hoping. That is good for everyone."*

Beat.

**"Tsa'u lu…"** Tsa'rawm started. **"Tsa'u lu ngay nì'aw."**
*"That is… That is only true."*

**"Tsa'u lu ngay sì 'ipu,"** Sänrri said, with the confidence of someone who has decided these things are not in conflict.
*"It is true and funny."*

**"Ngay nì'aw,"** said Mìreya.
*"Only true."*

**"Tsakrr pelun nga lrrtok si?"**
*"Then why are you smiling?"*

Mìreya stopped smiling immediately.

**"Pelun po lrrtok soli?!"** Atanvi asked Kaymi, delighted.
*"Why did she smile?!"*

**"Po ke plltxe ayoeru,"** Kaymi said.
*"She will not tell us."*

**"Oe ke lrrtok soli,"** said Mìreya.
*"I did not smile."*

**"Nga lrrtok soli. Oel tsat tsole'a."**
*"You smiled. I saw it."*

**"Ngal ke'ut tsole'a."**
*"You saw nothing."*

**"FRAPO NÄK!"** Sänrri declared, apparently deciding she had won this on a technicality. **"Tsakarem plolltxe!"**
*"EVERYONE DRINKS! The tsakarem has spoken!"*

**"Ayhoren ke lu fìfya,"** Teysaw said.
*"The rules are not like this."*

**"Oel ayhorenit leykatem,"** said Sänrri, serenely.
*"I am changing the rules."*

Teysaw looked at Txokän. Txokän shrugged. Everyone drank.

---

## Round Eight: Tìfyaw, Who Has Been Waiting

Tìfyaw stood up like someone who has been patient for a long time and is now, at last, permitted to stop being patient.

**"Tam. Oeru lu 'aw. Pamtseotu a ke fnan pamtseot, sì yrrap: tìketeng lu peu?"**
*"Alright. I have one. A musician who is not good at music, and a storm: what is the difference?"*

Silence.

**"Yrrap 'i'a."**
*"The storm ends."*

The sound Txewan made was entirely undignified, and she immediately hid her face in Syulang's shoulder.

Layon shouted something. Kaymi was laughing at Txewan laughing. Tsa'rawm had fallen backward. Pakì was simply making noise with no structure to it.

**"MA TÌFYAW!"** Txewan said from inside Syulang's shoulder.

**"Vospxìam oel pamtseot angim nìtxan stolawm,"** Tìfyaw said pleasantly. **"Oeru lu txe'lan."**
*"Last month I heard some very long music. I have a heart."*

**"Frapo näk,"** Mìreya said, because that seemed like the only available response.
*"Everyone drinks."*

**"FRAPO NÄK!"** Tìfyaw agreed, already doing so.

---

## Round Nine: Sìltsyu, Quietly

Sìltsyu te Naksa Mirel'ite stood up when it was her turn and smiled the small, careful smile she kept for situations she was not certain about.

**"Krra ikranìl tawit tsole'a maw yrrap,"** she began, softly, **"pol pelì'ut plolltxe?"**
*"When the ikran saw the sky after the storm, what word did it speak?"*

Everyone waited.

**"…'Oe frakrr terätxaw ngane.'"**
*"…'I was always coming back to you.'"*

No one laughed.

This was because the room had gone very still, the specific stillness of people who have felt something land somewhere unexpected, and several of them were looking at their mates, or at the ceiling, or at their gourds, and none of them had words for it.

Then Pakì, naturally, started crying.

**"MA PAKÌ—"**

**"TSA'U LOLU LOR!"** Pakì wept. **"Pelun po lu sìltsan nìtxan—"**
*"IT WAS BEAUTIFUL! Why is she so good—"*

**"Po frakrr lu fìfya,"** Yezì said, and her voice was completely flat, and she was also not quite looking at anything in particular.
*"She is always like this."*

**"Frapo näk,"** Atanvi said softly, because that seemed right.
*"Everyone drinks."*

Everyone drank. The game had become something else, briefly, and then Layon stood up and announced that he had ANOTHER joke, and the something else dissolved back into noise, which was also right.

---

## The Aftermath

Thirty minutes after the game formally ended, or possibly stopped having rules (the distinction had blurred), everyone was arranged across the chamber in various configurations of comfortable ruin.

**"Tsa uvan lolu fe',"** Teysaw said to the ceiling.
*"That game was bad."*

**"Tsa uvan lolu txantsan,"** said Tìfyaw, from the floor.
*"That game was excellent."*

**"Ayoe snolaytx,"** said Kaymi.
*"We lost."*

**"Ayoel peut snolaytx?"** Pakì asked.
*"What did we lose?"*

No one answered this, because no one could.

**"Ayfizayul awngati ke tslam,"** Rängay said eventually, with the particular gravity of the very drunk.
*"The ancestors would not understand us."*

**"Ayfizayuru lolu ayuvan,"** Atanvi said. **"Sa'nok plolltxe oeru teri fo. Fo lolu fe' nì'ul."**
*"The ancestors had games. Mother told me about them. They were worse."*

**"Fe' fyape?"** Tsa'rawm asked.
*"Worse how?"*

**"Po ke plolltxe oeru."**
*"She would not tell me."*

This seemed right.

Somewhere in the far distance, a very faint sound: Layon, who had wandered off during the recovery period to find someone new to tell about his jokes, bellowing something to a bewildered group of older clan members who had not signed up for this.

**"Zene tuteo…"** Sìltsyu started.
*"Someone should…"*

**"Kehe,"** said Teysaw.
*"No."*

**"Slä po…"**
*"But he…"*

**"Po zo. Po frakrr zo. Layonìl kawkrr tuteot kin."**
*"He is well. He is always well. Layon has never needed anyone."*

Sìltsyu considered this. It was true.

Txokän, who had not moved from his position against the wall in over an hour, said without opening his eyes: **"Fìtsengeri pam lu sìltsan."**
*"As for this place, the sound is good."*

Everyone waited for more. Nothing came.

**"…Srane,"** Teysaw said, after a moment.
*"…Yes."*

**"Sìltsan,"** said Txokän.
*"Good."*

This was, apparently, what he had wanted to say.

---

The night was called, from then on, *Txon a aysamsiyul meuiat holan*: the Night the Warriors Lost Their Honor. Rängay disputed this title on the grounds that honor had not been fully lost, only temporarily misplaced, and she had retained hers throughout. This was not entirely true, but no one felt strongly enough to argue it with her.

Seytu spent the following morning reexamining whether his joke had been funny. He concluded that it had possessed internal logical consistency, which he decided to count.

Roneyu's ***hasey*** was repeated, in various contexts, by several members of both the Troupe and the clan for the next several days, usually softly and usually when something was complete. Roneyu was not told about this. They noticed anyway. They did not say anything about it. They thought about it sometimes, in the way you think about something small that you want to keep.

The zangke was blamed for everything. The zangke had not done anything that the people present had not invited it to do, but this was agreed to be the official position.

Tìfyaw declared herself the winner.

This was also the official position.

---

## Epilogue: The Next Morning

Rawìl found the chamber at dawn. He stood in the entrance and took in the scene: scattered gourds, a dozen and a half adults in various states of horizontal recovery, Pakì asleep sitting up with a smile still on her face for no discernible reason.

Anake appeared at his shoulder.

They looked at each other.

**"Ayoe kawkrr tsauvan si,"** said Rawìl.
*"We are never playing that game."*

**"Mllte oe,"** said Anake.
*"I agree."*

They would. Eventually. That is a different story.

## How the jokes were rebuilt for Na'vi

The English puns do not survive translation, so each one was rebuilt from Na'vi itself or from the clan's own lore. **Layon**: an ikran that does not want to fly is a ***pa'li***, a direhorse (with Tsa'rawm, the clan's direhorse master, arriving one round too late to be offended). **Teysaw**: when the Tsahìk teaches so long that everyone sleeps, that is ***Uniltaron***, the Dream Hunt, from *unil* "dream" + *taron* "hunt"; Kaymi inhales her drink because the Tsahìk is her mother. **Pakì**: who sleeps more easily than anyone? ***Txolaw***, whose legendary laziness is canon; Mìreya's "that is not a joke, that is true" sets up a running gag that pays off in Sänrri's round. **Seytu** and **Roneyu** now share a word: Seytu says one beat alone is not ***'änsyem*** (complete), and Roneyu's fire that ends is ***hasey*** (done), *"tsa'u lu 'änsyem"*, the thing that is complete on its own. **Tìfyaw**'s punchline is *Yrrap 'i'a*, "the storm ends," leaving the bad musician's music implied to go on forever.

**Language notes.** *Pesu hahaw nìftue frato* models *frato* on the attested *atsawl frato* "the biggest" (Frommer's NYT dialog in the project's Na'vi book). *Plltxe* can only take *lì'u* as an object, so Sìltsyu asks *pol pelì'ut plolltxe*, "what word did it speak." *Mllte oe* "I agree" is a canon expression. *Tsakarem* and *zangke* are flagged unconfirmed in Reykunyu, but both are already AITM canon.
