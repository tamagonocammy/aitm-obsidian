*The bright surface hiding "Clancy is dead."*

Sänrri, the Lost Cohort and Txafwe (Umiri) are captured by the Mangkwan and held for months. They watch sacred rhythms twisted into heresy. Txafwe sees Varang working with Quaritch.

**Roneyu, the Keons of AITM.** Roneyu (Crona), Mangkwan-born and invisible, helps Sänrri unseen. They clear a path, perhaps through something that never looks like a person. Then they slip away that same night, unnoticed as always. To the Mangkwan, the Roneyu who vanished simply stopped existing. **Sänrri never learns who helped her**, just as Clancy never knew Keons saved him in "Saturday" by seizing Trash.

**The Toruk Makto.** Hiding and desperate, Sänrri reaches a place where she can make tsaheylu with Eywa. There she meets the **Second and Third Toruk Makto, Tunskxe (Simon) and Yoko'fya (Madoka)**. They are her Neds on her Voldsøy.

**The lesson.** Tsaheylu is a consented bond. The Mangkwan force it to dominate through pain, and pure Na'vi, who never needed to defend themselves inside the network, have no resistance to it. Tunskxe teaches will and Yoko'fya teaches hope. Together they teach Sänrri to **resist a forced bond and then overpower it from the inside**, the way Quaritch, paralyzed, held on through will alone. The Astikasya never initiate a forced bond themselves. Kiri remains the one true exception: she holds "sudo" over the whole Eywa network.

**The escape.** Apayì poses as Mangkwan and gets a message to Tsu'lan, while the Ashes work from inside. **Kaymi (Eri) and Tsu'lan (Yuji) go together** to bring Sänrri out. This is the Torchbearer with one Bandito at her side, and the start of Tsu'lan's personal bond with Sänrri.

The Ashes and the Cohort come home with Sänrri, carrying the warning: **"They are coming."**
