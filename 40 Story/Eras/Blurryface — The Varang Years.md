*Song: "Paladin Strait" belongs to the clan as a whole, with Sänrri as its throughline.*

**~6 months before the Siege: the events of Frontiers of Pandora.**

Varang's corruption spreads among the Mangkwan, and she allies with Quaritch.

**Bridgehead.** The Mangkwan occupy Bridgehead. Cpl. Miri Sugo defects, flees through the jungle, and survives an ambush by saying Nika's name. He is sent on to the Resistance.

**Delta-7.** The Astikasya raid Mining Outpost Delta-7. Kobeni hides behind the air filters, hears Japanese from Sänrri, and defects.

The ikran-mounted crossbow is developed.

**Within the Mangkwan:** Syayvi and Tìmeyo, sheltering with the Mangkwan, witness the heresy and begin gathering the Scattered Ashes from inside.

**OPEN:** Miri's profile says he was ambushed by "the Nine Spears," but Syayvi only becomes the Ninth during the Siege. Either the name existed earlier with a different ninth, or Miri names them in hindsight.
