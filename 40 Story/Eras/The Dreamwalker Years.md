Dr. Tomori Takamatsu arrives on Pandora with RDA Sci-Ops. Nika helps her into her avatar body. She defects and follows Jake Sully's path.

She trains as tsakarem under 'Ipxerey. The Lost Cohort (Rängay, Pxayu, Apayì, Pamyaw) forms around her. She clashes with Rängay and falls for Kaymi.

Under the Tree of Souls, she undergoes the consciousness transfer and becomes Sänrri for good, with "The Line" as its anchor. The scene is planned but not yet written.

**~15 years before the Siege — the Great Games.** Sänrri is already bonded with Kaymi. On the same night at the Heart of the Plains, Rawìl (Atanvi and Mìreya) and Anake (Tsa'rawm and Rewon) are conceived.

Lì'may is rescued from an RDA facility and adopted by Kaymi and Sänrri.
