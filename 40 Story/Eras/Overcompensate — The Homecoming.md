*Sänrri's song; the "we both" is Sänrri and Kaymi.*

Sänrri returns to the Falls changed. She tells the clan about Tunskxe and Yoko'fya, and they take it as a **good omen**: Toruk Makto only rise in times of great sorrow, and two of them chose to speak to the clan's tsakarem.
