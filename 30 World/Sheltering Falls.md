**Description:** Breathtaking refuge carved deep within mist-shrouded Spires. Hidden among towering cliffs, veiled in perpetual fog.

**Features:**
- Intricate woodshaping techniques  
- Trees molded into bridges, walls, sanctuaries, platforms
- Blends naturally into mountainside
- Multiple levels utilizing cliff faces
- Waterfalls providing both beauty and sound masking
- Hidden passages (proved crucial during Siege)

**Atmosphere:**
- Constant mist provides cover
- Bioluminescent plants light pathways at night
- Sound of falling water everywhere
- Temperature-regulated by surrounding cliffs
- Feels both fortress and sanctuary
