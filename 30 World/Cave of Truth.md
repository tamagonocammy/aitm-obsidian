**Status:** Most sacred site

**Location:** Heart of Astikasya territory, deep within mountain

**Features:**
- Vast cavern system
- Waterfalls cascade into glowing pools
- Bioluminescent aquatic life
- **Submerged Tree of Souls** beneath pools
- Rare and powerful connection site

**Uses:**
- Soul transfers
- Naming rites  
- Healing rituals
- Ancestral gatherings
- Direct communion with Eywa
- 'Ipxerey can communicate with ancestors here

**Significance:**
- Waters carry memory
- Mists whisper old stories
- Where Eywa's voice is clearest
- Final boss arena during Siege (Luka confrontation)

**Access:**
- Restricted to spiritual ceremonies
- Tsahìk must approve entry
- Multiple underwater passages
- Some routes known only to elders
