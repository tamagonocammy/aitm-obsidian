## Overview

The **Astikasya Clan** is renowned for their spiritual depth, innovative spirit, and sacred connection to Eywa. Once primarily skilled herbalists, a tragic event reshaped their collective identity, forcing them from ancestral forests to the Spires of the Clouded Forest where they evolved into formidable hunters, visionary artisans, and fearless defenders.

**Core Philosophy:** True wisdom lies in honoring the past while embracing change as a living force.

**Leadership:**
- **Olo'eyktan:** Eyfuwen te Samaya Tìrol'itan
- **Tsahìk:** 'Ipxerey te Samaya Kxar'o'ite  
- **Eyktanay (future Olo'eyktan):** Kaymi te Samaya 'Ipxerey'ite
- **Tsakarem (future Tsahìk):** Sänrri te Tsawtral Hikari'ite

### The Nine Spears of the Mists

See also the group note: [[Nine Spears of the Mists]]

**Purpose:** Elite warrior cadre protecting gentle kingdom

**Leadership:** Kaymi (The Brazen)

**Structure:** Nine members, each with title and ceremonial weapon

**Current Members:**
1. **Kaymi** - The Brazen (Crimson Spear)
2. **Atanvi** - The Merciful (Tender Spear)
3. **Txewan** - The Phantom (Singing Spear)  
4. **Tsa'rawm** - The Thunderous (Scarred Spear)
5. **Rewon** - The Unerring (Balanced Spear)
6. **Tsu'lan** - The Righteous (Immovable Spear)
7. **Syayvi** - The Penitent (Ashen Spear)
8. **Tsu'ong** - The Wise (Tempered Spear)
9. **Ketxe** - The Unbound (Unleashed Spear)

**Philosophy:** 
- "Gentle kingdom ringed in spears"
- Strength serves defense not conquest
- Creates space for life, song, peace to flourish
- Each spear represents sacred vow

**Sacred Vows:**
- Lead with courage (Brazen)
- Heal with compassion (Merciful)
- Guide through shadow (Phantom)
- Endure all storms (Thunderous)
- Strike with precision (Unerring)
- Stand immovable (Righteous)
- Seek redemption (Penitent)
- Counsel with wisdom (Wise)
- Fight unbound by past chains (Unbound)

**Ceremonial Use:**
- Carried during major ceremonies
- Critical battles
- When planted in circle, form symbolic protective ring
- Placed around sacred spaces

**Joining Process:**
- Consecration ritual at Tree of Souls
- Spear blessed by Tsahìk
- Bearer swears oath before Eywa
- Witnessed by clan

### Adaptation Philosophy

## Language Notes

### Family Terms

**Parents:**
- sa'nok - mother
- sa'nu - mom, mama
- sempul - father
- sempu - dad, papa
- sa'sem - parent or set of parents

**Children:**
- 'ite - daughter
- 'itan - son
- 'eveng - child
- 'evi - kid

**Siblings:**
- tsmuk - sibling
- tsmuktu - sibling-person
- tsmuke - sister
- tsmukan - brother

**Extended Family:**
Expressed as relationships:
- "tsmuke sa'nokä oeyä" - sister of my mother (aunt)
- "sa'nok sempulä ngeyä" - mother of your father (paternal grandmother)

### Common Phrases

- **Irayo si** - Give thanks
- **Srane** - Yes
- **Kehe** - No
- **Oe lu [name]** - I am [name]
- **Ma yawne** - My beloved
- **Ma tsamsiyu** - My warrior
- **Ma txe'lan** - My heart
- **Eywa ngahu** - Eywa be with you
