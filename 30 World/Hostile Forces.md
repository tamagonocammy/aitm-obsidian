**The Mangkwan (Ash People):**
- Under Varang's corrupted leadership
- Collaborated with RDA/Quaritch
- Occupied Sheltering Falls during Siege
- Performed heretical rituals
- Some members (like Scattered Ashes) escaped

**The RDA:**
- Ongoing threat
- Know Sheltering Falls location (post-Siege)
- Created ANAKT Fireteam
- Mining operations endanger Western Frontier
- Represent everything antithetical to Na'vi existence
