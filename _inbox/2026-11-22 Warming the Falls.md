# Warming the Falls: research and a proposed custom (proposal)

> [!warning] Proposal, not canon
> Everything in the "Proposed custom" section is a suggestion for Millie to rule on. The research section reports what the sources say and where they stop. Nothing here has been written to the Rulings Log. Scenes: [[2026-11-22 Warm Platform Scenes]].

## What the game and its sources say about the Clouded Forest and the Spires

**Where they are.** The Clouded Forest is a region of *Avatar: Frontiers of Pandora* on the Western Frontier. The Kame'tire healers live in it, in an area called the Hollows. The Spires of the Clouded Forest are a separate, mountainous region from the *Secrets of the Spires* DLC, "near" the Clouded Forest. Sheltering Falls sits in the middle of the Spires and is the main settlement of the Ke'awa Kame'tire, who travel by ikran. The Spires list Spire Falls, Splintered Arches, Stone Tusks, the Jagged Range, Tangled Peaks and Warrior's Rise as subregions, with an RDA site (Grinder Facility Omega) in the north-east. (James Cameron's Avatar Wiki, pages "Clouded Forest", "Spires of the Clouded Forest", "Sheltering Falls", which is a stub.)

**Is it modeled on North America?** Partly confirmed, with a caveat. The Avatar wiki says the Clouded Forest is "reminiscent" of the redwood forests of the Pacific Northwest. A fan account on X says it "took direct inspiration" from the Pacific Northwest. I could not reach a developer interview or an official Ubisoft or Massive statement that says this, so treat it as a strong fan reading, not a confirmed design fact. Ubisoft's own article describes the Clouded Forest only as "foggy and mysterious" and "misty". The Spires are a different region from the Clouded Forest, so the Pacific Northwest comparison does not automatically cover them.

**Cold, snow and seasons.** I found no source that gives the Spires or the Clouded Forest a cold climate, snow, or a temperature system. The words I found are fog, mist and mountains. On Pandoran seasons, I found no official statement. A Learn Na'vi forum thread and a Reddit thread discuss it, and they are fan speculation with unsourced numbers for Pandora's axial tilt (16 and 29 degrees both appear). An astronomy blog argues the tilt would belong to Polyphemus and not to Pandora. So whether Pandora has seasons is exactly where the sources leave it: undecided.

**Plants, animals and materials that a warming custom can use.** From the gamepressure.com biome guide, checked against the biome names on the Avatar wiki:

- Boulderlands (a Clouded Forest biome): large rocks, with Blue Cloud Moss growing on them near Singer's Falls; Rock Orchid; Dawnsheen Branch on top of the Stone Sentinels.
- Cluster Pines (Clouded Forest): Cloud Pine Bark; Echo Stalker packs (blind predators that hunt by sound; tooth, light hide, lean meat); Stangfly Ant Nectar; Dense Bone in the caves.
- Near some Tranquil Clearings in the Clouded Forest: Sailfin Blossom Fiber.
- Hides in the game: Echo Stalker and Arrow Deer give light hide; Bladehead, Sturmbeest and Hammerhead Titanothere give heavy hide. Those last three live on the grasslands and in the bamboo, so the Falls would trade for them.
- Stormgliders patrol above the mountaintops of the Spires and the Clouded Forest.
- Fire Seed is an explosive plant. It makes a poor heat source and a good reason for a safety rule.
- Nantang (viperwolf): the game places them in the Great Roots biome, not the Spires. The wiki also says the Spires have no thanators and no feral wildlife at all. AITM already has Sänume followed by a nantang pack, so this is no contradiction in the story. It only means the pack is an AITM choice, not a game fact.
- The game does not list a heat source, a fireplace, or a warming craft for any clan. I found nothing on how the Sarentu or any other clan stays warm. Everything about heat below is invention.

## What the vault already says about warmth at the Falls

- [[Sheltering Falls]]: "Temperature-regulated by surrounding cliffs", constant mist, waterfalls everywhere. Nothing more.
- [[Astikasya Yellow]]: the root would not grow in "the cold, thin soil of the Spires", and the clan spent "the first seasons" at the Falls without yellow.
- [[The Lake]]: the Northern Reaches are "cold country".
- Mismatch to know about: the vault describes the Falls as carved "deep within" the Spires, built into cliff faces with woodshaped trees ([[Woodshaping]], [[Sheltering Falls]]). The Cave of Truth is the part "deep within the mountain". It does not say the whole settlement is inside the mountain.
- Mismatch to know about: an older project document says Sänrri's ikran is Zìsya, and the vault, the Rulings Log and the project rules say Towa. The vault wins.
- No kotatsu, hearth custom, blanket custom or sleeping custom exists in the vault.

## Proposed custom: the warm platform (working title, English only)

**What it is.** Each family keeps a woodshaped platform with a ring of river stones sunk into the floor. The stones are heated at the clan hearth and carried over in a hide pouch, then fed into the ring one at a time with bone tongs. In the cold weeks the family sits with their feet by the ring and their backs to the wall. It warms feet and hands and leaves everything else in the draft.

**What Sänrri adds.** The skirt. Her kotatsu is a low flat board on short legs set over the stone ring, with a heavy cover hung over the edges to the floor on every side. The heat stays under the board and around the waist. In AITM terms, the clan already has the heat and the sitting. Sänrri brings the trapped-warmth idea from her land, and the clan has the materials and the craft to build it in an afternoon.

**Materials, mapped to the game list above.**

- Stones: Boulderlands rock. *(Proposal: the game never uses rock for heat.)*
- Board: woodshaped (vault canon), with Cloud Pine Bark as edging or underlay. *(Proposal: the game lists the bark only as a crafting material.)*
- Cover: a heavy sleeping hide lined with Sailfin Blossom Fiber weave; the light hides from Echo Stalker hunts are the local choice, and heavy hides arrive by trade from allied clans.
- Possible lining: Blue Cloud Moss for insulation. *(Proposal.)*
- Safety rule worth keeping: never Fire Seed near the ring.

**Na'vi vocabulary.** No Na'vi phrase is composed for the custom. Attested words that could feed a name, from the project lexicon: *sang* "warm", *tskxe* "rock, stone", *tìsom* "heat", *wew* "cold", *tsurokx* "rest", *heyn* "sit", *hahaw* "sleep". My direction: keep the custom described in English until Millie picks a name, then compose two or three options and check them on Reykunyu before showing them. The scenes use only *sa'nu*, *irayo* and *nantang*; *sa'nu* and *irayo* were checked live on Reykunyu on 2026-11-22.
