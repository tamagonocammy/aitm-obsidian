## 1. The Cohort cracks

Source: a Japanese fan's prediction for the *MyGO!!!!! // Ave Mujica* series (January 2027), not the show itself. Translated:

> Something knocks Tomori into a slump → rejected by Tomori, Taki, who believed in "for life" (一生), loses it → Raana calls out Anon's guitar for carrying her anxiety → Soyo points out the ego Taki had unconsciously been demanding from MyGO!!!!! → Anon, worried, shows up at Tomori's house uninvited, sees the lyrics, and explodes → Tomori breaks down.

AITM mapping (Claude):

1. [[Sänrri]] goes silent. (already ruled)
2. [[Rängay]] is shut out by the one person she believed would be there forever; the door turns into rage. (the door is ruled; the rage is proposed)
3. [[Pamyaw]], blunt and following her own rhythm, tells [[Apayì]] that her playing is all fear.
4. [[Pxayu]], who loses herself in what others need, sees it in someone else and tells Rängay what she was really demanding from the Cohort: that they stay exactly as they were, for her.
5. Apayì goes to Sänrri's shelter uninvited and finds what Sänrri has been making alone.
6. Sänrri breaks.

> [!note] Canon note
> Na'vi do not read or write, so Apayì cannot read lyrics. **(Claude)** She could find songcords Sänrri has been knotting in her silence, a song she will not sing aloud; Txewan's Singing Spear is already wrapped in "clan songcords." Needs Millie's ruling.

## 2. The families answer the critic

**(Millie)** The people who have to answer for the most are the leaders, Eyfuwen and 'Ipxerey. 'Ipxerey would defend her greatest asset and in-law, Sänrri: she let a human into the clan strategically, and that human has since reached Tunskxe, the clan's Founder and Second Toruk Makto, and Yoko'fya, the Third. Her line: *"We needed her."*

**The Samayas**
- [['Ipxerey]] (Millie, wording by Claude): *"I did not bring a human into this clan out of kindness. We needed her. She has spoken with the one who founded us. Carry that to the other clans, envoy."*
- [[Eyfuwen]] **(Claude)**: the Founder's line runs through him. Narayra calls Sänrri an outsider; his ancestor chose to speak to her. He says it at the feast, smiling, the way he tells every story.
- [[Mìreya]] **(Claude)**: the strategist with exceptional English, the one who can out-argue a professional envoy point by point, and enjoys it.

**Hitori and Suletta (Millie)**
- [[Atanvi]] **(Claude)**: Sänrri is her sister-in-law. She stands up to Narayra without stuttering once, and everyone at the fire notices.
- [[Txewan]] **(Claude)**: her stutter vanishes when she performs, and Sänrri's voice is gone, so Txewan carries the evening song at the fire, the one the children used to fall asleep to. She does not argue with Narayra. She keeps the song going.

**The Haykxas (Claude)**
- [[Sänume]]: 'Ipxerey's finest student, who "hates humans except Sänrri," snaps at Narayra.
- [[Tsa'rawm]]: loud about it.
- [[Kayan]]: the mowarsiyu, the closest thing Narayra has to a peer; his sense of justice may grant her a point on procedure while he still sides with the clan.
- [[Lamaytxa]]: feeds her wildly exaggerated war stories to watch her face.

**The Ngotxìs (Claude)**
- [[Syulang]]: sits Narayra down and makes her eat.
- [['Eweyn]]: the clan's liaison to the Sky People, the "other" envoy; Narayra has opinions about that.
