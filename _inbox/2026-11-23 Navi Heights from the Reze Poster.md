# Na'vi heights from the Reze-arc poster

Date: 2026-11-23. Status: **accepted** by Millie on 2026-11-23, with a conservative adult adjustment and exceptions for Txewan and 'Eweyn. The ruled numbers are in [[Rulings Log]] ("Ruled on 2026-11-23: Na'vi heights"); where this note differs, the log wins.

## Scale in use

The Nine's heights come from Astra's conversion of 2026-11-12, as Millie pasted it in this thread. The rule is H(Na'vi) = H(human) x 5/3, rounded to two decimals, one factor for everyone with no split by sex. It was calibrated on 1.65 m human = 2.75 m Na'vi. That calibration is a design convention for the AU, not a verified Na'vi average.

For comparison, the Avatar Wiki gives the average male Na'vi at 292 cm and females slightly smaller, about 2.8 m. Some fans dispute that and cite about 9 ft (2.74 m). Under the 5/3 scale, Tsa'rawm at 2.88 m sits about 4 cm under the wiki male average.

Arithmetic check of the Nine under 5/3: Syayvi 1.55 gives 2.58, Txewan 1.56 gives 2.60, Ketxe 1.60 gives 2.67, Rewon 1.65 gives 2.75, Atanvi and Kaymi 1.70 give 2.83, Tsa'rawm and Tsu'lan 1.73 give 2.88, Tsu'ong 1.85 gives 3.08. All match Astra's list.

| Character | Height | Source status |
|---|---|---|
| Denji | 173 cm (180 cm transformed) | Exhibition chart, official |
| Aki | 182 cm | Exhibition chart, official |
| Angel | 155 cm (165 cm with halo) | Exhibition chart, official |
| Reze | 165 to 167 cm | No official figure found. Fan estimates only |

Game Rant lists Denji 183 cm and Aki 190 cm with no source cited. I treat it as unreliable.
