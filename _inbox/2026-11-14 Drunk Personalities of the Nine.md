# Drunk Personalities of the Nine (proposal)

> [!success] Accepted
> Brainstormed with Claude and accepted in full by Millie on 2026-11-14. Recorded in the [[Rulings Log]]. Lines marked *on the page* already appear in the derivative stories "Kava with the Nine" and "The Night the Warriors Lost Their Dignity"; everything else is new.

How much each person changes after too much zangke or kava, from biggest change to smallest. Built from the vault profiles and the two derivative drinking scenes.

## The Nine

### [[Txewan]]: biggest change
Sober, she takes the smallest sip anyone has seen and hides behind [[Syulang]] (*on the page*). Her music mentor is [[Tìfyaw]], whose profile already says she is fond of alcohol and her tipsy antics are legendary. Drunk Txewan turns into Tìfyaw: she climbs on things, plays too loud, tells strangers they are beautiful. Her stutter vanishes the way it does when she performs. The next morning she wants to dig a hole in the moss and live in it. This is why she drinks so slowly.

### [[Atanvi]]: big change
The stutter goes away completely and she starts giving speeches with total conviction, including the "My point still stands" bit with [[Tsa'rawm]], which ends with both of them, then [[Txewan]], crying in a pile about how beautiful women are. One word from [[Mìreya]] and the stutter comes back.

### [[Rewon]]: big change
She is the one who says "Do not get drunk!" (*on the page*). Drunk Rewon is giggly and clingy with Tsa'rawm, says out loud every soft thing she thinks about him and the children, and denies all of it in the morning with a straight face.

### [[Ketxe]]: big change
Sober, her gentle manner hides a mind always working out blast radii. Drunk, she stops hiding it and explains seed-pod chemistry to the circle with pure joy while everyone quietly moves the fire away from her.

### [[Tsu'ong]]: medium change
Already self-mocking about the duel ("I truly was a moron," *on the page*). Drunk, he tells the duel story himself, louder each time, and the mud he fell in gets deeper with every telling.

### [[Tsu'lan]]: medium change
Already warm, so the change is in volume. He challenges everyone to arm-wrestling, then falls asleep sitting upright mid-match, and nobody in the clan can move him.

### [[Kaymi]]: small to medium change
"Kaymi, who did not get sentimental, had gotten sentimental" (*on the page*). The steady one goes soft and tells people she loves them, Sänrri most of all, then acts like it never happened.

### [[Tsa'rawm]]: smallest change
He gets louder until he suddenly gets sincere, as in his thank-you to Ketxe (*on the page*). Drunk Tsa'rawm is sober Tsa'rawm with fewer brakes.

### [[Syayvi]]: depends on the era
In the Siege era she is pregnant with Sìlpey and sets her cup down empty (*on the page*). A drunk Syayvi belongs to the flashback arc, Scattered Ashes, Burning Hearts: the cold dignity cracks and the clan sees the girl from before the exile laughing.

## Mates

### [[Mìreya]]: big change
Sober, she will not admit she smiled (*on the page*). Drunk, she clings to Atanvi and lists Atanvi's good qualities in strategic detail, in order, with supporting evidence.

### [[Sänrri]]: small change
At the zangke stage where everything is extremely interesting and needs a thorough explanation (*on the page*, the palulukan lecture). She becomes more herself. Possible addition: she sings, since her profile gives her a powerful singing voice.

### [[Syulang]]: medium change
The clan's mediator starts organizing everyone, assigning seats, ending arguments nobody was having. By the end she is crying about how proud she is of Txewan.

### [[Nì'syu]]: smallest change, handle with care
Her serene exterior covers the secret of who she really is. Drunk, the mask holds perfectly, and that is the unsettling part. Better used for a quiet moment than a joke.

## Siblings

### [[Kayan]]: big change
Stoic and blunt, but his profile says he gets attached easily and hates seeing people hurt. Drunk Kayan cries over each of his siblings in turn and tells them they are his responsibility.

### [[Lamaytxa]]: small change
She already lies and speaks in an archaic, self-important way. Drunk, the lies get bigger.

### [[Txolaw]]: small change
Legendarily lazy; drunk, he falls asleep in the warmest spot and stays there. His aversion to touch stays respected.

### [[Sänume]] and [['Eweyn]]: young adults, light touch
Sänume corrects Sänrri's plant facts; 'Eweyn corners [[Nika]] with questions about Earth. Keep both small.

### [[Tìmeyo]]: left out on purpose
Tìmeyo/Kerevi is a DID system. Treating a switch as a drunk gag would not fit the character, so this note does not give her a drunk version.
