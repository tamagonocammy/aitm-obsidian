# Txomì and other source characters (proposal)

> [!question] Proposal, not yet ruled
> Millie's ideas from the 2026-11-15 chat, with Claude's notes marked **(Claude)**. Nothing here is canon until it goes into the [[Rulings Log]].

## Txomì: Homura Akemi, founder of the Mangkwan

- **(Millie)** Every god needs a devil. Homura becomes **Txomì, founder of the Mangkwan clan**, thousands of years ago, in the same era as the Third Toruk Makto. The death of her mate drove her into despair.
- **(Millie, answered "yeah" in chat)** Her mate is [[Yoko'fya]] (Madoka), the Third Toruk Makto. How, when and why Yoko'fya died is still open.
- **(Claude)** Two founders mirror each other: [[Tunskxe]] founded the Astikasya; Txomì founded the clan that would hunt them.
- **(Claude)** Parallel to *Rebellion*: Homura refuses to let Madoka go. The Mangkwan's forced tsaheylu (Millie's theory: a consenting bond, forced to dominate) becomes Txomì's grief carried down the generations: never lose anyone again, even if it means taking them.
- **(Claude)** Irony: while the Mangkwan hold Sänrri captive, Yoko'fya teaches her to resist the clan her own grieving mate founded.
- **Name check:** *Txomì* (txo-mì) is valid Na'vi phonology and not an existing word on Reykunyu; nearest real word *txon*, "night." Not checked against Kelutral's generator.
- **Canon flag:** the Mangkwan are canon from *Avatar: Fire and Ash*; their founding by Txomì is an AITM addition.
