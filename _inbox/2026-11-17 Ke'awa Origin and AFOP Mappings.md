# Ke'awa Origin and AFOP Mappings (proposal)

> [!success] Accepted
> Built with Claude and accepted by Millie on 2026-11-17. Recorded in the [[Rulings Log]].

## Where the Astikasya come from

Millie built the Astikasya from the **Ke'awa Kame'tire** of *Avatar: Frontiers of Pandora*, a small cadet branch of the Kame'tire she saw potential in.

In the game (per the James Cameron's Avatar wiki): in 2138, some Kame'tire who did not trust Mokasa's account of the Sarentu massacre left for the Spires and formed the Ke'awa Kame'tire under Tsu'lo. Tsu'lo had been the Kame'tire's best hunter and was like a brother to Anufi, the tsahìk, and was let down by her retreat and her cooperation with Mokasa. In *From the Ashes*, the Mangkwan poison the Kame'tire's water; Anufi catches it and moves the clan into the Spires with the Ke'awa. In the game the two groups reunite.

## How AITM reconciles it with the Founder

**The old name, revived.** The Astikasya were an ancient clan founded by [[Tunskxe]] thousands of years ago. It faded and was absorbed into the Kame'tire long ago; only the Samaya line kept the memory of the Founder (the descent runs through [[Eyfuwen]]). In 2138, when the doubters leave, they take back the old name. The clan is young as a separate people and ancient at its root.

- This is why the Kame'tire dialect is the Astikasya's closest relative (see the Accent Reference).
- It fits the clan's existing description: once herbalists, driven by a tragedy from their ancestral forests to the Spires of the Clouded Forest, where Sheltering Falls lies. That is the Ke'awa's move from the Kame'tire to the Spires.
- It is why Sänrri reaching Tunskxe matters: she reconnects a young clan to a root thousands of years old.
- **Divergence from the game:** in AITM the Astikasya never reunite with the Kame'tire. The split stays an old wound between two peoples.
