# Why the ANAKT Exists (proposal)

> [!success] Accepted
> Millie's idea, put in order with Claude and accepted by Millie on 2026-11-16. Recorded in the [[Rulings Log]].

## Ardmore's problem

General Frances Ardmore runs the RDA on Pandora. Recom Miles Quaritch costs her a fortune and is persona non grata to her: he spends everything chasing Jake Sully in a personal vendetta and ignores the rest of the war. So she builds a force of her own. The [[ANAKT Fireteam]] are latest-model Recoms who answer to her and to no one else.

## The Mangkwan play

When the alliance with the Mangkwan comes up, Ardmore sneers at them in public ("pirates, fire people, savages"). In private she sees a tool. She dresses the ANAKT in Mangkwan colors (red, black and ash), sinks them into the clan's culture and gives them carte blanche: weapons, the clan's drug, killing, raiding, burning, no limits. In return they must be the example. The goal is for the Mangkwan to look at the ANAKT and think: these are better than Varang, and the Sky People back them directly.

The selling point is [[Luka]]. Varang is Na'vi, feathered and respected, and a bullet ends her. Luka can be shot, killed and printed again from his backup. To a clan that lives on fire and symbols, a leader who comes back from death is a god, and that god is sponsored by the RDA.

## Why it fails

The same way it failed with Quaritch: Recoms have goals of their own. Quaritch fixed on Jake. Luka fixes on Tomori.

The spark is small. Quaritch notices Tomori/[[Sänrri]] and mentions her to Ardmore as a minor detail. Ardmore sends Luka and the ANAKT, who join the Mangkwan and are sent to the Falls. Luka's *"Hello, Dr. Takamatsu"* is him greeting his Jake.

Ardmore dies in *Avatar: Fire and Ash*. With her gone, the ANAKT lose their leash: no one above them, carte blanche still in hand, and Luka's obsession free to run toward the Falls.
