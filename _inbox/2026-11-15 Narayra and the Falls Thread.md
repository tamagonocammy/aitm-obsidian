## Narayra

**Narayra te Ikutxo Tosiwi'ite** (source: Maki Shiina, Taki's older sister, four years older, admired brass-band president and trumpet player). [[Rängay]]'s older sister, a daughter of Tosiwi like her. She is the Astikasya's envoy to other Na'vi clans: composed, persuasive, admired across the Western Frontier, the voice that speaks for the Falls in other clans' Hometrees. Like Maki, who only appears in *MyGO* as a name and a nameplate, she is talked about but never seen until after the Siege. She was away for the Mangkwan captivity, the Occupation and the Siege. She comes home because Sarentu storytellers spread the news of the Nine Spears and she heard her sister's people named.

She is the voice of the critic: why is everyone acting as if this is all fine? To her the clan looks like it lost itself: a former dreamwalker as tsakarem, a Tawkami woman and a Tipani-blooded man carrying sacred spears, and the twin the clan exiled standing among the Nine. She is contemptuous toward Sänrri and toward the Spears who were not born Astikasya.

Name: generated with Kelutral's name generator; checked on Reykunyu (valid syllables na-RAY-ra, not an existing word, no collision). Case forms per Kelutral: Narayral, Narayrat, Narayraru / Narayrar, Narayrayä, Narayrari. Earlier candidates Ngayran and Aweyi were not used.

## The Falls thread of the Wild Hunt

The arc cross-cuts between the Hunt and the Falls. The Siege and the Hunt are only days apart (like *The Way of Water* and *Fire and Ash*), so everyone's grief is still raw and [[Lì'may]] is still three.

1. **The Silence.** Sänrri stops singing, then speaking. "The Contract." The clan rebuilds around the silence.
2. **The Door.** [[Rängay]] goes to Sänrri's shelter uninvited. Sänrri pushes her out and lets the hanging fall closed in her face. Rängay to Kaymi: *"Her song is gone."*
3. **The Envoy.** Narayra arrives just after Tsu'lan leaves on the Hunt: the clan's informal ambassador is gone when the formal envoy walks in. Rängay: *"You decided to show up, after all of this?"*
4. **The Critic.** Kaymi steps in front of Sänrri: *"I flew into Mangkwan land and carried her home on my back. I did not do that so you could look at her like that."* Tsu'ong answers calmly, which angers her more. Ketxe smiles and says nothing. Syayvi: *"You are not wrong about me."* That is the first thing that gets through Narayra's composure. She asks where the Righteous is; nobody at the Falls can answer. Lì'may plants herself in front of Sänrri: *"Sa'nu is not Na'vi! Sa'nu is not human! Sa'nu is MY SA'NU!"* (*sa'nu*, "mommy," checked on Reykunyu.)
5. **Backslide.** "Backslide" is Rängay's song for this stretch. With her perfect sister home she slides back into being "Narayra's little sister," snaps at [[Nìla]] over a ritual detail in exactly Narayra's tone, hears it, and stops mid-sentence.
6. **Both.** The Hunt comes home. Yukeyo's *"Is this justice or is this grief?"* and Narayra's question (is this healing or denial?) sit side by side. The answer to both is both. Narayra stays, unsettled.

The full draft prose is in the Claude project's `AITM_08_The_Wild_Hunt_Arc.md`, section "The Falls Thread."

## The Hunt's riders

"Nine" is the Flying Troupe (Teysaw, Txokän, Layon, Sìltsyu, Seytu, Yezì, Pakì, Paysìn, Roneyu). The Hunt is the Troupe plus whoever joins: Tsu'lan, Yukeyo and Tsyokanì.
