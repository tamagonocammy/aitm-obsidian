# Lost Cohort relationship chart (adapted from the official MyGO!!!!! chart)

Source: the SPICA MyGO!!!!! relationship chart, 20 arrows between five members. Mapping from the vault: Tomori is [[Sänrri]], Taki is [[Rängay]], Soyo is [[Pxayu]], Anon is [[Apayì]], Rāna is [[Pamyaw]]. Arrow directions were checked against the image. Soba, candy and guitar picks became things the vault already has. Spoken lines have no contractions.

Status tags: **vault** rests on a line in the character notes, **extrapolated** is my reading, **open choice** needs Millie's decision.

## Pet names (added 2026-11-24, Millie asked for the update)

Apayì calls Sänrri *ma oeyä 'eylan* ("my friend") and Pxayu *ma yawnetu* ("loved one"). *Ma* is the vocative marker (Wikibooks 5.5, Reykunyu), *'eylan* and *yawnetu* are in Reykunyu, and *Ma oeyä 'eylan!* is Wikibooks' own example. Wikibooks says Na'vi has no regular diminutive system, so no clipped names were made up. The live parser was not rerun on these exact phrases, and Kelutral was not checked on diminutives.

## Open points

- The sweet fruit Pamyaw trades hunts for is called "tawtute fruit" in her note, and Reykunyu lists tawtute as "Skyperson". The fruit name needs a ruling.

- The chart shows the bonds before the Falls thread (Rängay shut out, Pxayu's call-out, Pamyaw's remark to Apayì, the songcords).

## The arrows

### Sänrri and Apayì

- **Sänrri to Apayì** (vault): “We were lost together.”  
  Official chart: いっしょに迷子 (Lost together). Apayì saw her own reflection in Sänrri, and their pact was “I will see you, if you see me” (Apayì note). The Lost Cohort note still lists “why the group is called Lost” as open, so this line would answer it.
- **Apayì to Sänrri** (extrapolated): “Ma oeyä 'eylan! (My friend!)”  
  Official chart: ともりん！ (Her nickname for Tomori). Wikibooks gives “Ma oeyä 'eylan!” as an attested way to call to a friend, and ma is the vocative marker. The Cohort bond is a friendship, so the endearment is friend, not loved one. Millie asked for this update 2026-11-24.

### Sänrri and Rängay

- **Sänrri to Rängay** (vault, unspoken): “Rängay is someone I can rely on.”  
  Official chart: 頼りになる (Dependable). Being held to Rängay's standard is how Sänrri knows she belongs, and she has never told her (Sänrri note).
- **Rängay to Sänrri** (vault, unspoken): “Her voice is the best I have heard.”  
  Official chart: 歌声、最高 (Her singing voice is the best). Rängay watches Sänrri lead ceremonies with fierce, unspoken pride (Rängay note).

### Sänrri and Pamyaw

- **Sänrri to Pamyaw** (vault): Understands her in music and silence.  
  Official chart: 音で通じる (We connect through sound). They rarely spoke and understood each other in the language of music and silence (Pamyaw note).
- **Pamyaw to Sänrri** (vault): “This is interesting.”  
  Official chart: おもしれー女 (An interesting woman). Her own words when she first appeared and stayed: Sänrri “made things interesting” (Pamyaw note).

### Sänrri and Pxayu

- **Sänrri to Pxayu** (extrapolated): “I will not let go of you.”  
  Official chart: 一生、離さない (I won't let go, for life). Pxayu's wound is being left, and Sänrri sits with people in grief without hurrying them (Sänrri note). Direction checked on the official chart: Sänrri to Pxayu.
- **Pxayu to Sänrri** (extrapolated): “You are so stubborn.”  
  Official chart: 頑固だよね (You're stubborn, aren't you). The vault says Sänrri does not need Pxayu to be stable, she needs her to be real, and that Sänrri blames herself and deflects. The line itself is my reading.

### Rängay and Apayì

- **Rängay to Apayì** (extrapolated): “I will grant her the effort.”  
  Official chart: 努力は認める (I'll give her credit for the effort). Apayì sits in silence beside Rängay when conviction threatens to crack her (Apayì note). Rängay's side is my reading.
- **Apayì to Rängay** (vault): “Why can she not just say it?”  
  Official chart: 素直じゃな〜い (You're not being honest!). Apayì reads Rängay's jaw tightening and knows the sternness is armor (Apayì note).

### Rängay and Pamyaw

- **Rängay to Pamyaw** (open choice): “Here. Take the fruit.”  
  Official chart: ほら、飴 (Here, candy). Pamyaw trades choice cuts of her hunt for sweet fruit (Pamyaw note). The note names it tawtute fruit, but Reykunyu lists tawtute as “Skyperson”, so the fruit name needs your ruling.
- **Pamyaw to Rängay** (vault): “Let us play it together. My way.”  
  Official chart: ライブやろ (Let's do a live). Her experimental variations on the old songs showed Rängay that honoring the old does not mean refusing to explore it (Pamyaw note).

### Rängay and Pxayu

- **Rängay to Pxayu** (extrapolated): “She is Cohort, same as I am.”  
  Official chart: 同じバンド (Same band). The Cohort survived the Mangkwan captivity as a unit (Lost Cohort note). The line is my reading.
- **Pxayu to Rängay** (extrapolated): “Mind how you say it!”  
  Official chart: 言い方！ (The way you say it!). Rängay corrects people fast and the younger ones fear her intensity (Rängay note).

### Apayì and Pamyaw

- **Apayì to Pamyaw** (vault): Leaves a smooth singing stone where Pamyaw practices.  
  Official chart: ギターうますぎ (Too good at guitar). From the Apayì note. The chart's guitar praise becomes a gift, because Pamyaw's instruments in the vault are her voice and her flute.
- **Pamyaw to Apayì** (vault): Sits nearby and hums until Apayì stops performing.  
  Official chart: ピック貸す (Lends a pick). When Apayì spiraled into performance anxiety, Pamyaw hummed an old melody beside her (Pamyaw note).

### Apayì and Pxayu

- **Apayì to Pxayu** (extrapolated): “Ma yawnetu! (Loved one!)”  
  Official chart: そよりん！ (Her nickname for Soyo). Reykunyu lists yawnetu as “loved one”. Apayì and Pxayu are mates and co-parents of Lawarey in the vault, so it is a mate's endearment, said loudly enough for Pxayu to object.
- **Pxayu to Apayì** (vault): “Stop calling me that.”  
  Official chart: その呼び方やめて (Stop calling me that). Their legendary bickering is the sound of a bond that has not broken (Relationship Lore).

### Pamyaw and Pxayu

- **Pamyaw to Pxayu** (extrapolated): Turns up with her own unspiced meal and sits down.  
  Official chart: おそば持参 (Brings her own soba). Pamyaw refuses spiced stews, and once left a smooth stone in Pxayu's path that said “I am still here” (Pamyaw note). The visit is my scene.
- **Pxayu to Pamyaw** (extrapolated): “She is far too free.”  
  Official chart: 自由すぎる (Way too free). Pamyaw comes and goes as she pleases, and Pxayu reads a missed glance as someone leaving (Pxayu note).
