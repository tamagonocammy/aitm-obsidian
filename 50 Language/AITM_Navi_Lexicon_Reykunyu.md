# Na'vi Lexicon (from Reykunyu)

Compact Na'vi → English (and Spanish, where Reykunyu has it) word list, with Reef Na'vi spellings, for AITM, built from Reykunyu's full dictionary data (reykunyu.lu/words.json), downloaded 2026-10-28. 3254 entries.

**How to read an entry.**
`word [STRESS·ed·syllable] (type) — English | ES: Spanish · infixes: … · cases: … · ⚠ STATUS · note: …`

In the stress guide, the stressed syllable is in capitals: *sa'nu* [SA'·nu]. Infix positions are marked with dots: `tsp..ang` means the infix goes after *tsp* (tsp‹ol›ang); `p.lltx.e` has two slots, first after *p*, second after *lltx* (p‹ol›lltx‹ei›e). Pronoun entries list their case forms in the order subjective / agentive / patientive / dative / genitive / topical.

**Reef Na'vi.** Headwords are in Forest Na'vi spelling. Where Reef Na'vi (RN) spelling differs, the entry shows `RN:`. The RN spelling follows Reykunyu's rules: syllable-initial px/tx/kx become b/d/g (not after f or s), the glottal stop drops between two different vowels inside a word, u becomes ù in the words that have it, and unstressed ä often becomes e (not always: Frommer notes *rää*). RN also pronounces sy as "sh" and tsy as "ch", without changing the spelling. See `AITM_Reef_Navi_Reference.md`.

**Status flags.** ⚠ UNCONFIRMED means probably real but not officially confirmed, so flag it before canon use. ⚠ LOAN marks a loanword from an Earth language (Sky People words). ⚠ UNOFFICIAL marks a word not from Frommer.

**Not in this list.** Reykunyu adds new words regularly. If a word is missing here, check reykunyu.lu before deciding it doesn't exist, and refresh this file every few months.
