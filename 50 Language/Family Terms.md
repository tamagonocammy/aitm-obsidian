these are the only family terms currently:
sa'nok "mother"
sa'nu "mom, mama",
,
sempul "father
sempu "dad, papa",
,
sa'sem "parent or set of parents",
tsmuk "sibling"
tsmuktu "sibling-person",
tsmuke "sister",
tsmukan "brother",
,
'ite "daughter",
'itan "son",
'eveng "child"
'evi "kid"

i tend to express grandparents as "mother's mother, father's mother, etc." and uncle/aunt as "father's brother, mother's sister, etc."

so "tsmuke sa'nokä oeyä" sister of my mother (my aunt) or "sa'nok sempulä ngeyä" mother of your father (your paternal grandmother) etc.
