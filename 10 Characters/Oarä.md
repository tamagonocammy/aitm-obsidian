## Profile

**Personality:** Oarä is nine, and her hair hangs over her eyes. She says very little and shows people instead, the way Pamyaw does. She is a weirdo with the energy of a feral kid who stuck around, and she is fine with that. She forages fungus and rot and tastes what she finds to learn what it is, and she is quietly pleased when someone is disgusted. On Pandora that is dangerous, and the clan has stopped asking her to quit. She keeps each thing she has tasted as a knot on a cord.

She is as nonchalant about danger as her parent Tswesya. She climbs the highest peaks at Sheltering Falls and goes to look in thanator dens, and she turns up on ledges and out of caves with no warning, which startles the older teens every time. Around humans she goes skittish, with one exception: Sänrri, whom she counts as belonging to the clan. She wants an ikran bond and does not want what comes with it, because a bond would tie her to the clan responsibilities and cut into her wandering.

From Katirì she has rhythm, and she taps out complicated beats on rocks and hollow logs wherever she is. Her aunt Waynhì worries about her safety, and Oarä hears it as someone giving orders. She pushes every limit her parents set and still likes having both of them close. Mìrì has been extra kind to her lately and found her a perfect drumming stone. Oarä cares a great deal about the stone and has not wondered why Mìrì is being kind.
