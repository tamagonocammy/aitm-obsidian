## Profile

**Personality:** A nature-obsessed wildchild with the nonchalant energy inherited from her parent Tswesya, Oarä treats death-defying exploration as casually as others treat morning walks. She appears and disappears like a forest spirit, materializing from impossible heights or hidden caves without warning, often startling the older teens who never get used to her sudden manifestations. With few words but sharp observational skills, she speaks mainly through actions, following the "show don't tell" philosophy that mirrors Pamyaw's approach to life. Despite her fearless attitude toward Pandora's dangers - casually scaling the highest peaks of Sheltering Falls or investigating thanator dens - she becomes notably skittish around humans, though she makes an exception for Sänrri whom she recognizes as truly belonging to the clan. Her dream of someday bonding with an ikran conflicts with her fierce independence, as she knows such a bond would tie her more closely to clan responsibilities and limit her solitary wandering. She has inherited her mother Katirì's love for rhythm and drumming, often found tapping out complex beats on rocks or hollow logs during her explorations. While Waynhì's protective concern for her safety comes across as intimidating authority to Oarä's free spirit, she finds comfort in the steady presence of both her parents, even as she pushes every boundary they set. Her mysterious comings and goings have made her something of a legend among the clan's children, though she remains blissfully unaware of young Mìrì's carefully hidden crush, viewing the twin's recent extra kindness as simple friendship while being far more interested in the perfect drumming stone Mìrì found for her.
