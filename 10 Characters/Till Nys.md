## Profile

**Personality:** Till Nys is the Resistance's quiet engine and its most overlooked asset, a fact he is entirely comfortable with because visibility has never been something he wanted. He came to Pandora in the same wave as most of Earth House, young and pragmatic and disinclined toward grand statements, and he has remained in the Resistance for the same reason he stayed in Earth House through all of it: because the work needs doing, and he knows how to do it, and leaving would mean someone else has to figure it out, and that someone else probably will not do it as well. He is not a man of many words. This is not shyness or coldness or the suppression of some richer inner life waiting to be revealed; it is simply accurate. He thinks in systems and functions, and most of what he has to say is already communicated through what he does, which is thorough and quiet and almost never wrong. Nika trusts him with everything she does not have time to oversee personally, and this is the highest compliment she knows how to give and the highest one he knows how to receive. He and Miri have developed a working language of minimal vocalization and shared technical shorthand that functions beautifully in the workshop and occasionally baffles everyone else in the building. They do not explain it. Till does not fully understand how it started either, only that one day Miri left a half-finished schematic on his bench without comment and Till picked it up and finished the section Miri had been stuck on and left it back, and the whole thing was resolved in silence, and neither of them felt the need to discuss it. He is a third-year mechanical student who came to Pandora in the same intake as Aliya, which means he has known her longer than he has known most of the others, and there is a specific quality to how he orbits her during difficult situations: not hovering, not intervening, just present, slightly closer than usual, in a way that Aliya finds profoundly steadying and that Till would describe, if pressed, as simply being nearby, which he supposes is accurate. He is not indifferent to the Na'vi or to Pandora. He finds the planet extraordinary in a way he does not have adequate words for, which is saying something, and the ikran in particular represent to him a category of creature so far beyond his engineering vocabulary that he has taken to simply standing very still when they are near and observing carefully, the same approach he takes to anything he does not yet understand but intends to. He was nearly killed during the Siege when a Mangkwan raider came through the back entrance of the supply depot, and he handled it with a wrench and a great deal of adrenaline and told no one the specifics afterward. There is a small dent in the wall of the depot that he has not repaired. He does not entirely know why. He thinks perhaps it is useful to have one thing in his environment that is not fixed.
