## Profile

**Personality:** Ojelo Gabel has been told his whole life that he is too much, too loud, too present, too cheerful, too willing to bet on the wrong things, and he has listened carefully to all of this feedback and elected to be exactly the same way regardless, because in his experience the people who told him to be less of himself were trying to make their own lives easier at the expense of his. He is the Resistance's mood-maker, a title nobody officially assigned him and that everybody unofficially relies on, because the sustained psychological pressure of fighting a corporate military operation from a series of improvised bases in an alien jungle has a particular effect on morale and Ojelo is, functionally, the thing that counteracts it. Not through false cheerfulness, which he is too perceptive for, but through the specific quality of his presence: the way he names the thing everyone is feeling before anyone else will, the way he finds the genuinely funny part of a terrible situation without minimizing the terrible, the way he is honest about being scared in a manner that gives other people permission to be scared too, which turns out to be enormously stabilizing. He is a mechanic by training and a talented one, though Nika has had to implement a firm policy about him customizing equipment without informing anyone because his modifications are excellent and also occasionally alarming, and she would like to know in advance. He came from a background with very little financial stability and arrived at the RDA already expert at the specific mathematics of how to make inadequate resources function, a skill that translates perfectly to Resistance operations and that Miri respects deeply and expresses by not complaining when Ojelo borrows his tools, which is very nearly a declaration of love in Miri's emotional vocabulary. His personal prohibition against earning money through weapons is something he carried from Earth and kept through the RDA’s Sci-Ops and through Pandora and through everything that has happened since, a line he has never crossed and will not cross, even when the cause is unambiguously just, because the principle is about the means and not the ends and he made up his mind about it a long time ago. This has led to exactly one major argument with Chuchu, which lasted three hours and ended with both of them eating together in stubborn, companionable silence, which is how most of their arguments end. The Astikasya children think he is magnificent. This began when Lamaytxa challenged him to an arm wrestling match on a dare from Lì'may, and he lost immediately and spectacularly and responded with such good-natured, theatrical devastation that both children dissolved into absolute hysterics, and the story spread through the clan's younger population with considerable embellishment. He remains deeply committed to eventually winning a rematch and is prepared to wait as long as necessary.
