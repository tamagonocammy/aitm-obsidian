> [!abstract] At a glance
> **Background:** Former RDA Sci-Ops who helped Tomori transition to Na'vi avatar. Lost hand during RDA escape - wears metal prosthetic as badge of honor. Na'vi call her "Silver Hand" (friend of the People). Like Norm, pilots avatar for field ops but maintains human identity. Led Resistance after Alma Cortez stepped down and Jake Sully contact lost. Genuinely cares for team, struggles with moral complexities. Bridges human tech and Na'vi values through Astikasya friendship. Desk portrait of wife Sabina Fardin on Earth.

## Profile

**Personality:** Nika is very kind and compassionate, often willing to accept new people and help them with any issues they face. As the leader of Earth House, she maintains her gentle nature while being an outstanding mechanic and engineer who gets excited over technical details. She tends to avoid conflict and is modest about her skills, usually deflecting praise regarding her mechanical abilities. Nika has a special connection to the Astikasya clan through her work helping Tomori transition into her Na'vi avatar body during their time at RDA Sci-Ops. Despite losing her hand during her escape from the RDA, she wears her metal prosthetic as a badge of honor - the Na'vi respect her as a “friend of the People” and call her “Silver Hand” in recognition of her sacrifice and dedication to their cause. Like Norm Spellman, she pilots her own avatar body for field operations alongside the Na'vi, though she always returns to her human form and maintains her human identity. She genuinely cares for her fellow Resistance members and struggles with the moral complexities of their fight against the RDA. Her background in assembly work from childhood gave her exceptional mechanical skills, making her essential in maintaining and developing equipment for the Resistance operations. She now leads the Resistance’s efforts after Alma Cortez stepped down and contact was lost with Jake Sully, a responsibility she carries with characteristic determination despite the weight of command. During the Siege of the Falls, she led the efforts on defending and guarding the settlement alongside the children who had made a guerilla group and the cunning tsahìk of the Astikasya, 'Ipxerey. Though she maintains her caring, big-sister personality that her team looks up to, she has grown into a capable leader who bridges the gap between human technology and Na'vi values through her friendship with the Astikasya clan. On her desk sits a cherished portrait of her wife Sabina Fardin back on Earth, a reminder of what she’s fighting to protect...
