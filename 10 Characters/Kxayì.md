## Profile

**Personality:** A pure concentration of gremlin energy in Na'vi form, Kxayì inherited her father's loud personality and her aunt Lamaytxa's chaos, creating a whirlwind of noise and unpredictability wherever she goes. Her dream to become a legendary warrior like Tsa'rawm and Kaymi drives every impulsive decision, though her path toward this goal is anything but traditional. She approaches hunting with chaotic innovation, creating techniques so unorthodox they either fail spectacularly or succeed beyond anyone's imagination. Her rivalry with Notä pushes both girls to excellence, though Kxayì's competitive nature means she'll challenge literally anyone to anything - including challenging Kaymi to sparring matches (she loses every time but keeps trying). Despite being genuinely annoying at times with her inability to control her volume or energy, her fierce loyalty to family is absolute; she'd fight Toruk itself for any of them. She hero-worships Kaymi to an embarrassing degree, often mimicking the eyktanay's mannerisms (poorly) and declaring herself "future protector of the clan" to anyone who'll listen. Her unpredictability is her greatest strength and weakness - enemies can't anticipate her moves because even SHE doesn't know what she'll do next. Beneath the noise and chaos, she's desperately trying to prove she's more than just "Tsa'rawm's loud daughter," though she'd die before admitting this vulnerability.

## Notes

- **Inspirations (ruled 2026-11-21).** A goblin through and through. Her main inspiration is Gigi Murin of Hololive, with traits from her aunt [[Lamaytxa]] and, most of all, her father [[Tsa'rawm]]. She is daddy's girl.
- **Gigi's traits (ruled 2026-11-21).** She does things because she thought it would be funny, and decides in the middle of the jump. Thinking makes her head hurt. She tracks well and gets lost on the way home, the opposite of [[Mawpay]]'s mental map. She runs one bit for days until everyone is worn out. She goes soft only with her family and the small kids, [[Lì'may]] among them.
- **She is gay (ruled 2026-11-21).** She noticed that girls were her thing. Tsa'rawm's reaction was roughly "if you are my daughter, of course", and they share it even more now. The vault has no orientation field, so it is recorded here.
- **The crush on Notä (ruled 2026-11-21).** She challenges everyone to everything, so the crush looks like a louder version of their rivalry. [[Notä]]'s explosive botany under [[Ketxe]] is the best thing she has ever seen, and she cannot say why she keeps hanging around. Between the two it reads as a bromance or a situationship, "Autofister but Na'vi", after Gigi Murin and Cecilia Immergreen of Hololive. Notä's side is in her own note.
