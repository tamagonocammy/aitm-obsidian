## Profile

**Personality:** Chuchu is a troubled but fiercely determined young woman who has endured discrimination and hardship her entire life, making her incredibly hardy and resilient. She has a gruff personality and reacts aggressively to anyone who oppresses the weak, particularly despising the RDA not only for destroying Pandora but for their exploitation of Earth as well. Her hot-headed nature makes her prone to picking fights, especially when defending her friends, and she prefers being direct and confronting problems head-on rather than backing down. Despite her tough exterior, Chuchu has a protective big-sister instinct and genuinely cares for her fellow Resistance members. She knows Tomori from their past and loves teasing her about being an "alien princess" now, though this comes from a place of affection rather than malice. When Astikasya clan members visit the Resistance base, she's surprisingly good with the children, though she's always freaked out by their sheer size compared to humans. Her strength and fighting spirit have earned respect even from the Na'vi - after arm wrestling with Tsa'rawm and putting up an impressive fight despite losing, he declared her "hardy like a Zeswa warrior" and gained genuine appreciation for this tough Sky Person who refuses to back down from any challenge. During the Siege, she was able to help the clan by defending them with her guns, a shotgun and a sniper rifle, against the Mangkwan army that had gathered below in the base of the Falls, racking numbers until she was seen by a raider, who she defeated with a shotgun blast while defending the Ngotxì twins.
