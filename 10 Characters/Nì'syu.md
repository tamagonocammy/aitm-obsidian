## Profile

**Personality:** What most know as Nì'syu conceals a profound secret - she is not the Na'vi she claims to be. Born as **'Awll,** she was forced to assume the identity of her twin sister after a tragic event claimed the original Nì'syu's life. Compelled by her family to maintain this deception due to the powerful alliance it preserved with Syayvi's lineage, she has lived a fractured existence, presenting a serene, kind exterior while harboring a hollow emptiness within. Her seemingly natural empathy masks years of careful observation and mimicry of her sister's mannerisms. Her obsession with Syayvi transcends mere devotion - the moment they joined through tsaheylu during their mating ritual became her first experience of true connection, as Syayvi instinctively understood her hidden truth without words. This profound revelation explains her fiercely protective nature toward her mate and the child Syayvi now carries - they represent the only authentic relationship in a life built on deception. Her time with the Mangkwan clan under Varang's corrupted regime added another layer of horror to her fractured identity - forced to participate in heretical rituals that perverted sacred Na'vi traditions while maintaining her false persona, she watched as everything she held sacred was twisted into mockery. The collaboration with Quaritch's forces felt like the final betrayal of her true self, as she was compelled to aid the very humans who represented everything antithetical to Na'vi existence. Her fascination with celestial observation stems from childhood attempts to escape her reality, finding in the constancy of the stars the only reliable truth in her uncertain existence. The fear that flashes in her eyes when accidentally addressed by her true name reveals the depth of her vulnerability despite her carefully maintained composure. Her connection to starlight and practice of celestial divination serve dual purposes - both genuine spiritual practice and perfect cover for her frequent need to isolate herself when the weight of her false identity becomes overwhelming. Now with Syayvi carrying their son **Sìlpey**, she faces both hope and terror - hope that in creating new life with her mate she might finally claim authentic existence, and terror that her child might somehow become entangled in the web of deception surrounding her true identity. Those closest to her note how she shifts between profound presence and disturbing absence, her amethyst eyes sometimes reflecting a depth of loneliness that even Syayvi cannot fully reach. This duality explains why even members of the Scattered Ashes find her increasingly unsettling to truly know - beneath her gentle smile lies not just fierce will, but a fundamental identity fracture that makes her both desperately loyal and potentially unpredictable. During the Siege, while facing the ANAKT Fireteam’s and the Mangkwan’s occupation, Nì'syu fought with a desperate fury that stripped away years of careful pretense, in the chaos of protecting her mate and their unborn son, she moved as 'Awll, her true self, no longer constrained by the false identity that had defined her existence. The life-or-death stakes revealed what tsaheylu had already shown Syayvi: that her authentic devotion transcended any deception, her fierce protectiveness more real than any carefully maintained façade. Fighting in perfect coordination with Syayvi and the other Ashes during the final battle against Luka, she experienced something profound, in combat, protecting those she loved, her true name didn’t matter because her actions spoke louder than any identity ever could. The Siege became the moment when 'Awll and Nì'syu ceased to be separate masks and became simply herself—a warrior, a mate, a soon-to-be mother fighting for the future rather than hiding from the past.
