## Profile

**Personality:** A distinguished elder who effortlessly blends the roles of warrior, artist, and nurturer, Tse'hayu serves as both the clan's master cook and keeper of musical traditions. Once a formidable warrior-bard who fought alongside Eyfuwen in their younger days, he now channels his passion into two distinct but equally important crafts: creating exquisite traditional instruments and preparing the communal meals that bring the clan together each evening. His approach to both music and cooking reflects his perfectionist nature, every dish must be seasoned precisely, every instrument tuned to spiritual as well as auditory perfection. As the clan's head cook, he takes immense pride in ensuring everyone is well-fed, often seen before dawn preparing elaborate feasts and teaching younger clan members the sacred recipes passed down through generations. His direct, sometimes blunt communication style masks a deeply caring nature, he believes in pushing others to reach their potential through honest feedback rather than empty praise, whether they're learning to play an instrument or properly season a stew. His mentorship of Txewan nurtured her natural musical gifts while his patient cooking lessons taught 'Eweyn that even adventurous spirits need grounding in traditional skills. Despite his serious demeanor during instruction, he transforms completely when either performing music or presenting a particularly successful meal, his joy infectious enough to get even the most reserved clan members celebrating around the evening fires. His partnership with Mitsìyo demonstrates how strength and gentleness complement each other perfectly: where she provides emotional support through weaving and counsel, he nourishes both body and soul through music and food. Though he rarely speaks of his warrior past, his strategic mind makes him a valued advisor to the clan leadership, and his experience in battle, art, and sustenance gives him unique insight into the balance between protecting tradition and embracing necessary change.
