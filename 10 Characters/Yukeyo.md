## Profile

***Personality:*** Tall and powerfully built, Yukeyo carries herself with an almost apologetic softness that belies her considerable tracking skills and bone-crafting talents. Born to the Anurai clan, she developed an uncanny ability to read terrain and track prey across arid valleys, along with deep knowledge of viperwolf behavior from years of observation. Despite her obvious abilities, she struggles with self-doubt and stutters when nervous, especially around a certain righteous warrior from the mountains. Her bone-craft tends toward practical elegance: perfectly weighted tools and beautiful-sounding instruments that she dismisses as "simple" compared to her clan-mates' elaborate work. What people miss beneath her anxiety is a dry wit that emerges in perfectly-timed observations and a fierce competitive streak during hunts. She notices small details about people with extraordinary precision, making her both an indispensable tracker and a deeply empathetic companion. Her relationship with Tsu'lan began during a two-week visit where he treated her with genuine respect and kindness, leaving her completely unprepared for the depth of feelings that developed. After a chaotic visit to Sheltering Falls, including enthusiastic matchmaking by the entire Nine Spears and an accidental sparring victory over Kxayì, she's now managing a long-distance relationship while learning to believe she's always been enough as she is. The Astikasya have essentially adopted her without permission, a development she finds both overwhelming and secretly wonderful.
