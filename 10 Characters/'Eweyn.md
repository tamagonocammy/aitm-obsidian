## Profile

**Personality:** Young, curious, and energetic to the core, 'Eweyn is a born explorer and a whirlwind of enthusiasm, always seeking new adventures and experiences in Pandora's vast mysteries alongside her inseparable ikran, Sìltu. As one of the youngest adults, often alongside her mate Sänume with whom she shares a spirited rivalry bordering on love and an insatiable thirst for knowledge, 'Eweyn is a free soul, frequently seen gathering fruits or soaring through the skies. She adores her young nieces Mìyo and Mìrì and nephew Netezu, greatly enjoying telling them stories of her explorations, showing them new things she has found in the wild, and matching their playful energy. Her relationship with her older sister Txewan is good and affectionate, though she loves to tease her a lot, often making her sister embarrassed but also helping to lighten Txewan's anxious moods. Unlike many Na'vi, 'Eweyn is unafraid of Sky People technology; her innate curiosity leads her to constantly ask Sänrri and the Resistance about Earth and her life there, building an exceptionally broad perspective and a future ripe with discovery. She is often seen with the Resistance’s HQ deep within the Clouded Forest, sharing findings and information with Nika.
