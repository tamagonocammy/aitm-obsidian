## Profile

**Personality:** Sharp-minded and naturally organized, Mawpay has become the strategic brain behind many of the clan children's adventures. This 11-year-old approaches challenges methodically, often seeing practical solutions that her more impulsive peers overlook. Where her brother turns to music, Mawpay finds her voice through planning and problem-solving. She maintains an impressive mental map of the territory, remembering paths and landmarks with uncanny precision. Despite her practical nature, she harbors a deep appreciation for beauty, collecting useful objects that also happen to catch the light in fascinating ways. Her relationship with her mother Rängay is built on mutual respect for order and discipline, while she shares quieter moments with her father Txafwe, absorbing his calm wisdom. Among the other children, she's formed a particularly close bond with Lawarey, the two often working together as the "sensible ones" of the group. Though younger than many of her companions, Mawpay often finds herself mediating disputes or refining their wild schemes into workable plans. She balances her responsible nature with genuine childhood wonder, her serious expressions frequently breaking into excitement when discovering something new about Pandora's endless mysteries.
