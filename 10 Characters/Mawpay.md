## Profile

**Personality:** Mawpay is eleven, and she remembers every path and landmark in the territory, which is why the other children's plans end up passing through her. She looks at a wild scheme, finds the part that will not work and fixes it, though she is younger than most of them. She may be the sharpest of the children. She corrects adults on facts, politely at first, and expects to be taken seriously. She wants to be recognized for being good at things and not for being somebody's daughter, and Kxayì wants the same thing at a much higher volume.

Her father Txafwe looks stern, and around Mawpay he goes soft and foolish in public. She puts up with it with great dignity. With her mother Rängay she has a relationship built on order and discipline, and with Txafwe she has quieter moments. Her sister Nìla turns to music, and Mawpay turns to planning. She collects objects that are useful and also catch the light. Her serious face breaks into excitement when she finds something new about Pandora. She and Lawarey are the sensible ones of the group.
