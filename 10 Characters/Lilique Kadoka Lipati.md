## Profile

**Personality:** Lilique Kadoka Lipati is the person in the room who has already quietly handled three things before the meeting starts, and she would like credit for none of them because she was happy to do it and also she is too busy to explain. She is a first-year who carries herself like someone who has been running operations since she was twelve, which is not entirely inaccurate; she grew up in a household that required its children to be competent early and rewarded initiative with more responsibility, which she accepted with such genuine enthusiasm that the lesson took root in ways her family had not entirely intended. She is warm, she is sociable, she loves gossip about other people's romantic situations with a sincerity and investment that is occasionally overwhelming, and she is also, underneath all of this and running simultaneously with it, meticulously organized in a manner that would alarm people if they looked closely at her planning documents, which she keeps in her head. She came to Pandora on a management track scholarship and she has applied everything she knows about resource allocation and group dynamics to the Resistance with the matter-of-fact competence of someone who does not think of this as exceptional because she cannot imagine doing it any other way. Martin relies on her operationally in ways he has not fully acknowledged and she has not pointed out, because pointing it out would make him feel badly and she likes Martin and also she needs him to keep doing his job. She once organized the entire resupply operation for the Siege's preparation while also maintaining three separate social conversations and keeping track of who was upset at whom and why, which she considers all part of the same general task of keeping a group of frightened, exhausted humans functional under pressure. She is not afraid of anything in the sense of backing down from it. She has a deep, specific aversion to weaponry — she will not handle firearms, will not participate in weapons maintenance, will plan around their use but declines proximity — that she has had since before Pandora and does not explain and does not need to explain. The Resistance accommodates it without comment. During the Siege she coordinated logistics from the interior base with absolute clarity of focus, tracking supply routes and personnel positions and communicating to Nika in real time, and she was still somehow aware that Kobeni was about to spiral and checked in with her between transmissions in a voice that had no business being that calm. She thinks about Earth sometimes, about the specific texture of a life that had problems she understood the scale of, and she lets herself miss it briefly and then returns to the task, which is what she has always done with the things she cannot go back to. She is building something here, in this improvised base, with these strange and difficult people who chose this the same way she did, and the fact that it is hard and frightening and not what she planned does not diminish her investment in it. If anything, it clarifies it. She knows what she is doing and she knows why, and on Pandora, at this altitude, in this mist, with these people, that turns out to be enough.
