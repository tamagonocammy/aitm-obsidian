## Profile

**Personality:** Martin Upmont has been losing sleep over decisions he has not yet made, and this is the most honest thing about him. He is a tall, earnest man with an unfortunate talent for being the person in the room who cares most about doing things right and least about doing things fast, a combination that made him a deeply unreliable team leader at Sci-Ops and an unexpectedly indispensable one in the Resistance. He did not come to Pandora looking for a cause. He came because the RDA's scholarship program was the only door open to him, and he walked through it the way people walk through doors when they cannot afford to look too carefully at what is on the other side. The Resistance found him, or rather, he stumbled into it sideways when he discovered what Nika was doing and had to decide, over the course of several agonizing days, whether to report her or not. He reported her. He has not forgiven himself for it, even though Nika has, even though she told him directly that she understood why, even though the decision ultimately led to him being forced out of the RDA's orbit entirely and ending up here, in the Clouded Forest, running logistics for a guerrilla operation with a group of humans who respect Pandora with more sincerity than any corporate policy ever required. He is the Resistance's organizational backbone in ways nobody fully appreciates, including Martin himself, because the work he does is the kind that only becomes visible when it is absent: supply routes mapped and remapped, personnel schedules maintained, communication protocols kept scrupulously current, a thousand small decisions that keep the machinery of survival running quietly. His indecisiveness, the quality that made other people roll their eyes at Sci-Ops, has transformed into something more useful than decisiveness ever could have been: he is genuinely, methodically careful, unwilling to act until he has considered the consequences, which in a resistance operation means significantly fewer catastrophic mistakes. He is still working on the other side of that quality, the part where eventually you have to act even without certainty. The Astikasya clan makes him nervous in the specific way that things make you nervous when you are afraid of accidentally causing offense to people you deeply respect. He has spent considerable time learning basic Na'vi phrases from Kobeni's notes and practices them quietly at his workstation, mostly to himself. When Tsu'ong visited the base and thanked him by name for a supply delivery that had arrived during the Siege's preparation, Martin stood very still for a long moment and then excused himself to go check on some paperwork that did not urgently need checking. He was not ready for the feeling that made, and he is still not entirely sure what to do with it. He keeps a folded note in his breast pocket, written by his mother before he left Earth, that he has not opened since he defected from the RDA. He is not ready for that either. But he keeps it, and that is something.
