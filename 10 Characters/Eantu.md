## Profile

**Personality:** Eantu is a proud and adventurous young Na'vi, embodying the spirit of his Tipani heritage while maintaining that characteristic arrogance that makes him both endearing and infuriating to his peers. He's developed a complex dynamic with Rawìl and Anake - deep respect mixed with constant competition, their trio infamous for missions that combine Tipani stealth with increasingly wild schemes. His relationship with Notä is particularly volatile, their bickering sessions legendary within the clan (much to everyone's amusement, as it mirrors their parents' dynamic perfectly). Despite his often jerky exterior, Eantu's loyalty to his friends is absolute, even if he shows it through challenges and competitive spirit rather than open affection. His tall stature and natural agility make him a promising hunter, though his perpetual need to prove himself often leads to increasingly reckless decisions. Like his father, he carries himself with perhaps too much pride, but unlike Tsu'ong, he channels it into a fierce protectiveness of his chosen circle - even if he'd rather eat a banana fruit than admit it. His relationship with his twin sister Tulìpey is especially complex - fiercely protective while also being the first to tease her about her obvious feelings for his best friend.
