## Profile

**Personality:** Atanvi has a family motto: if you move forward you gain two, if you run you gain one. She says it when she is scared. She says it most when she is most scared. She has said it, over the years, in so many situations that it has become something close to a prayer, a small ritual that gets her from the edge of a thing to inside of it, past the point where her body wants to stop and her mind keeps going anyway.

She stutters when she is nervous, which is frequently, because she is a person who feels the weight of other people's attention as something physical, something that lands on her skin and makes it hard to think. She has been a warrior since she was young, trained by Kaymi with a severity that came from complete belief in her, and she is genuinely, formidably skilled, and this fact surprises people every time because she does not look like what they expect when they think of the word formidable. She looks like someone who would rather apologize to a plant for startling it than hurt anything deliberately. Both of these things are true simultaneously. She stopped being confused by this a long time ago.

She came to her own shape through the Eye of Eywa, alongside her clan sister and fellow Spear Txewan, and the experience was not like the stories, not the vast and gentle transformation some describe. It was exactly as vast but it was also frightening and overwhelming and she emerged from it with a clarity about herself that she had not had before and a gratitude so large she did not know how to hold it for several days. She still does not know how to hold it fully. She has stopped trying to and started simply carrying it.

Mìreya is the great fact of her adult life in the same way that breathing is a fact, not something she thinks about constantly but something that, when it is absent, is immediately and catastrophically noticeable. She loves her with a completeness that embarrasses her when she thinks about it too directly, not because she is ashamed of it but because the scale of it makes her feel small in a way that is somehow wonderful, like looking up at the night sky. She is the person Mìreya lets in behind the walls. This is not a small thing to be. She knows exactly what it cost Mìreya to let anyone in there at all, and she treats the knowledge with the reverence it deserves.

Her relationship with 'Ipxerey is the most complicated emotional labor of her adult life and the one she is most committed to. She sees her mother clearly, which means she sees all of it: the wisdom that was paid for by years of causing harm, the care that learned itself slowly from nothing, the strategic mind that still occasionally treats love as a problem to be optimized. She does not pretend these things are not there. She also does not let them be the only thing. She is building a relationship with 'Ipxerey as she finds her now, in this life, in this version, and she is doing it the way she does most things: by moving forward even when she is scared, because moving forward is what gains two.

She apologizes to plants when she startles them. She has always done this. She is going to keep doing it. Rawìl pretends not to notice. Notä has asked her to do it less in front of their friends and Atanvi has promised nothing.

She carries the **Tender Spear** not because she is soft, though she is also soft, but because she understands that the mercy that requires the most courage is the kind you extend to someone who has not yet earned it. She has been that person. She has been on the receiving end of unearned mercy and knows what it made possible. She wants to be that for whoever needs it, even when it is hard. Especially when it is hard.

> **Personality:** A naturally shy but deeply determined Na'vi who often stutters when nervous but shows remarkable strength when protecting others. Despite her timidity in social situations, she's one of the clan's finest warriors - though she'd blush to hear it said. She lives by the family motto "if you move forward you gain two, if you run you gain one," using it to push through her own doubts and help others face theirs. As the youngest daughter of the Olo'eyktan and Tsahìk, she carries both leadership and spiritual responsibilities, though she often doubts herself until Mìreya or her siblings remind her of her worth. Her connection to Eywa is profound but endearingly awkward - she's been known to apologize to plants for startling them. She underwent her gender transition through the Eye of Eywa alongside Txewan, maintaining her new form through sacred herbs and medicines, an experience that deepened both their bond and their understanding of themselves. She leads the parent group with a mixture of stuttered instructions and unwavering determination, especially when the children need protecting. Most comfortable with her chosen family - blood-oath siblings Tsa'rawm and Txewan, beloved mate Mìreya, and all their children - she nonetheless pushes herself to help anyone in need, even if she has to stammer through it. Her skill in combat and ikran flying is remarkable, though she'd rather teach younglings about forest patterns than fight. Despite her anxieties, she's the first to stand against injustice, often surprising everyone (including herself) with her fierceness when her loved ones are threatened. This duality - the shy teacher who can become a warrior in an instant - makes her a unique and beloved member of the clan. As of now, and as an honor she has as the younger daughter of the olo’eyktan, she serves as **the Merciful** among the Nine Spears, carrying the **Tender Spear** as a reminder that compassion and combat can coexist.
