## Profile

**Personality:** 'Ipxerey became who she is now through a process she would describe, if pressed, as necessary and that she would not wish on anyone. She was not always the tsahìk whose voice carries the weight of genuine wisdom. She was not always someone whose guidance could be trusted to serve the person being guided rather than the guide's own ends. She was someone who loved her family with the whole of herself and then watched everything burn and then made the burning mean something by spending the next years of her life turning it into a weapon. The weapon was precise and patient and aimed at a specific target and she was very good at using it. She was also, in the process, doing to her daughters what had been done to her family: making them instruments of her purpose rather than people with their own.

She knows this. She has always known this, in the way that people who are very intelligent and very focused know the thing they are doing wrong and do it anyway because the goal feels more real than the cost. What she did not fully know, until Eywa made it undeniable, was that the cost was the point. You do not get to use people you love as pieces and also keep them whole. You have to choose, and she had chosen, and the choosing had consequences she had not let herself see clearly until she could no longer look away.

Her redemption is not a clean arc. She would be suspicious of anyone who described it as one. What happened was more like the slow process of a tree growing back after fire: it looks dead for a long time, and then one day something green appears, and then another day more green, and it is never quite the same tree that burned, but it is alive, and alive is not nothing. She learned to put what she knew, the strategic precision, the cold intelligence, the ability to see exactly how a system works and where to apply pressure, into the service of the people in front of her rather than the objective behind them. This is harder than it sounds. She still catches herself planning instead of listening. She still, occasionally, sees the board before she sees the person. The difference now is that she stops, and corrects, and asks the question she should have been asking all along: what does this person actually need.

Her ability to communicate with those who have passed through Eywa's network is not something she takes lightly or uses casually. It is the most sacred thing she does, and she does it with a reverence that surprises people who knew her before, who remember her as someone for whom strategy came before reverence. What she found, in the roots of the Tree of Souls, was that the people she had lost were not gone. They were changed. And what they carried into the network was not the shape of what she had wanted from them but the shape of who they actually were, which was sometimes different, and which she was learning, slowly and with difficulty, to love on their own terms.

She trained Sänrri as tsakarem with the same precision she once applied to everything else and with a genuine care she is still learning to show without armor around it. Sänrri's particular combination of profound faith and complete scientific abandon once baffled her. Now she finds it clarifying in ways she cannot fully articulate. She delegated the role when the time was right not as a concession but as a recognition: Sänrri carries it better than she did. This is not a small thing to admit. She admitted it without being asked. This is also not a small thing.

She and Eyfuwen are the two people who have known each other longest and who understand each other most completely, which means that they sometimes drive each other completely insane. She loves him with the particular ferocity of someone who knows exactly how close she came to losing him, not to death, but to who she was becoming, and who is grateful every day that he was stubborn enough to stay in the room when she was most frightening. She does not say this aloud. He knows. This is, after many years, enough.

She is the living proof of the thing the clan believes: that what a person has done is not the same as what a person is capable of becoming. She carries this not as comfort but as responsibility.

> **Personality:** Wise and experienced, always offering advice and guidance to those around her, 'Ipxerey embodies the profound transformation from a woman driven by loss and vengeance into a spiritual leader whose depths of understanding come from having walked through darkness and emerged into light. As the de facto spiritual leader of the clan alongside her mate and olo'eyktan Eyfuwen, she guides the clan in connecting deeply with Eywa, her own journey from the calculating, manipulative strategist she once was into a compassionate teacher making her uniquely qualified to help others find their way through their own spiritual growth. Her vast knowledge of Na'vi history and traditions is complemented by hard-won wisdom about the dangers of letting grief and anger consume one's purpose, often acting as the living memory of the clan while ensuring that past mistakes are never repeated. 'Ipxerey possesses the unique ability to communicate with ancestors through the roots of the Tree of Souls, providing guidance in times of crisis, her own experience with loss and the weight of difficult choices lending gravity and authenticity to her counsel. She maintains the keen intellect and strategic mind that once made her formidable, but now channels these gifts toward building and protecting rather than destroying, her devotion to her family, especially her grandchildren Rawìl, Notä, and Lì'may, serving as both her anchor and her greatest source of joy. Having trained both her daughters-in-law Mìreya and Sänrri as tsakarem, she made the wise decision to delegate the role to Sänrri due to her position as mate to the eyktanay and eldest daughter Kaymi, demonstrating the same calculated precision she once used for darker purposes now applied to ensuring the clan's spiritual future. Beneath her serene exterior lies someone who understands the true cost of power and vengeance, making her guidance all the more valuable to those who seek to avoid the paths that once nearly consumed her, her transformation from one who manipulated others to one who nurtures their authentic growth representing the very best of what redemption and spiritual awakening can achieve.
