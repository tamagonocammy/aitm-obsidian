## Profile

**Personality:** Despite his striking appearance with warrior markings and the stern demeanor typical of the seafaring Tayrangi clan, Txafwe speaks with unexpected politeness and maintains a straightforward, easygoing nature that contrasts with his intimidating exterior. Born to the Tayrangi, the Ikran People of the Eastern Sea, his formative years were spent as a gifted ceremonial rhythm-keeper who led a small ensemble of song-sharers that traveled between neighboring settlements. However, his wandering spirit eventually called him beyond the familiar waters of his homeland - embarking on an epic solo journey across over 300 miles of Pandora on his ikran, from the Eastern Sea to the mysterious Western Frontier. It was during this great migration that he discovered the mist-shrouded Clouded Forest and the hidden Astikasya clan, where he met and fell in love with Rängay. Choosing love over his nomadic lifestyle, he settled at Sheltering Falls and started a family, bringing Eastern Sea musical traditions and ikran flight knowledge to his new home. During his youth, using traditional percussion instruments carved from hollow seedpods and resonant driftwood from Tayrangi shores, he pushed his performance group to excellence, coordinating their appearances and securing rare opportunities to play at inter-clan gatherings. However, during the most significant opportunity of their young lives - a performance before the Tsahìk of five major clans - Txafwe arrived to find himself abandoned by his ensemble-mates, who had succumbed to fears of inadequacy. Left alone before the assembled spiritual leaders, the experience scarred him deeply, leading him to eventually seek refuge with the Astikasya clan where he found healing through his bond with Rängay and their children Nìla and Mawpay. His capture by the Mangkwan clan, along with his mate and the Lost Cohort, became his greatest nightmare realized - once again, those he trusted were torn away, though this time through violence rather than abandonment. During their imprisonment under Varang's corrupt regime, Txafwe witnessed the spiritual heresy and collaboration with Quaritch that made his skin crawl, his musician's soul recoiling from how they perverted sacred rhythms for twisted ceremonies. Though he presents as calm and collected since their escape, those closest to him recognize the deep trauma in how he constantly questions his own reliability - he struggles with intense guilt over being unable to protect his family and the Cohort from capture, his trust issues now extending to doubting whether others can truly depend on him. His playful moments with Rängay - balancing fruits on her head during meditation or leaving carefully arranged shell collections - represent his desperate attempts to rebuild the gentle intimacy that captivity nearly destroyed. The knowledge that his children waited at Sheltering Falls, not knowing if their parents would return, haunts him deeply, making his protective instincts even more fierce while simultaneously making him terrified of failing them again. During the Siege, Txafwe’s ceremonial rhythm-keeping training found new purpose as he coordinated the Scattered Ashes’ movements in combat, his drums and calls guiding allies through the chaos in a way that transformed his greatest trauma: being abandoned during performance, into his greatest strength as a battlefield conductor. Fighting alongside Rängay and the Lost Cohort he’d failed to protect during their Mangkwan captivity, he finally had the chance to be reliable, to be present, to be the warrior who didn’t let his family get torn away. His participation in the final coordinated strike against Luka, moving in perfect synchronization with the Nine Spears and his fellow Ashes, answered the question that had haunted him since his youth: he was not the Na'vi who would be left behind, but the one others could depend on. The Siege didn’t erase his guilt, but it gave him something more powerful—proof through blood and victory that when it mattered most, he would not fail again.
