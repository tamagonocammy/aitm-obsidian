## Profile

**Personality:** Easygoing and musically gifted, Nìla has an innate talent for finding rhythm in everything around her. The 13-year-old serves as the unofficial peacekeeper among the clan's children, her laid-back attitude balancing the more intense personalities. Despite her relaxed demeanor, she approaches her music with surprising focus, often lost in complex compositions that blend traditional Na'vi melodies with patterns she discovers in nature. Her bond with her ikran Ìley is uniquely musical - they communicate through rhythmic signals rather than conventional commands. The other kids trust her with their secrets, knowing she's more interested in creating the perfect sound than spreading gossip. Though she pretends to be too cool for most things, her face lights up with genuine excitement whenever anyone shows interest in her latest musical discovery. She manages her position as Txafwe and Rängay's daughter by adopting a go-with-the-flow attitude that lets her move between the clan's different social circles with ease. When not providing the soundtrack to the other children's adventures, she can be found experimenting with makeshift instruments or teaching the younger kids songs that inevitably drive their parents crazy.

## Notes

- **She/her (ruled 2026-11-21).** Nìla is a girl. She knows it and asks to be called she. Gender among the Na'vi is fluid and nebulous, and she has years before anything is final.
- **The Eye (ruled 2026-11-21).** The Eye of Eywa is a coming-of-age rite, like everything else the People mark that way. It gives a Na'vi a place with the People, and it is how Nìla will transition when she comes of age.
- **In the meantime (ruled 2026-11-21).** Her mother [[Rängay]] (Taki) gets her some things that she takes. What they are is not established.
- **Trans mother and trans daughter (ruled 2026-11-21).** Rängay is a trans woman and Nìla is a trans girl. Her father [[Txafwe]] is a trans man. Her parents both came to themselves through the Eye of Eywa.
- **Surname (ruled 2026-11-21).** Her source name is Riko Yahata, the same family surname as [[Mawpay]]'s source name, Mirei Yahata.
- **Inspiration (ruled 2026-11-21).** Mizuki Akiyama from *Project Sekai*, for personality only; the source does not state Mizuki's gender. Everyone tells Nìla things and Nìla tells nobody anything.
- **Name ending (open).** Her full name still ends in -'itan. Whether it changes now or at the Eye is Millie's call.
