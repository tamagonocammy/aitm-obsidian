## Profile

**Personality:** Easygoing and musically gifted, Nìla has an innate talent for finding rhythm in everything around him. The 13-year-old serves as the unofficial peacekeeper among the clan's children, his laid-back attitude balancing the more intense personalities. Despite his relaxed demeanor, he approaches his music with surprising focus, often lost in complex compositions that blend traditional Na'vi melodies with patterns he discovers in nature. His bond with his ikran Ìley is uniquely musical - they communicate through rhythmic signals rather than conventional commands. The other kids trust him with their secrets, knowing he's more interested in creating the perfect sound than spreading gossip. Though he pretends to be too cool for most things, his face lights up with genuine excitement whenever anyone shows interest in his latest musical discovery. He manages his position as Txafwe and Rängay's son by adopting a go-with-the-flow attitude that lets him move between the clan's different social circles with ease. When not providing the soundtrack to the other children's adventures, he can be found experimenting with makeshift instruments or teaching the younger kids songs that inevitably drive their parents crazy.
