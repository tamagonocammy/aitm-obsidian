## Profile

**Personality:** Lawarey is ten and has a class-president aura in a small kid's body. She settles the children's disputes, and when someone has been wronged she hands over a stone whose meaning only she knows. The stones are pretty ones, picked with her mother Apayì's eye, and she gives different ones to different friends. She counts heads at every gathering and notices who is missing. It comes from Pxayu's fear of being abandoned and Apayì's gift for noticing, and from waiting at the Falls while both of them were held by the Mangkwan. When she is overruled she turns her back with her arms crossed and her nose in the air, and says hmph, and a few minutes later she does the kind thing anyway.

She notices small things and acts on them. She fixes Lì'may's hair ornaments, brings extra food for whoever might need it, and remembers what everyone likes without being told. She does not start adventures, but she keeps the group together, and she has a peace offering ready before a fight is over. Her best friend is Mawpay, the other sensible one. Kxayì is nothing like her, wears her out and fascinates her at the same time, and the two of them are friends. Mìreya has noticed her interest in healing and teaches her in small pieces.
