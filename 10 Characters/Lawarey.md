## Profile

**Personality:** Perceptive and thoughtful, Lawarey moves through the clan with quiet awareness that belies her 10 years. Neither shy nor bold, she observes more than she speaks, filing away details others miss. Her natural caretaking instinct emerges in small, meaningful gestures – adjusting Lì'may's hair ornaments, preparing extra food for whoever might need it, remembering everyone's preferences without being told. Despite her reserved demeanor, she forms deep friendships, particularly with Mawpay and surprisingly with the much more exuberant Kxayì, whose boundless energy she finds both exhausting and fascinating. Lawarey inherited her mother Pxayu's empathetic nature and her mother Apayì's eye for beauty, creating a unique blend that manifests in her tendency to notice exactly what others need before they ask. Her emerging interest in healing practices has caught Mìreya's attention, leading to informal apprenticeship moments that Lawarey treasures. Though she rarely initiates adventures, she's often the one who keeps everyone together, quietly bridging conflicts with perfectly timed observations or small peace offerings she's prepared in advance. Her collection of pretty stones serves as both comfort objects and a silent language of friendship – she gives specific ones to different friends based on meanings only she fully understands.
