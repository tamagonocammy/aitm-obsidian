## Profile

**Personality:** Aliya is an intuitive and laid-back person who likes to take her time with everything she does. She's calm, honest, and never in a rush, using soft language while maintaining a relaxed presence that stands out among her fellow Resistance members. Known for her unique divination skills using stones, she often provides surprisingly accurate insights about people and situations, though she's averse to getting involved with weapons and armed conflict. Despite her peaceful nature, she's one of the more level-headed members of the Resistance and serves as a stabilizing influence during tense situations. Her background in animal care and tea production from her hometown has given her practical skills in maintenance and resource management. She's very observant of others and often apologetic when problems arise, preferring to work behind the scenes to keep things running smoothly. Though she speaks gently, she's not afraid to offer honest advice or gentle teasing to her friends, especially about romantic matters. Her fortune-telling abilities have proven surprisingly accurate in predicting future events, making her an unexpectedly valuable asset to the Resistance's strategic planning, even if she prefers to focus on the spiritual and peaceful aspects of their mission.
