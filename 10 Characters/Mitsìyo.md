## Profile

**Personality:** Gentle and nurturing with an unshakeable inner strength, Mitsìyo serves as the clan's emotional anchor and one of its most respected elders. Her warm presence and natural empathy make her the go-to confidante for clan members of all ages, from worried parents seeking advice to youngsters needing comfort after difficult days. As a master weaver and keeper of domestic traditions, she creates beautiful ceremonial garments and teaches younger Na'vi the intricate patterns that tell their clan's history through textile art. Her patient teaching style and infinite capacity for understanding helped shape both Txewan's gentle nature and 'Eweyn's confidence, though she never plays favorites among her beloved grandchildren. Despite her soft-spoken demeanor, Mitsìyo possesses the quiet authority that comes from decades of wise counsel, and even the most stubborn clan members find themselves listening when she speaks. Her bond with Tse'hayu spans many seasons of shared music-making and child-rearing, their partnership serving as a model of enduring love for the younger generations. She has an uncanny ability to sense when someone is struggling emotionally, often appearing with warm food and gentle words exactly when they're needed most. Her greatest joy comes from watching her family thrive, especially seeing how Txewan's musical gifts and 'Eweyn's adventurous spirit carry forward the best traditions of their lineage while creating new ones.
