## Profile

**Personality:** The first thing you need to understand about Tsa'rawm te Haykxa Tse'kam'itan is that he grew up with nothing. Not the Na'vi understanding of nothing, which still contains Pandora and Eywa and the living network. The specific, grinding, human-shaped nothing of severe poverty, of not knowing whether there would be food, of surviving on the edges of things that were not built to accommodate him. He does not talk about this often. He does not need to, because everything he is now is still partly constructed from the memory of what it felt like to have nothing, and that memory makes him treat every meal and every hunt and every morning with his children and every moment of belonging with the fierce appreciation of someone who knows exactly what the alternative looks like.

  

He is loud. He is crass. He arrives in every room like weather. His humor is the kind that makes people either laugh or wince and he is genuinely not sure which outcome he prefers, though he will tell you he prefers the wince while laughing. This is the exterior, and it is not a performance in the sense of being false, it is simply the specific shape his joy took when he finally found something worth being joyful about, which turned out to be enormous. But underneath the noise is something older and quieter and more careful, the specific wisdom of someone who was forced to read situations before he had the luxury of responding to them, who learned to know the difference between a threat and a posture before he learned to read anything else. His bluntness creates unnecessary conflicts sometimes. His empathy resolves most of them.

  

The direhorse bond is the thing most people find hardest to explain about him. He communicates with them differently than anyone else the clan has observed, not through the technical precision of the tsaheylu alone but through something more continuous and conversational, the way you speak to someone you have known long enough that silences have content. The Zeswa, the plains clan that gave the Western Frontier its **Great Games** and its tradition of riding that is almost dance, have a word for this quality of riding. Tsa'rawm did not grow up Zeswa. He became what the Zeswa describe anyway, which suggests the quality precedes the tradition.

  

Kayan adopted him as a brother and it remade both of them. Tsa'rawm does not use the word brother as a technical designation. He uses it as a description of something that happened to his whole understanding of what he was made of, which expanded to include Kayan and then Lamaytxa and then Sänume and then everyone they brought with them into the family that grew around the original fact of belonging. His loyalty to these people is not conditional and does not have a ceiling. He would give his life for any of them without the theatrical hesitation that precedes some declarations of loyalty. He would give it immediately and practically and then wish he had found a more efficient way to do it.

  

Atanvi and Txewan are his blood-oath siblings, the bond forged in the specific grammar of shared commitment rather than shared origin, and he is the reason both of them became more themselves in public. This is not something he planned. He simply cannot be around people he loves without making it somewhat louder and more physical than they initially expected, and it turns out that being loved loudly is a useful thing to experience if you have previously lived in the quieter kinds of doubt. Atanvi needed someone who was not going to be careful around her potential. Txewan needed someone who found her funny when she was trying to be invisible. Tsa'rawm provided both, enthusiastically and without being asked.

  

He met Rewon during one of the worst afternoons of her life, which also happened to involve a thanator, and he was not calm or subtle about the intervention. She was irritated. He was louder than the situation required. He was also correct, which she has never fully admitted to him because she does not need to and he already knows. What happened between them afterward was slow and built on evidence and the particular trust of two people who have very different relationships with the word. He waited, without impatience, for the evidence to accumulate. This is the quality people most consistently underestimate about him: the patience. It does not look like patience because it is located inside someone who does everything else at high volume. But when there is no immediate action needed, he is the most unhurried person in the Astikasya. He will sit with a meal for hours. He will watch his children sleep without needing them to wake up.

  

During the **Siege of the Falls**, he killed Till. This is not a heroic statement in the triumphant sense. Till was a thing that was happening to his home and to his family and it needed to stop, and Tsa'rawm stopped it the way he stops things that need stopping, completely and without ceremony. The **Scarred Spear** was already scarred before the Siege and it gained more during it and he carries every mark on it with the same matter-of-fact ownership that he carries his own. A scar is a record. He does not try to make records prettier than they are.

  

The chainsaw he recovered from the wreckage of the **ANAKT Fireteam's** last stand is something the clan regards with a mixture of awe and wariness. Tsa'rawm regards it with something closer to recognition. He does not fully know how to explain this and does not try. There is a quality of the thing that feels familiar, a specific honesty about what it is and what it does and the fact that it does not pretend to be anything else. He is also simply delighted by it in a way that he would not describe as spiritual but that the elders have begun to discuss in those terms, quietly, and in his absence.

  

He is **the Thunderous**. Not because he is the loudest, though he frequently is, but because thunder is what happens when something enormous moves through the world and does not apologize for being enormous. He does not apologize. He learned very early that the world does not reward that particular performance of smallness, and by the time he found a place worth belonging to he had already decided that taking up his full amount of space was the only honest way to occupy it.

  

He takes nothing for granted. This is the thing underneath all of it, the quiet root of everything loud above it. Every meal. Every hunt. Every morning his children are alive and his mate is beside him and his siblings are reachable. Every day the Falls are standing. He has known the absence of all of these things in different ways and the knowing stays. The **Scarred Spear** he carries is the most honest possible description of what he is: something that went through a great deal and did not break, and bears the record of having gone through it, and keeps going.

> **Personality:** Energetic and optimistic, a warrior to the core, always ready to help and protect his own. Tsa'rawm is an expert direhorse rider, demonstrating great skill and dexterity in races and handling these majestic animals. His connection with direhorses is almost mystical, able to communicate with them in a way that amazes even the most experienced Na'vi. His bravery in battle is matched only by his compassion outside of it, always willing to help any clan member in need. Crass and loud in his way of being and talking, Tsa'rawm is the most boisterous and loud of the bond siblings alongside Atanvi and Txewan, him being the reason that they have become more outgoing and extroverted. He was adopted as a brother by Kayan, taking his surname and becoming brothers in all but blood, this being repeated also with his sisters Lamaytxa and Sänume. Despite his rough exterior and tendency toward blunt honesty that sometimes creates unnecessary conflicts, he has a good sense of empathy and is willing to save those in danger as much as he can, showing his kind-hearted nature beneath his shortcomings. His personality comes off as rude and harsh in an almost childish way, but this masks a deeper wisdom born from hardship. As someone who lived in severe poverty before joining the clan, he doesn't take anything for granted and approaches life with infectious enthusiasm. His adventurous spirit means he's always willing to try new experiences and isn't afraid of the unknown, often being the first to volunteer for dangerous missions. Though he can be impatient and reckless, he's oddly patient when there's no immediate action needed, content to relax and enjoy peaceful moments with his family. His loyalty to his chosen siblings is absolute, and while he loves to tease them mercilessly, he would give his life to protect any of them without hesitation. As a gift for his many merits in service for the clan, he now stands as **the Thunderous** among the Nine Spears, his **Scarred Spear** bearing witness to countless battles fought to protect his family and clan, while also wielding a fearsome **chainsaw** he found in the fight against the ANAKT Fireteam and their Mangkwan raiding army.
