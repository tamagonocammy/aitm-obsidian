## Profile

If the other Recoms are tragedies, Luka is a perfected blasphemy: a soul not resurrected but distilled into its most potent poison. His neural backup performed a terrible alchemy, refining base cruelty into infinite patience and raw obsession into a dark, meticulous art. He studies people not to understand them, but to find the precise point where pressure becomes fracture. His Recombinant form, engineered to the exacting specifications of Dr. Heperu, is a weapon of terrifying elegance: built for endurance that outlasts hope, grace that disarms, and deception that becomes truth. He moved through the Mangkwan clan’s ranks not as an outsider, but as a convert more fervent than the born, wearing their red, black, and white ash like a second skin, singing their war songs with a conviction that masked a hollow core desperate to belong. He was the Fireteam’s true pivot, the silent architect for whom Ivan’s tactics and Hyuna’s morale were mere instruments. His relationship with Hyuna was his masterpiece of corruption: a possession forged from the guilt of her brother Hyunwoo’s death, a love indistinguishable from a slow-acting toxin. His campaign during the Siege was his ultimate canvas. He hunted the Astikasya not merely to kill, but to dissect the spiritual bonds that defined them, targeting Sänrri’s hard-won faith and Syayvi’s fragile redemption. His defeat was not a military failure, but a spiritual catastrophe. Broken by Tsu'lan, the Spears, and the Scattered Ashes, he was reduced to a ruin of flesh and failing systems. Yet, from that ruin, a new purpose was forged. Hauled before Recom Miles Quaritch and the Mangkwan clan’s tsahìk Varang in Bridgehead, he offered no excuse, only a cold, simmering vow that earned him not execution, but a more terrible mandate. Now, he is being re-forged with a chillingly specific intent. The blunt instrument of the ANAKT fireteam is gone. In its place is a scalpel. His all-consuming fixation has narrowed from the clan to the man who embodies its spirit: Tsu'lan. Luka will become a reflection, a phantom limb. He will study the Immovable Spear’s honor, his tactics, his very essence, not to replicate them, but to wear them as the ultimate disguise. His new war is not of ash and fire, but of perception and trust. He will become the living doubt, the flawless impersonation of their protector, aimed at proving that the strongest bond can be severed from within by a perfect, loving lie. The story of the ANAKT Fireteam is a closed chapter of carnage. The story of the Strangler Fig is just beginning: a silent, patient campaign to become the crack in the foundation of the Astikasya clan’s world.
