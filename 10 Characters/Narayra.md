## Profile

Composed, persuasive and admired across the Western Frontier, Narayra is the voice that speaks for the Falls in other clans' Hometrees. Like Maki in *MyGO*, who only appears as a name and a nameplate, she is present in the story only as someone others mention until after the Siege. She was away for the Mangkwan captivity, the Occupation and the Siege, and came home when Sarentu storytellers spread the news of the Nine Spears.

She arrives just after Tsu'lan leaves on the Hunt and asks the question nobody else will: why is everyone acting as if this is all fine? From where she has been, the clan looks like it lost itself. She is contemptuous toward Sänrri and toward the Spears who were not born Astikasya. Syayvi's *"You are not wrong about me"* is the first thing that gets through her composure.
