## Profile

*Personality:* Miri is a whirlwind of internal fury masked by profound, silent tension. He moves with the wary stillness of a feral animal that has learned motion attracts predators, yet his mind is in constant, furious calculation. His mechanical genius is not a talent he can turn off; it is a hyper-awareness, a sixth sense. He perceives the stress-song of fatigued alloys, sees thermal bleed in power conduits, and feels impending failure as a physical wrongness deep within his own bones. This intensely sharpened perception was forged during his time in the RDA's engineering corps and honed by terror after his defection. Witnessing the Mangkwan clan's occupation of Bridgehead, a direct consequence of the recent collaboration between Recom Miles Quaritch and Varang, prompted him to flee, running for days through Pandora's jungles. This heightened state was a result of days spent in terror, running through the jungles of Pandora., makes him seem detached or cold. In truth, he is acutely, painfully observant, processing every person and environment as a complex system to be analyzed for both threat and function. His deepest fear is becoming a disposable tool again, even for a righteous cause, and his driving force is a desperate, almost violent need for autonomy. He does not offer loyalty; it must be earned through demonstrated respect for his skills and his right to choose how to use them. He communicates in flat, technical statements, his voice often a toneless rasp, as if emotional expression is a system he has deliberately disconnected. Praise makes him uneasy, feeling like an appraisal of his utility, while criticism of his work, if technically accurate, is the only form of attention he readily accepts. Beneath this engineered exterior lies a deep well of moral conflict and unspoken yearning. He is capable of fierce, protective loyalty to the few who bridge the gap to his humanity, not through pity but through shared silent language of problem-solving. He connects through action, not words: repairing a comrade's sidearm without being asked, leaving a perfectly customized tool where it's needed, taking the most dangerous watch so others can rest. His humor, when it rarely surfaces, is bone-dry and technical, so absurd it loops back to being funny. He is haunted less by violence he has seen than by the elegant, destructive potential he can envision in any tool, a constant reminder of the weapon he refuses to become. His defection from the RDA was less an escape and more a terrified, stumbling exile after being ambushed by the Nine Spears in the jungle. Facing death, he begged for his life and mentioned Nika Nanaura-Fardin, Silver-hand. That recognition became his salvation. Given a waterskin, journey-bread, and a direction with the message "The Nine Spears send you," he walked for three days to reach the Resistance’s new HQ in the Clouded Forest. When Nika tossed him a wrench and gestured to a gutted and stolen AMP suit without words, he understood the unspoken contract: prove his worth through his hands, and he would earn his place. In the rare moments his guard drops, he reveals the soul of a stranded artist, forever seeking to build and mend in a world designed for breaking. On his workbench sits a half-finished upgrade to Nika's prosthetic, integrated with subtle Na'vi aesthetics, a silent thank you for the life she gave him and the person he is becoming.
