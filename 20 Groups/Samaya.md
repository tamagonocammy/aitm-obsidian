> [!info] Synopsis written 2026-11-12 from the notes in this vault. Nothing in it is new canon; gaps are listed under Still open.

The Samaya are the family of the clan's two leaders, Olo'eyktan [[Eyfuwen]] and Tsahìk [['Ipxerey]], together with their daughters and the mates, children and adopted child who grew out of them. They were one of the three families that settled Sheltering Falls after the migration *(Prologue era note)*. In the story they hold the clan's leadership and its spiritual guidance in one household, and two of the Nine Spears, Kaymi and Atanvi, are daughters of the house.
### Who they are
- [[Eyfuwen]]: olo'eyktan; mate of 'Ipxerey, father of Kaymi and Atanvi, grandfather of Rawìl, Notä and Lì'may; old war-companion of [[Tse'hayu]].
- [['Ipxerey]]: tsahìk; mother of Kaymi and Atanvi; teacher of Sänrri (tsakarem), [[Sänume]] (healer) and [[Mìreya]] (strategy and botany).
- [[Kaymi]]: eyktanay, The Brazen of the Nine Spears; daughter of Eyfuwen and 'Ipxerey; mate of Sänrri, adoptive mother of Lì'may.
- [[Atanvi]]: The Merciful of the Nine Spears; Kaymi's sibling; mate of Mìreya, mother of Rawìl and Notä; blood-oath sibling of [[Tsa'rawm]] and [[Txewan]].
- [[Sänrri]]: tsakarem, formerly the human Dr. Tomori Takamatsu; mate of Kaymi, adoptive mother of Lì'may; 'Ipxerey's heir in spiritual leadership.
- [[Mìreya]]: tiretu, botanist and strategic advisor; mate of Atanvi, mother of Rawìl and Notä; close as sisters to [[Syulang]] and [[Rewon]].
- [[Rawìl]]: 15, future eyktanay and master weaver; son of Atanvi and Mìreya; best friend of [[Anake]], courting [[Tulìpey]].
- [[Notä]]: 13, inventor and hunter; daughter of Atanvi and Mìreya, named after her maternal grandmother; rival of [[Eantu]] and of her cousin [[Kxayì]].
- [[Lì'may]]: 3, adopted daughter of Kaymi and Sänrri, rescued from an RDA facility; plays with [[Mìyo]] and [[Mìrì]].
### What defines them
The house is a line of leaders and healers: Eyfuwen has encouraged his daughters since childhood, and 'Ipxerey came from darkness and vengeance and has built her way toward light while her children watched *(Relationship Lore)*. Sänrri entered the family through 'Ipxerey's mentorship and through Kaymi, who chose her "with the same ferocity she brings to combat" *(Relationship Lore)*. Lì'may "made them a family before they fully realized they already were one" *(Relationship Lore)*. Atanvi and Mìreya began on opposite sides of a tension, when Tsu'ong challenged Atanvi for Mìreya and lost, and became a couple who "chose each other repeatedly" *(Relationship Lore)*.
### Ruled on 2026-11-12
- The Samaya are the direct descendants of Tunskxe, the Second Toruk Makto and founder of the [[Astikasya]], who lived thousands of years ago. The line runs through [[Eyfuwen]], and Rawìl and Notä inherit that legacy. The descent is history and symbol and gives no inherited power. See [[Ambition and Hope — The Ones Who Know All]].
