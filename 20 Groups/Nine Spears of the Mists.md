The Nine Spears are the elite warrior cadre of the Astikasya Clan, forming the protective ring around their gentle kingdom. Led by Kaymi, the eyktanay, they represent the clan's commitment to defending life without being consumed by war. Each Spear carries both a title and a ceremonial weapon, embodying a philosophy that binds them as much as it arms them.

  

The members are: **Kaymi** (the Brazen, wielding the *Crimson Spear*), **Atanvi** (the Merciful, carrying the *Tender Spear*), **Txewan** (the Phantom, holding the *Singing Spear*), **Tsa'rawm** (the Thunderous, wielding the *Scarred Spear*), **Rewon** (the Unerring, using the *Balanced Spear*), **Tsu'lan** (the Righteous, bearing the *Immovable Spear*), **Syayvi** (the Penitent, with the *Ashen Spear* crafted from wood burned during the Siege of the Falls), **Tsu'ong** (the Wise, carrying the *Tempered Spear*), and **Ketxe** (the Unbound, wielding the *Unleashed Spear*).

  

The Nine Spears embody the Astikasya principle of being "a gentle kingdom ringed in spears." Their strength serves not conquest but defense, creating space for life, song, and peace to flourish. Each ceremonial spear represents a sacred vow: to lead with courage, to heal with compassion, to guide through shadow, to endure all storms, to strike with precision, to stand immovable, to seek redemption, to counsel with wisdom, and to fight unbound by past chains.

  

The spears are carried during major ceremonies and critical battles; when planted in a circle, they form a symbolic protective ring around the clan's sacred spaces. Warriors who join the Nine Spears receive their ceremonial weapon through a consecration ritual at the Tree of Souls, where the spear is blessed by the tsahìk and the bearer swears their oath before Eywa.

  

Yet the Spears are not merely weapons. They are declarations, each one a testament to the soul of the warrior who carries it, shaped by hands that will never carry one themselves.

  

During the Siege of the Falls, the Nine Spears stood against the ANAKT Fireteam and the Mangkwan clan's forces, each facing enemies that tested not just their combat skills but their core philosophies. Syayvi earned her place among them through her heroic defense despite being among the Scattered Ashes, the Ashen Spear — the only one ever reforged — symbolizing both her redemption and her unborn child Sìlpey. Tsu'ong and Ketxe, once outsiders seeking *uturu*, proved their worth in battle and their commitment to the clan's values, earning their spears in recognition of the wisdom gained through humility and the freedom won through breaking chains.

  

Their stand became legend, remembered not merely as battle but as living proof of their beliefs. Some say they carry the spirit of the clan's ancient founder; others believe they were simply Eywa's chosen in a time of great need. Either way, their tale endures as a reminder that true strength lies not in violence, but in the courage to protect gentleness itself. Nine points of a single spear, raised in defense of a gentle kingdom.
