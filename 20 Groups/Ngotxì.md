> [!info] Synopsis written 2026-11-12 from the notes in this vault. Nothing in it is new canon; gaps are listed under Still open.

The Ngotxì are the family of the clan's musicians, cooks and weavers, and one of the three families that settled Sheltering Falls *(Prologue era note)*. It has two generations of elders and parents: Tse'hayu and Mitsìyo, their children Txewan and 'Eweyn, and Txewan's mate Syulang with their three children. Txewan is The Phantom of the Nine Spears, so the family also sits inside the clan's war circle.
### Who they are
- [[Tse'hayu]]: elder, master cook and instrument maker; former warrior-bard who fought with Eyfuwen; mate of Mitsìyo, father of Txewan and 'Eweyn; the first to put an instrument in Txewan's hands.
- [[Mitsìyo]]: elder, master weaver and the clan's emotional anchor; mate of Tse'hayu, mother of Txewan and 'Eweyn.
- [[Txewan]]: The Phantom of the Nine Spears; composer and singer; mate of Syulang, mother of Mìyo, Mìrì and Netezu; blood-oath sibling of [[Atanvi]] and [[Tsa'rawm]]; also called Txewì by Tswesya.
- [['Eweyn]]: young adult explorer and Resistance liaison; Txewan's younger sister; romantic rival and partner of [[Sänume]].
- [[Syulang]]: maternal counselor to the clan; mate of Txewan; close as sisters to [[Mìreya]] and [[Rewon]].
- [[Mìyo]]: 8, cheerful dancer; twin of Mìrì, daughter of Txewan and Syulang.
- [[Mìrì]]: 8, the older twin by minutes, calm planner who looks like Txewan; daughter of Txewan and Syulang.
- [[Netezu]]: 1, son of Txewan and Syulang; loves his mothers' voices and music.
### What defines them
Music runs through the family. Tse'hayu taught Txewan first, then [[Tìfyaw]] and [[Tswesya]] guided her, and Txewan and Syulang "met through music" *(Relationship Lore)*. Txewan, Syulang, [[Katirì]] and Tswesya form the Woven Harmony, a music circle that became a chosen family *(Relationship Lore)*. Mitsìyo and Tse'hayu are held up as "a model of enduring love" *(Mitsìyo's profile)*. Syulang has built her household as a place where Txewan's anxiety "has less power" *(Relationship Lore)*.
