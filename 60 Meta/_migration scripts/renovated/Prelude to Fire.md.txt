## Synopsis

Sänrri cannot sleep at a fire on the far shore of the Lake, so she tells Kaymi what happened to her. She was first held in a place with white lights, where she lived in a blue body grown in a tank. When a Toruk Makto rose against the people who held her, she followed that Toruk Makto's people, and their hunters gave her a name. *(scene: Welcome Back to Trench, part I)*

The Mangkwan then captured Sänrri, the Lost Cohort and Txafwe, and held them for months. The scene describes them singing Na'vi songs backward and forcing their kuru into people who did not want it. One night someone opened a path for Sänrri, and she never saw who it was. The Rulings Log names that helper as Roneyu, born Mangkwan, who escaped unnoticed. *(scene: Welcome Back to Trench, part I; Rulings Log)*

Per the Rulings Log, Kaymi and Tsu'lan go together to bring Sänrri home, and no one else from the Nine. In the scene, Kaymi brings out the small clay pot of yellow she carried to the Mangkwan country and back, and she paints the lines back onto Sänrri's face, since the Mangkwan had wiped them off on the first day. Sänrri says she is going home to the Falls. *(scene: Welcome Back to Trench, part I; Rulings Log)*

Under the old tree at the Lake, Sänrri gives herself to Eywa, and her voice runs down the roots and up the rivers toward the Cave of Truth. She says the same thing three times, in Japanese, English and Na'vi: two riders of the great wings put something in her hands. The Second and Third Toruk Makto, Tunskxe (Simon, *Gurren Lagann*) and Yoko'fya (Madoka Kaname, *Madoka Magica*), taught her to resist forced tsaheylu and then master it from inside. The summary says she comes back with more power, and the pack does not say what that means in practice. *(scene: Welcome Back to Trench, part II; Rulings Log)*

Millie's theory, from *Fire and Ash*, is that tsaheylu is a consenting bond, and that the Mangkwan force it to dominate through pain. The log says the Na'vi lack the "ambition" to resist it, while avatars and Recoms have it. The Toruk Makto teach Sänrri to recover ambition and hope. The Astikasya never start a forced tsaheylu themselves, and Kiri is the exception. *(Rulings Log)*

In the Cave of Truth, 'Ipxerey hears Sänrri's voice from the roots. She asks two questions and then says yes. Six Spears gather at the pool: Atanvi, Tsa'rawm, Rewon, Txewan, Tsu'ong and Ketxe. Katirì sets a slow rhythm on her drum and then leaves them. A figure in a tsahìk's cloak comes out from beneath the Tree of Souls, and its voice is Sänrri's. In the sand it draws the Mangkwan camp, the ikran hill, the route to the Lake, and the sky people's floating fort at Bridgehead, joining the two places with a stick. *(scene: Welcome Back to Trench, part III)*

The figure warns that the people of the ash are coming, not this season but soon, and that they do not come to talk. This is the arc's "They are coming." Then it teaches the six to say their own names aloud and keep pushing against the forced bond. The scene puts the lesson this way: "La esperanza es lo que agarra. La voluntad es lo que empuja." Each Spear with a red line on the left palm must find ten more and teach them, and those ten find ten more. *(scene: Welcome Back to Trench, part III; Rulings Log)*

Partway through, the figure collapses, and Atanvi catches it before it hits the stone. The hood falls back, and the face is 'Ipxerey's. She says Eywa asked her, and she said yes. She tells them that Kaymi and Tsu'lan are on the far shore and will cross in the morning. Rewon says he saw her hands and did not want to say so. *(scene: Welcome Back to Trench, part III)*

At dawn Sänrri wakes under the roots, and Kaymi is still awake beside her with the Lance across her knees. They fly low over the water on Rahaylo, with Tsu'lan and the others riding behind in a line on ikran that the Lost Cohort and the Scattered Ashes took out of the Mangkwan country. Sänrri steps into the shallows on her side of the Lake and smiles toward the fog that hides the Falls. *(scene: Welcome Back to Trench, part IV)*

The Overcompensate note has Sänrri return to the Falls changed and tell the clan about Tunskxe and Yoko'fya. The clan takes it as a good omen, since Toruk Makto only rise in times of great sorrow, and two of them chose to speak to the clan's tsakarem. *(Era: Overcompensate)*

Sänrri teaches the lesson to the Nine first and then to the whole clan before the Siege, so they are ready for what is coming. Tìmeyo and Kerevi share one system, which makes their will stronger because they already live the inner fight every day. Kerevi knows the Mangkwan technique but would never use it on clanmates, because of Syayvi. *(Era: Clancy)*

The arc ends with the clan leaving to fight beside Jake Sully, weeks before the Siege. Those left behind are 'Ipxerey, Nika and Earth House, Tsu'lan, Syayvi and the Scattered Ashes, the elders and the children. *(Era: Clancy; Siege note: Before the Siege)*

## Who is in it

- Sänrri: captured by the Mangkwan, escapes through a path an unnamed insider opened, and speaks to the Cave of Truth from the roots of the Lake.
- Kaymi: goes with Tsu'lan to bring Sänrri home, paints her yellow, and flies her back on Rahaylo.
- Tsu'lan: goes with Kaymi to bring Sänrri home, and stays at the Falls when the clan leaves for Fire and Ash.
- Tunskxe (Simon): the Second Toruk Makto, who teaches Sänrri that the will inside her is hers.
- Yoko'fya (Madoka Kaname): the Third Toruk Makto, who teaches her that wanting good things is allowed.
- 'Ipxerey: the tsahìk, Atanvi's mother, who carries Sänrri's voice into the cave, collapses, and says she agreed.
- Atanvi: one of the six Spears in the cave, who catches 'Ipxerey and is the last to receive the red line.
- Tsa'rawm: one of the six Spears, who shouts his name and sits last.
- Rewon: one of the six Spears, who keeps his bow drawn and later admits he saw 'Ipxerey's hands.
- Txewan: one of the six Spears, who says her name twice.
- Tsu'ong: one of the six Spears, who studies the map.
- Ketxe: one of the six Spears, who recognizes the three-stripe marking and laughs.
- Katirì: the drummer who sets the rhythm and leaves the cave.
- Rängay, Pxayu, Apayì, Pamyaw and Txafwe: taken by the Mangkwan with Sänrri, and shown sleeping at the Lake.
- Tìmeyo: sleeps sitting up among the Scattered Ashes at the Lake.
- Roneyu: named only in the Rulings Log, as the Mangkwan-born helper who escaped unnoticed.
- Kerevi: named in the Clancy note as the one who knows the Mangkwan technique, and not in this arc's scene.
- Syayvi: left behind at Fire and Ash, and later the Ninth Spear.
