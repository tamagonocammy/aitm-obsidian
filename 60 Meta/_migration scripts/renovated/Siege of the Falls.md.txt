## Where it sits

The eighth arc. It takes place weeks after the events of *Avatar: Fire and Ash*, about six months after *Frontiers of Pandora* and its DLCs. In the eras it runs from [[Clancy — The Teaching, the Occupation and the Siege]] into the opening of [[Breach — The Final Battle and the Wild Hunt]].

It is the "Shibuya Incident" of the AU: everything goes wrong, and everything changes after it.

## Before the Siege

**The warning.** Sänrri, the Lost Cohort and Txafwe were held by the Mangkwan. Kaymi and Tsu'lan bring Sänrri home, and the message that comes back with the captives is "They are coming." See [[Prelude to Fire]].

**The teaching.** Sänrri teaches what Tunskxe and Yoko'fya taught her, how to resist the forced tsaheylu and then master it from inside, first to the Nine and then to the whole clan.

**Fire and Ash.** The clan leaves to fight beside Jake Sully. Those left behind include 'Ipxerey, Nika and Earth House, Tsu'lan, Syayvi and the Scattered Ashes, the elders, and the children.

## The Occupation

Days to weeks; the exact length is not settled. While the clan is away, the Falls are taken by Mangkwan warriors and by the ANAKT Fireteam, under Varang's regime working with the RDA.

The teenagers, led by Rawìl, Anake and Tulìpey (all fifteen), run a guerrilla resistance out of the hidden passages, like the Firelights in *Arcane*: stolen supplies, hit-and-run strikes, protecting the younger children. They use human weapons, which breaks the Third Law about metal. The same war paint symbols are worn, painted for survival and no longer for ceremony.

Syayvi, pregnant, fights beside Tsu'lan to protect the children.

## The return

The adults come home victorious and find their home stolen.

- Rawìl gives tactical reports like a commander.
- Anake tries to joke and his voice cracks.
- Notä screams "WHERE WERE YOU?!" and clings to Mìreya.
- Kxayì is furious and sobbing at once.
- Some of the youngest do not recognize their parents until later.

## The Siege

The fighting outside, over the Lake, lasts about two to four hours; the fights inside the Falls may run longer. The ANAKT Fireteam falls in an escalating gauntlet, each fight with its own place and its own rules, in the manner of *Destiny 2: Forsaken*. The reveal and destruction of the Fireteam belongs to "My Clematis" (RUBYEYE & C!naH).

**Syayvi** earns the Ashen Spear during the Siege and becomes the Ninth Spear, the Penitent. With her, the [[Nine Spears of the Mists]] are complete.

**Earth House.** Nika, Chuchu, Kobeni and Aliya bring human tactics and support. Chuchu's shotgun blast protecting Na'vi children, which happens outside the Falls: "THEY'RE CHILDREN YOU FUCKIN' BASTARD!" She taught Tsa'rawm human curse words, which he uses on Till, and taught Tsu'lan boxing, which he uses on Luka. Chuchu is the clan's "Trudy," and she survives.

**The Battle over the Lake.** "Paladin Strait." While the Nine fight inside the Falls, the rest of the clan meets the Mangkwan riders in the open air. The Mangkwan scatter, outmatched by the ikran-mounted crossbows and the Resistance. The Falls are unobtanium, and their magnetism shuts down RDA machines, so the RDA cannot fight there directly. Tsa'rawm's AMP suit chainsaw, stolen and made his own, still works inside, because it is built from ceramics and other non-magnetic materials.

## The final battle: the Nine against Luka

"City Walls." The first time all nine fight as nine, and AITM's "Nico and the Niners."

Luka opens: "So few, so proud, so... emotional. Hello, Dr. Takamatsu." He takes Sänrri with a forced tsaheylu. Kaymi and the other Spears notice she is not answering and go for her.

Inside the bond, Luka and Sänrri fight with their fists. He appears in three of her memories: her human life, the day the Astikasya found her, and the day she became Astikasya. By the end she is wholly Na'vi, and with the chorus the Nine strike Luka.

It is one fight in three stages: Tsu'lan's "I'm you" moment, which leaves him with facial scars; the full Nine striking together with the Scattered Ashes; and Kaymi and Sänrri at the center. The sacred pond runs red.

Tsu'lan lets Luka go. He escapes wounded: "There are many of us." Unlike the song, Sänrri is not corrupted. Luka dies later, at the end of [[The Wild Hunt]].

## Aftermath

- The clan rebuilds and argues over whether to keep the human weapons or return to the old ways.
- Children were made into soldiers, and the clan has to live with how exposed it was.
- Tìmeyo and Yawyay leave for the reef, back to the Ta'unui. Syayvi and Nì'syu stay, and Sìlpey is coming.
- Mizi wanders the mist, unable to join either side, guiding lost children home.
- "The Contract" is Sänrri's song after the Siege.

## Themes and parallels

- **Thousand-Year Blood War and the Shibuya Incident:** home invaded while its protectors are away, sacred spaces corrupted, children becoming soldiers, targets hunted one by one. The "Special War Powers" (Sänrri, Kaymi, Rawìl, Rewon, Nika) are a direct reference to *Bleach: TYBW*.
- ***Seven Samurai*:** a community defended against overwhelming force.
- ***Destiny 2: Forsaken*:** named bosses hunted in turn, with Luka as the Fanatic.
- ***Kill la Kill*:** retaking a home from the forces occupying it.
- Mountain people are built different.

"Everything is allowed in war," but at what cost to your soul? The Astikasya survive, and lose parts of their identity and innocence doing it.

## Ruled on 2026-11-12

- **Casualties** are heavy, but not among the named characters. Whether any main character dies is still undecided, because the founding idea of the AU was "what if the characters who die in canon actually lived."
- **The Occupation** lasts days to weeks.
- **The fighting** lasts about two to four hours over the Lake, maybe more inside the Falls.
- **Tsa'rawm's chainsaw** is ceramic and non-magnetic, so it works inside the Falls.
- **Ivan** is dead. The two Spears against him are Rewon and Tsu'ong. **Till** is dead.
- **Chuchu's moment** happens outside the Falls.
- **Notä's minigun** is gone. Rest in peace.
- **"Special War Powers"** is a *Bleach: TYBW* reference.
