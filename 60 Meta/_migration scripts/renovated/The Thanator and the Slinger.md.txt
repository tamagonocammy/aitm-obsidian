## Synopsis

Before the Falls, Tsu'ong was an arrogant warrior. He challenged Atanvi for Mìreya as if she were a prize, and Atanvi defeated him, which humiliated him and cracked the certainty his identity rested on *(Tsu'ong's profile)*. The Rulings Log fixes the order of events: that challenge came first, and only after it did Tsu'ong leave to explore the world *(Rulings Log)*.

Ketxe came from the Tawkami clan, where she turned botanical knowledge toward explosive compounds. Varang and the Mangkwan captured her and controlled her. She "disappeared" from Tsa'rawm, her first love, so that Varang could not reach him through her, and she later escaped into a self-imposed exile *(Ketxe's profile)*.

Out in the world, Tsu'ong met Ketxe. Each had survived becoming something they did not want to be, and they built a quiet bond on respect for the distance each had travelled *(Relationship Lore)*. The Rulings Log confirms that he met her while exploring, and that the two of them later came to the Falls together *(Rulings Log)*.

The author's summary has Tsu'ong and Ketxe coming to the Falls seeking uturu, with the young Korhe twins already mingling with the other children *(author's summary)*. Tsu'ong's profile describes him arriving with Ketxe and their children, Eantu and Tulìpey, with no arrogance at all, and the Falls took the family in as an adopted home and a sanctuary *(Tsu'ong's profile)*.

Old wounds come back into view. Tsu'ong treats Atanvi and Mìreya with a careful respect that began in shame, and the twins' rivalry with Notä carries the same charged tension that once ran between Tsu'ong and Atanvi *(Relationship Lore)*. Ketxe's history with Tsa'rawm stays unspoken but present, and her relationship with the Haykxa family, including Rewon and Sänume, remains careful *(Relationship Lore, Ketxe's profile)*.

The author's summary says these old wounds build to a boiling point. The pack does not describe that point, what sets it off, or who it involves *(author's summary)*.

The author's summary says the knowledge of Sänrri mediates between the old and the new. Tulìpey studies healing under Sänrri, who is the current tsakarem and is being prepared to become tsahìk, so Sänrri is already the teacher of the younger generation *(author's summary, Tulìpey's profile)*.

The Resistance gets a place near the Falls. Earth House is a Sky People resistance led by Nika "Silver Hand," and it is a strong ally of the Astikasya *(The Mark of Earth House)*. Before the Siege, its members wore a mark made of a blackened right hand for allegiance to Nika and cyan for Earth House as a whole *(The Mark of Earth House)*.

## Who is in it

- Tsu'ong: returns to the Falls with Ketxe to seek uturu, and his old challenge to Atanvi is one of the wounds that resurface.
- Ketxe: arrives with Tsu'ong and has to face the clan and the Haykxa family, along with her history with Tsa'rawm.
- Eantu: one of the twins, already mingling with the other children at the Falls, and in volatile rivalry with Notä.
- Tulìpey: the other twin, studying healing under Sänrri.
- Atanvi and Mìreya: the couple at the centre of the old wound with Tsu'ong; the pack does not say how the wound returns here.
- Tsa'rawm: Ketxe's first love, whose history with her is unspoken but present.
- Notä: volatile with Eantu, mirroring the old tension between Tsu'ong and Atanvi.
- Sänrri: the knowledge that mediates between old and new.
- Nika: leads Earth House, which gets a place near the Falls.
- Earth House: the Sky People resistance, which the Rulings Log says has ten members.
- Rewon and Sänume: of the Haykxa family, with a careful relationship with Ketxe.
