## Synopsis

The pack's picture of Pandora starts about six months before the Siege. Varang's corruption is spreading through the Mangkwan, and she has allied herself with Quaritch. The Mangkwan occupy Bridgehead, and Cpl. Miri Sugo defects, runs through the jungle, and gets past an ambush by calling out Nika's name before he is sent on to the Resistance. *(Era: Blurryface)*

In the same stretch of months, the Astikasya raid Mining Outpost Delta-7. Kobeni hides behind the air filters, hears Sänrri speaking Japanese, and defects. The ikran-mounted crossbow is being developed at this time. *(Era: Blurryface)*

Inside the Mangkwan, Syayvi and Tìmeyo shelter among them, watch the heresy unfold, and begin gathering the Scattered Ashes from within. *(Era: Blurryface)*

The RDA stays a standing threat. Its mining work pushes toward the Western Frontier, and the notes set the company against everything Na'vi life is built on. *(Hostile forces note)*

Sänrri is the center of the Lost Cohort, a circle that formed around her spiritual training under 'Ipxerey. Each member arrived with a different wound and a different reason to stay. Sänrri counts as part of the Cohort by ruling. *(Lost Cohort note; Rulings Log)*

The pack does not describe how the taking happens. It says only that Sänrri, the Lost Cohort and Txafwe are captured by the Mangkwan, and that this captivity is the Scaled and Icy era. *(Rulings Log)*

The captives are held for months. They are made to watch sacred rhythms bent into heresy, and Txafwe sees Varang working alongside Quaritch. *(Era: Scaled and Icy)*

Rängay is made to take part in corrupted ceremonies while her mate is elsewhere and her children wait at home. Her profile says the capture struck at the core of her soul, because she had to watch Eywa's Eye mocked. *(Rängay's profile)*

Pxayu, cut off from her daughter Lawarey, catches herself copying the Mangkwan ceremonies and is horrified at how well she adapts. Pamyaw loses her instruments, which her profile describes as Eywa's breath being stolen from her. *(Pxayu's profile; Pamyaw's profile)*

Apayì keeps the others' spirits up. Her small observations about the ash in the cell and about Rängay's voice work as lifelines, reminding each of them who they still are. *(Apayì's profile)*

Roneyu, Mangkwan-born and easy to overlook, helps Sänrri without being seen. The helper clears a path and then slips away that same night, so Sänrri never learns who it was. The Rulings Log links Roneyu to the Keons of AITM. *(Era: Scaled and Icy; Rulings Log)*

The children stay behind. Nìla and Mawpay wait at home with their mother Rängay, and Lawarey waits at the Falls. Apayì's knowledge that Lawarey is waiting gives her fierce determination. The pack does not say how any of them are cared for while their parents are held. *(Rängay's profile; Apayì's profile)*

What Sänrri learns from the two Toruk Makto, Tunskxe and Yoko'fya, belongs to the next arc, and her return home belongs to the arc after that. *(Rulings Log)*
