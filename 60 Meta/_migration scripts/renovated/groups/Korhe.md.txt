The Korhe are the smallest family: Tsu'ong, Ketxe and their twins Eantu and Tulìpey. Unlike the Samaya, Haykxa and Ngotxì, they are not listed among the families that settled Sheltering Falls *(Prologue era note)*. In the arc The Thanator and the Slinger, Tsu'ong and Ketxe come to the Falls seeking uturu with the twins already mingling with the other children *(The Thanator and the Slinger)*. Both parents are Nine Spears.
### Who they are
- [[Tsu'ong]]: The Wise of the Nine Spears; mate of Ketxe, father of Eantu and Tulìpey; once challenged Atanvi for Mìreya and was humiliated; brought Tipani combat techniques.
- [[Ketxe]]: The Unbound of the Nine Spears; mate of Tsu'ong, mother of Eantu and Tulìpey; born to the Tawkami clan, a maker of explosive botanical compounds; first love of [[Tsa'rawm]].
- [[Eantu]]: 15, proud adventurer; twin of Tulìpey; respects and competes with [[Rawìl]] and [[Anake]]; volatile with [[Notä]].
- [[Tulìpey]]: 15, strategist and healer apprentice under Sänrri; twin of Eantu; courted by Rawìl.
### What defines them
Tsu'ong and Ketxe are "two people who found redemption in each other": he was arrogant and had to be humiliated, she was captured and weaponized and had to escape *(Relationship Lore)*. Ketxe was controlled by Varang and the Mangkwan and "disappeared" from Tsa'rawm to protect him *(Ketxe's profile)*. The old tension between Tsu'ong and Atanvi shows again in the twins' rivalry with Notä *(Relationship Lore)*. The Falls became the family's "adopted home" and sanctuary *(Tsu'ong's profile)*. Eantu and Tulìpey are original characters by @lindholm_lizzie *(Rulings Log)*.
