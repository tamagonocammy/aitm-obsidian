## Synopsis

Sänrri, the Lost Cohort and Txafwe are captured by the Mangkwan and held for months. The author places this captivity in the "Scaled and Icy" era, which replaces the older idea that Sänrri led the Cohort's rescue. The era's surface looks bright, but underneath it is the idea that Clancy is dead. *(Era: Scaled and Icy; Rulings Log)*

While hiding, Sänrri finds a place where she can make tsaheylu with Eywa. The pack does not name that place. There she meets the Second and Third Toruk Makto, Tunskxe (Simon from Gurren Lagann) and Yoko'fya (Madoka Kaname from Madoka Magica). The author casts them as Sänrri's Neds of Voldsøy. *(Era: Scaled and Icy; Rulings Log)*

The author's theory of tsaheylu treats it as a consenting bond. The Mangkwan force it onto others to dominate them through pain. Na'vi lack the "ambition" to resist a forced bond, while avatars and Recoms have it. This theory comes from the author's notes on Fire and Ash. *(Rulings Log)*

Tunskxe and Yoko'fya teach Sänrri to resist a forced bond first, and then to master it from inside. The route runs through recovering "ambition" and "hope." The Astikasya never start a forced tsaheylu themselves, and Kiri is the one exception, since she holds "sudo" over the whole Eywa network. *(Rulings Log; Era: Scaled and Icy)*

The escape works on two fronts. Apayì poses as a Mangkwan and passes a message to Tsu'lan, while the Ashes work from inside the Mangkwan. Kaymi (Eri) and Tsu'lan (Yuji) go together to bring Sänrri home, and the pack says it is those two alone, not the full Nine. This is where Tsu'lan's personal bond with Sänrri begins. *(Era: Scaled and Icy; Rulings Log)*

Roneyu is Mangkwan-born and goes unnoticed by the Mangkwan. They help Sänrri without being seen. The pack says they clear a path, perhaps through something that never looks like a person. They slip away that same night, and to the Mangkwan the Roneyu who vanished simply stopped existing. Sänrri never learns who helped her. The author compares this to Clancy never knowing that Keons saved him in "Saturday," and Roneyu is AITM's Keons. *(Era: Scaled and Icy)*

Roneyu's profile fills in the background. They were raised in Varang's corrupted clan and taught to be useful rather than taught how to be a person. Their escape from the Mangkwan was quiet, and nobody tracked them when they left. Later the Flying Troupe found them on the road, and the profile does not date that escape against Sänrri's captivity. *(Roneyu's profile)*

Sänrri comes home with the Cohort and the Ashes, carrying the warning "They are coming." The Rulings Log ties her return to the Falls to Overcompensate. She tells the clan about the two Toruk Makto, and they take it as a good omen. She teaches the lesson to the Nine first, then to the whole clan, before the Siege. *(Era: Scaled and Icy; Rulings Log)*

## Who is in it

- Sänrri: the arc's centre, captured, hidden, taught to resist and then master forced tsaheylu, and back at the Falls with the warning.
- Lost Cohort: captured with Sänrri and comes home with her.
- Txafwe (Umiri): captured with Sänrri, and sees Varang working with Quaritch.
- Varang and Quaritch: the pair Txafwe sees working together.
- Mangkwan: hold the captives and force tsaheylu to dominate through pain.
- Tunskxe (Simon): the Second Toruk Makto, who teaches resistance.
- Yoko'fya (Madoka Kaname): the Third Toruk Makto, who teaches hope.
- Roneyu (they/them; Crona): Mangkwan-born, helps Sänrri unseen, then slips away.
- Kaymi (Eri): goes with Tsu'lan to bring Sänrri out.
- Tsu'lan (Yuji): goes with Kaymi; this arc starts his bond with Sänrri.
- Apayì: poses as a Mangkwan and carries a message to Tsu'lan.
- The Ashes: work from inside the Mangkwan.
- Kiri: the one exception to the rule against forced tsaheylu.
- The Nine: learn the lesson from Sänrri after she returns.
- Syayvi: appears only in the open question about her and Roneyu.
