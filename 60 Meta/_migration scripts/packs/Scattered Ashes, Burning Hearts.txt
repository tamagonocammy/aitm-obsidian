- Sänrri, the Lost Cohort and Txafwe are captured by the Mangkwan. That captivity is the "Scaled and Icy" era. This replaces the older idea that Sänrri led the Cohort's rescue.

## Profile

**Personality:** Syayvi, once named Tswa’avä as a mark of exile, is a charismatic yet deeply conflicted individual who leads The Scattered Ashes with a mix of strength and hidden pain. Originally part of the Astikasya clan alongside her twin sister Tìmeyo, her life changed forever when they were exiled for killing their father after his neglect led to their mother Luey’s death. Branded with a new name and forbidden from connecting with Eywa, she and her sister found temporary refuge with the Mangkwan clan, only to discover corruption and heresy towards Eywa that drove them to forge their own path under the rule of the Mangkwan’s tsahìk: Varang. Due to this, using her natural charisma and emotional intelligence helped gather others to their cause, including her devoted mate Nì'syu, and sister Tìmeyo, two other Na'vi from the Tayrangi and Ta’unui clans, Txafwe and Yawyay, and the Astikasya’s **Lost Cohort**, a group of women who were Sänrri’s closest companions during her spiritual training, they formed The Scattered Ashes (Na'vi: **Veykawniraä Txeva**) as a group of escapees from the Ash People. Now returning to the Astikasya clan years later during the Second Pandoran War, she carries herself with cold dignity that masks deeper wounds – guilt over past choices, unresolved feelings about her exile, and complex emotions about seeing Sänrri in the role of tsakarem. During the Siege of the Falls, when the ANAKT Fireteam occupied Sheltering Falls while the adults were away assisting Jake Sully and his family against the RDA and Varang herself, Syayvi proved her commitment to her adopted home beyond question. Pregnant with Nì'syu’s child, she fought with desperate ferocity alongside Tsu'lan against overwhelming odds, protecting the clan’s children and defending the sacred spaces she had once been forbidden to enter. Her coordination with the wandering warrior became legendary, two exiles fighting side by side, their combined strength a testament to redemption earned through action rather than words. When the other Spears finally returned, Syayvi stood among them not as a petitioner seeking acceptance, but as a warrior who had already bled for the clan. Her valor during those brutal hours earned her **the Ashen Spear**, carved from wood burned during the Siege itself, and recognition as the Ninth Spear, **the Penitent** who chose to protect rather than destroy. In the final confrontation against Recom Luka in the Cave of Truths, Syayvi fought alongside the other Eight Spears and her Scattered Ashes in perfect synchronization, her redemption arc reaching its culmination as she struck in unison with those who had once cast her out. The sacred pond ran red with the blood of the Strangler Fig, and Syayvi’s spear, forged from her own past failures, became an instrument of justice. Her participation in that coordinated strike, fighting to protect the very clan that had exiled her, represented the complete transformation from Tswa’avä the outcast to Syayvi, the protector. Despite often casting herself as the antagonist, Syayvi’s true strength lies in her ability to create belonging for others who feel lost, even as she struggles with her own place in the world. Her journey of redemption brings her full circle to confront not just the clan that exiled her, but the consequences of all her choices since then. Now carrying Nì'syu’s child, she faces the additional weight of bringing new life into a world she’s still trying to find her place in, her impending motherhood adding both desperate hope and paralyzing terror to her already complex emotional landscape. Beneath her composed exterior lies a profound love for those she left behind, though she buries these emotions under layers of self-recrimination. Her leadership of The Scattered Ashes represents both her greatest achievement and her deepest struggle – creating a new family while still yearning for the one she lost, and now preparing to nurture new life while haunted by the destructive patterns of her own upbringing. The Ashen Spear she now wields serves as a constant reminder that redemption is not granted: it is earned, again and again, through the choice to protect rather than destroy.

**Personality:** Tìmeyo is quiet and withdrawn, carrying herself with a stoic exterior that masks profound emotional fragility. She struggles to express herself properly, her few carefully chosen words often landing wrong—perceived as blunt or insensitive despite genuine care. When stress becomes overwhelming, she doesn't break down but simply *stops*, dissociating so completely she becomes functionally nonverbal, lost somewhere deep inside where the world cannot reach her. This is when **Kerevi** emerges: a fully distinct alter within their DID system, born during the killing of their father when that trauma became too immense for one consciousness to bear alone. Where Tìmeyo is paralyzed by emotion, Kerevi is present and confident. But she's also deeply *childish* not in body but in spirit. She speaks in a patchwork of borrowed phrases collected from everyone Tìmeyo has known: Syayvi's commanding declarations, Kaymi's protective instincts, Eyfuwen's storytelling cadences, even Varang's twisted rhetoric and Quaritch's military efficiency. She mimics unconsciously, cobbling together a personality from fragments because she was born with no template for how to be. "Why are we walking?" she'll ask when a destination is fifty paces away. Her problem-solving is dangerously simplistic: "If the RDA wants this territory, we kill all of them. Problem solved." ## Profile

**Personality:** What most know as Nì'syu conceals a profound secret - she is not the Na'vi she claims to be. Born as **'Awll,** she was forced to assume the identity of her twin sister after a tragic event claimed the original Nì'syu's life. Compelled by her family to maintain this deception due to the powerful alliance it preserved with Syayvi's lineage, she has lived a fractured existence, presenting a serene, kind exterior while harboring a hollow emptiness within. Her seemingly natural empathy masks years of careful observation and mimicry of her sister's mannerisms. Her obsession with Syayvi transcends mere devotion - the moment they joined through tsaheylu during their mating ritual became her first experience of true connection, as Syayvi instinctively understood her hidden truth without words. This profound revelation explains her fiercely protective nature toward her mate and the child Syayvi now carries - they represent the only authentic relationship in a life built on deception. Her time with the Mangkwan clan under Varang's corrupted regime added another layer of horror to her fractured identity - forced to participate in heretical rituals that perverted sacred Na'vi traditions while maintaining her false persona, she watched as everything she held sacred was twisted into mockery. The collaboration with Quaritch's forces felt like the final betrayal of her true self, as she was compelled to aid the very humans who represented everything antithetical to Na'vi existence. Her fascination with celestial observation stems from childhood attempts to escape her reality, finding in the constancy of the stars the only reliable truth in her uncertain existence. The fear that flashes in her eyes when accidentally addressed by her true name reveals the depth of her vulnerability despite her carefully maintained composure. Her connection to starlight and practice of celestial divination serve dual purposes - both genuine spiritual practice and perfect cover for her frequent need to isolate herself when the weight of her false identity becomes overwhelming. Now with Syayvi carrying their son **Sìlpey**, she faces both hope and terror - hope that in creating new life with her mate she might finally claim authentic existence, and terror that her child might somehow become entangled in the web of deception surrounding her true identity. Those closest to her note how she shifts between profound presence and disturbing absence, her amethyst eyes sometimes reflecting a depth of loneliness that even Syayvi cannot fully reach. This duality explains why even members of the Scattered Ashes find her increasingly unsettling to truly know - beneath her gentle smile lies not just fierce will, but a fundamental identity fracture that makes her both desperately loyal and potentially unpredictable. During the Siege, while facing the ANAKT Fireteam’s and the Mangkwan’s occupation, Nì'syu fought with a desperate fury that stripped away years of careful pretense, in the chaos of protecting her mate and their unborn son, she moved as 'Awll, her true self, no longer constrained by the false identity that had defined her existence. The life-or-death stakes revealed what tsaheylu had already shown Syayvi: that her authentic devotion transcended any deception, her fierce protectiveness more real than any carefully maintained façade. Fighting in perfect coordination with Syayvi and the other Ashes during the final battle against Luka, she experienced something profound, in combat, protecting those she loved, her true name didn’t matter because her actions spoke louder than any identity ever could. The Siege became the moment when 'Awll and Nì'syu ceased to be separate masks and became simply herself—a warrior, a mate, a soon-to-be mother fighting for the future rather than hiding from the past.

===== 20 Groups/The Scattered Ashes.md =====
> [!info] Synopsis written 2026-11-12 from the notes in this vault. Nothing in it is new canon; gaps are listed under Still open.

The Scattered Ashes are the exiles Syayvi gathered from inside the Mangkwan, after she and her twin Tìmeyo took shelter there and saw the heresy *(Blurryface era)*. The Relationship Lore calls them "an exile's found family", built around shared exclusion. They came home with the Lost Cohort and the warning "They are coming", proved themselves in the Siege, and then began to separate. Syayvi and Nì'syu stay at the Falls with Sìlpey on the way, and Tìmeyo and Yawyay leave for the Ta'unui reef *(Siege of the Falls)*.
### Who they are
- [[Syayvi]]: leader; The Penitent, Ninth of the Nine Spears with the Ashen Spear; twin of Tìmeyo, formerly named Tswa'avä in exile; mate of Nì'syu, pregnant with Sìlpey.
- [[Tìmeyo]]: twin of Syayvi; she and Kerevi are a DID system who share one note. Once destined to be tsakarem and Kaymi's mate. Leaves with Yawyay for the reef after the Siege.
- [[Nì'syu]]: also 'Awll, who took Nì'syu's name herself and knows who she is; mate of Syayvi.
- [[Txafwe]]: Tayrangi rhythm-keeper from the Eastern Sea; mate of [[Rängay]], father of Nìla and Mawpay; captured with the Cohort.
- [[Yawyay]]: from the Ta'unui coastal clan; a drummer who sought the Mangkwan willingly and lost faith in them; Tìmeyo's mate after the Siege.
### What defines them
Their shared ground is exclusion: the twins' exile for killing their father, the Mangkwan's corruption, and the slow work of being taken back in *(Relationship Lore)*. Kaymi watched them closely from their arrival, especially Syayvi *(Kaymi's profile)*. Kerevi knows the Mangkwan technique and would never use it on the clan, because of Syayvi *(Rulings Log)*. Their loyalty to each other held through the Siege *(Relationship Lore)*. The Ashes "proved themselves during the siege despite being exiles" *(Siege of the Falls)*.

===== RELATIONSHIP LORE (sections) =====
### Syayvi & Nì'syu/'Awll
An alliance that became love that became the most honest thing in 'Awll's fractured life. Nì'syu/'Awll was living a lie — born 'Awll, forced into her dead twin sister's identity to preserve a political alliance. She had been observed and performed and mirrored for so long that she had almost forgotten what it felt like to be seen. Syayvi understood her truth through tsaheylu without being told — a moment of connection so profound it became 'Awll's anchor. Their unborn son Sìlpey is both terror and hope: terror because deception has surrounded his conception, hope because new life is the most honest thing either of them will ever make.

### Tìmeyo's old bond with Kaymi
Tìmeyo was once destined to be tsakarem and to mate with Kaymi. Exile severed both callings. Tìmeyo carries this loss in ways she rarely speaks about. Kerevi occasionally references it with childlike directness and then has to watch Tìmeyo move through the complicated grief of hearing the truth out loud.

### 'Awll's secret: she is not Nì'syu
Born 'Awll, forced to assume her dead twin sister's identity to preserve a political alliance. Syayvi knows the truth through tsaheylu. No one else does. Post-Siege, 'Awll and Nì'syu have almost merged — she moves through the world as herself now, neither name quite capturing it — but the secret still exists structurally and will eventually need to be resolved.

> [!note] Ruled 2026-11-12
> 'Awll took Nì'syu's name herself. She was not forced into it, and she knows who she is.

### The Scattered Ashes
Syayvi, Tìmeyo/Kerevi, Nì'syu/'Awll, Txafwe, Yawyay. An exile's found family, built around shared exclusion and the need to forge something real out of displacement. Their loyalty to each other survived the Mangkwan corruption, the Siege, and the slow process of being slowly integrated back into the Astikasya's world. Post-Siege, Yawyay has formalized her new bond with Tìmeyo and is making plans to search for her lost clan. This is the beginning of the Ashes fragmenting in loving directions.

> [!note] Ruled 2026-11-12
> Yawyay is not searching for a lost clan. The Ta'unui are her home, and she leaves for her reef with Tìmeyo at the end of the Siege.
