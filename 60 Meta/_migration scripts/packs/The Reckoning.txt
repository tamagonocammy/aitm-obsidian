## The story's spine (November 2026)

- Sänrri, the Lost Cohort and Txafwe are captured by the Mangkwan. That captivity is the "Scaled and Icy" era. This replaces the older idea that Sänrri led the Cohort's rescue.
- In hiding, Sänrri makes tsaheylu in a place where she can reach Eywa and meets the Second and Third Toruk Makto: Tunskxe (Simon, *Gurren Lagann*) and Yoko'fya (Madoka Kaname, *Madoka Magica*).
- They teach her to resist the Mangkwan's forced tsaheylu and then master it from inside, by recovering "ambition" and "hope." The Astikasya never start a forced tsaheylu themselves. Kiri is the exception to the rule.
- Millie's theory, from *Fire and Ash*: tsaheylu is a consenting bond; the Mangkwan force it to dominate through pain; Na'vi lack the "ambition" to resist it, while avatars and Recoms have it.
- Someone inside the Mangkwan helped Sänrri, and she never learned who. It was Roneyu, born Mangkwan, who escaped unnoticed. Roneyu is AITM's "Keons."
- Kaymi and Tsu'lan go together to bring Sänrri home. It is those two, not the full Nine.
- Her return to the Falls is AITM's "Overcompensate." She tells the clan about the two Toruk Makto and they take it as a good omen. She teaches the lesson to the Nine, then to the whole clan, before the Siege.
- Tìmeyo and Kerevi: their system makes their will stronger. Kerevi knows the Mangkwan technique and would never use it on the clan, because of Syayvi.
- The Siege: see [[Siege of the Falls]], which carries every Siege ruling.
- Luka survives the Siege and dies at the end of [[The Wild Hunt]]. The version where Luka is re-forged at Bridgehead to impersonate Tsu'lan is discarded.
- Chuchu is the clan's "Trudy" and she survives. The Falls are unobtanium; their magnetism shuts down RDA machines and Earth House technology inside.
- Tamtey Kataru'ite, the protagonist of *Frontiers of Pandora*, is olo'eykte of the Sarentu. News of the Nine Spears spreads through the Sarentu.
- Tsu'lan is the physically strongest of the Nine. Tsu'ong, Kaymi or Tsa'rawm could give him a fight.
- The clan's color is yellow. See [[Astikasya Yellow]].
- The southern water is an inland lake, called "the Lake." Sänrri keeps a private name for it. See [[The Lake]].

===== ERA: Scaled and Icy =====
*The bright surface hiding "Clancy is dead."*

Sänrri, the Lost Cohort and Txafwe (Umiri) are captured by the Mangkwan and held for months. They watch sacred rhythms twisted into heresy. Txafwe sees Varang working with Quaritch.

**Roneyu, the Keons of AITM.** Roneyu (Crona), Mangkwan-born and invisible, helps Sänrri unseen. They clear a path, perhaps through something that never looks like a person. Then they slip away that same night, unnoticed as always. To the Mangkwan, the Roneyu who vanished simply stopped existing. **Sänrri never learns who helped her**, just as Clancy never knew Keons saved him in "Saturday" by seizing Trash.

**The Toruk Makto.** Hiding and desperate, Sänrri reaches a place where she can make tsaheylu with Eywa. There she meets the **Second and Third Toruk Makto, Tunskxe (Simon) and Yoko'fya (Madoka)**. They are her Neds on her Voldsøy.

**The lesson.** Tsaheylu is a consented bond. The Mangkwan force it to dominate through pain, and pure Na'vi, who never needed to defend themselves inside the network, have no resistance to it. Tunskxe teaches will and Yoko'fya teaches hope. Together they teach Sänrri to **resist a forced bond and then overpower it from the inside**, the way Quaritch, paralyzed, held on through will alone. The Astikasya never initiate a forced bond themselves. Kiri remains the one true exception: she holds "sudo" over the whole Eywa network.

**The escape.** Apayì poses as Mangkwan and gets a message to Tsu'lan, while the Ashes work from inside. **Kaymi (Eri) and Tsu'lan (Yuji) go together** to bring Sänrri out. This is the Torchbearer with one Bandito at her side, and the start of Tsu'lan's personal bond with Sänrri.

The Ashes and the Cohort come home with Sänrri, carrying the warning: **"They are coming."**
===== 20 Groups/The Lost Cohort.md =====
> [!info] Synopsis written 2026-11-12 from the notes in this vault. Nothing in it is new canon; gaps are listed under Still open.

The Lost Cohort is the circle that formed around Sänrri while she trained as tsakarem under 'Ipxerey, after she arrived on Pandora as the dreamwalker Dr. Tomori Takamatsu *(The Dreamwalker Years)*. Each member came with a different wound and a different reason. The Mangkwan captured Sänrri, the Cohort and Txafwe and held them for months *(Rulings Log)*. They came home with Sänrri carrying the warning "They are coming" *(Scaled and Icy)*.
### Who they are
- [[Sänrri]]: the circle's center; tsakarem; the Relationship Lore counts her as a member, but her own note does not carry the group tag.
- [[Rängay]]: "Spiritual Musician"; stern ritualist; mate of [[Txafwe]], mother of [[Nìla]] and [[Mawpay]]; once at odds with Kaymi over Sänrri.
- [[Pxayu]]: "Nurturer"; mate and co-parent with Apayì, mother of [[Lawarey]]; afraid of being abandoned.
- [[Apayì]]: "Social Perceptive"; co-parent of Lawarey with Pxayu; posed as Mangkwan to get a message to [[Tsu'lan]].
- [[Pamyaw]]: "Wandering Spirit"; no mate or family given; joined because Sänrri "made things interesting".
### What defines them
They were formed around Sänrri's spiritual training under 'Ipxerey, and each member's profile calls them the "Original Sänrri Cohort" *(Relationship Lore, member profiles)*. They survived the captivity as a unit and, after the Siege, their bonds were "tested and deepened" *(Relationship Lore)*. In captivity they watched sacred rhythms twisted into heresy *(Scaled and Icy)*. Pxayu caught herself mimicking Mangkwan ceremonies, Pamyaw lost her instruments, and Apayì kept spirits up *(member profiles)*. Pamyaw's way of belonging is to come and go and trust she will be understood *(Relationship Lore)*.

### The Lost Cohort
Sänrri, Rängay, Pxayu, Apayì, Pamyaw. Formed around Sänrri's spiritual training under 'Ipxerey. Each arrived with a different wound and a different reason. They held each other through the Mangkwan captivity. They survived as a unit. Post-Siege, the Cohort's bonds are tested and deepened — trauma that would have scattered a group of strangers has instead braided them tighter. Pamyaw's version of belonging is appearing and disappearing as she pleases and being certain she will be understood when she returns.
