[R  NEEDS MILLIE] coverage=0.76 | sources: _backup_2026-11-01/Avatar_ Into the Mists - Master List_cleaned.md
    Personality: Energetic and optimistic, a warrior to the core, always ready to help and protect his own. Tsa'rawm is an expert direhorse rider, demonstrating great skill and dexterity in races and handling these majestic animals. His connection with direhorses is almost mystical, able to communicate with them in a way that amazes even the most experienced Na'vi. His bravery in battle is matched only by his compassion outside of it, always willing to help any clan member in need. Crass and loud in his way of being and talking, Tsa'rawm is the most boisterous and loud of the bond siblings alongside Atanvi and Txewan, him being the reason that they have become more outgoing and extroverted. He was adopted as a brother by Kayan, taking his surname and becoming brothers in all but blood, this being repeated also with his sisters Lamaytxa and Sänume. Despite his rough exterior and tendency toward blunt honesty that sometimes creates unnecessary conflicts, he has a good sense of empathy and is willing to save those in danger as much as he can, showing his kind-hearted nature beneath his shortcomings. His personality comes off as rude and harsh in an almost childish way, but this masks a deeper wisdom born from hardship. As someone who lived in severe poverty before joining the clan, he doesn't take anything for granted and approaches life with infectious enthusiasm. His adventurous spirit means he's always willing to try new experiences and isn't afraid of the unknown, often being the first to volunteer for dangerous missions. Though he can be impatient and reckless, he's oddly patient when there's no immediate action needed, content to relax and enjoy peaceful moments with his family. His loyalty to his chosen siblings is absolute, and while he loves to tease them mercilessly, he would give his life to protect any of them without hesitation. As a gift for his many merits in service for the clan, he now stands as the Thunderous among the Nine Spears, his Scarred Spear bearing witness to countless battles fought to protect his family and clan, while also wielding a fearsome chainsaw he found in the fight against the ANAKT Fireteam and their Mangkwan raiding army. He lost his left eye in battle, a wound he wears with complete and slightly unnerving nonchalance — he wears a carved bone eye patch inlaid with bioluminescent clan markings, crafted by Zìsaw and Txolaw together, its patterns integrated seamlessly into the scar tissue around the socket, which glows faintly at night. Kxayì thinks this is the coolest thing she has ever seen. Anake has told at least fifteen different stories about how it happened, none of them true, all of them with Tsa'rawm's enthusiastic blessing. Rewon was the only one who saw him in the days right after the injury. She has never spoken about what that was like. He knows she remembers. Neither of them needs to say anything about it.
