Avatar: Into the Mists --- Working Reference
============================================

Oct 23, 2026 · \@Millie

Premise
-------

AITM follows the Astikasya, a clan of former herbalists who fled their ancestral forests after a tragedy and rebuilt themselves in the Spires of the Clouded Forest, in the Western Frontier. They became hunters, artisans and defenders without giving up who they were.

Home is **Sheltering Falls** (the Falls), a woodshaped settlement hidden in cliffs and fog. Its most sacred site is the **Cave of Truth** (Tìngayä Slär), where waterfalls pour into bioluminescent pools over a submerged Tree of Souls. South of the Falls lies an inland lake, which the clan calls simply the Lake.

The clan is led by **Olo\'eyktan Eyfuwen** and **Tsahìk \'Ipxerey**. Two sayings carry its philosophy: *adaptation is not betrayal, but reverence in motion*, and *a gentle kingdom ringed in spears*. The tension between them drives the story: how far can a people change and arm itself before it stops being itself?

The Astikasya\'s best-known innovation is the **ikran-mounted crossbow**. Much of the clan\'s adaptive work traces to **Sänrri**, formerly Dr. Tomori Takamatsu, a human avatar driver who became fully Na\'vi and now serves as tsakarem.

Arc timeline
------------

The story runs from the Occupation through the Siege to the Wild Hunt, the first arc after the Siege. The Siege is set weeks after *Avatar: Fire and Ash* and about six months after *Frontiers of Pandora*.

1.  **The clan leaves.** The Astikasya go to fight beside Jake Sully in the Fire and Ash war, leaving the Western Frontier exposed.

2.  **The Occupation.** Mangkwan forces, Varang\'s regime and the ANAKT Fireteam take Sheltering Falls. Elders, the injured and young children live under Mangkwan rule for weeks. The Occupation is short; the Siege is the long era.

3.  **The teenage resistance.** Rawìl, Anake and Tulìpey (all 15) lead a guerrilla resistance from hidden passages, Firelights-style. They use human weapons and break the Third Law about metal; Notä fights with a minigun.

4.  **The warning.** The Scattered Ashes and the Lost Cohort escape Mangkwan captivity and return: \"They are coming.\"

5.  **The Siege of the Falls.** The clan returns home to find it held. The Nine Spears and their allies face ANAKT in escalating fights: gauntlet fights, a two-phase fight with Hyuna, and a three-stage final with Luka in the Cave of Truth. Five Recoms fall; Mizi defects; Luka escapes.

6.  **The reunion.** Parents find their children hardened: Rawìl reporting like a commander, Anake\'s jokes cracking, Notä screaming \"WHERE WERE YOU?!\" while clinging to Mìreya.

7.  **The Wild Hunt.** Tsu\'lan leaves alone to keep his vow. Yukeyo, six of the Flying Troupe and Tsyokanì join him. Nine riders run Luka down in the Northern Ravines, and he dies at the blue hour before dawn.

8.  **Coming home.** Tsyokanì returns to the Falls with the riders. \'Ipxerey meets him at the entrance: \"You do not travel alone.\"

The Wild Hunt uses a Kizumonogatari visual language: drained color except bioluminescence, action shown only before and after, hard cuts to stillness. Its anthem is \"Ghost Riders in the Sky.\"

Spiritual practices and themes
------------------------------

**Transition through the Eye of Eywa.** A sacred journey in which the body answers spiritual truth, then is maintained with sacred herbs and medicines. Known practitioners: Atanvi and Txewan (together), Tìfyaw (years earlier), Txolaw and Rängay.

**Consecration of the Nine Spears.** Txolaw forges each spear through fire, chant and calls to Eywa. The tsahìk blesses it at the Tree of Souls, and the bearer swears an oath before Eywa with the clan as witness. Planted in a circle, the spears form a ring around sacred spaces.

**The Cave of Truth.** Site of soul transfers, naming rites, healing rituals and ancestral gatherings. It is also where Luka made his last stand in the Siege.

**Core themes.**

-   **The cost of protecting gentleness.** The children kept the Falls alive by breaking the Third Law, and their parents came home to strangers.

-   **Secrets that hold weight.** Txewan\'s killing of Sua; \'Awll living as Nì\'syu; Tìmeyo\'s severed destiny with Kaymi; Kayan\'s private spear.

-   **Found family as the variable that cannot be solved.** Nine Spears broke the Siege and nine riders ended the Hunt. Luka could calculate everything except people who show up unasked.

-   **Twenty One Pilots overlay.** Sänrri is the Clancy figure, Kaymi the Torchbearer, the Nine Spears her Banditos, and Luka the Nico / Blurryface figure.

**Threads worth writing**

-   **Laying down the weapons.** A cleansing at the Cave of Truth where Notä must give up her woodshaped weapon parts, setting Mìreya\'s discipline against Notä\'s invention.

-   **Mizi and the Woven Harmony.** Mizi, blind and wandering, crosses paths with Txewan\'s circle. Canon says she never learns the truth from Txewan, so any discovery must come from somewhere else, or not at all.

-   **\"There are many of us.\"** Luka\'s warning outlives him. Who else came out of the Gardens?

-   **Sänrri\'s rescue.** Kaymi and the other eight Spears bring Sänrri home; captor and timing still open.

-   **Sänrri\'s transfer.** The consciousness transfer scene, parallel to Grace Augustine\'s, with \"The Line\" as its anchor.

-   **Lake customs.** Four directions offered (a children\'s first-water rite, a grief-release practice, a seasonal gathering, night-flight courting); none chosen yet.

-   **Yawyay and Tìmeyo at the coast.** A visit to the Ta\'unui is not in the files. It is an idea to consider, not canon.
