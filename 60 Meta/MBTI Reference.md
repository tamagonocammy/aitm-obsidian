**By Millie Caicedo**

A complete reference of MBTI personality types for the cast of *Avatar: Into the Mists*. Types for characters drawn from existing source canons (Witch from Mercury, Chainsaw Man, Bocchi the Rock!, MyGO!!!!!, Ave Mujica, Soul Eater, JJK, Gurren Lagann) are based on community votes from source fandom. Types for original characters and AU-specific adaptations are based on character profiles.

### Notes
- Tìmeyo and Kerevi are listed separately as they are a DID system with two distinct alters — Tìmeyo (INTP) and Kerevi (ENFP).
- Types for source canon characters reflect community votes from their respective fandoms and may differ slightly from how they present in the AU adaptation.
- The AU contains a notably high density of introverted feeling types (INFP, ISFP, INFJ, ISFJ), which reflects the AU's core thematic focus on trauma, healing, spiritual depth, and found family.
