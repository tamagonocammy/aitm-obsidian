# MyMuji Season Plan

How AITM handles *BanG Dream! MyGO!!!!! // Ave Mujica* while it airs. Agreed with Millie on 2026-11-15.

## The show

- Premieres **Friday 2027-01-08, 23:30 JST** on NTV's FRIDAY ANIME NIGHT block (09:30 Friday in Bogotá). Broadcast time may change.
- Expected about 13 episodes (both earlier shows had 13), so roughly January to early April 2027. Bandori seasons move fast.
- Only the [[The Lost Cohort]], the [[The Scattered Ashes]] and [[Narayra]] are really in play. Everyone else is safe.

## Rules

1. **The show inspires; it does not overrule.** AITM began as "what if the ones who die in canon had lived?" and diverges on purpose. An episode is a source of ideas, never an automatic retcon. The trailer is the model: it gave AITM Narayra and the Falls thread without breaking anything.
2. **Weird twists are ignored.** Plot turns like the Hatsune reveal and episode 11 of *Ave Mujica* are not followed automatically (Millie, 2026-11-15). The model case: AITM keeps 'Awll taking her twin's name, but drops the blood relation between Uika and Sakiko entirely. [[Syayvi]] and [[Nì'syu]] are mates from different families, with no kinship. Twists that read as pandering get the same treatment.
3. **A watch note per episode** goes to `_inbox/` as `YYYY-MM-DD MyMuji ep N.md`, in three parts: aired character facts (usually safe to map), story shapes to borrow (proposals), and collisions with AITM canon (Millie decides). Aired facts and fan speculation are kept apart.
4. **Cooling off.** A reaction waits at least one more episode in `_inbox/` before it can be ruled, unless Millie is sure.
5. **Mid-season rulings are provisional.** They go into the [[Rulings Log]] tagged *(provisional, MyMuji)*, so a later change of mind rolls back only those lines.
6. **Season-end review.** After the finale, every provisional line is confirmed or dropped once, and the tag comes off.

## Tripwires (added 2026-11-15)

Some twists are slipped in late and quietly, and one of them (the Hatsune reveal) would have forced a rethink of several AU pieces at once. These kinds of reveal are flagged red in every watch note and are **never proposed as canon**. AITM's default for all of them is to ignore.

- **New blood ties between characters AITM pairs or treats as unrelated** (secret parents, half-siblings, aunts, cousins).
- **Hidden-identity or "this person was someone else all along" reveals.**
- **Retcons of parents or family history** that would change why a character is who they are.
- **Pairing undercuts**: anything that recasts an AITM couple as abusive, coerced or incestuous.
- **Deaths or permanent injuries** of characters AITM keeps alive.
- **Age reveals** that would change who can be with whom.
