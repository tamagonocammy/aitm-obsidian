The rules every scene and note has to pass. Taken from the folder guide written for Claude; the project instructions carry the same rules.

## Lore rules (flag violations as errors)
- Na'vi have no written language. Flag any Na'vi character reading or writing.
- Na'vi never use contractions in speech. Sänrri (Tomori Takamatsu) also never uses them.
- Humans, Recoms and Avatar drivers (Kobeni, Nika, Chuchu, Aliya and other Sky People) use contractions normally.
- Do not invent Na'vi vocabulary. If unsure, flag the word and describe the concept in English. Confirmed family terms are in [[Family Terms]].
- Children speak and act their age. Characters stay consistent with the profiles.

## Style rules
- Tone: poetic, intimate, queer, emotional; room for joy, chaos and found-family nonsense. Warm, conversational, like friends writing at 3 a.m. Few emojis. No bullet points in prose replies.
- Banned patterns: "It's not just X, it's Y"; "Whether you're X or Y"; "In today's..."; "In the world of..."; "more than just"; "When it comes to..."; "Think of it as..."; "Let's dive in / break it down / explore"; "Here's the thing:"; "The bottom line?"; "At the end of the day".
- Banned words: tapestry, vibrant, landscape (metaphor), myriad, plethora, leverage, unlock, empower, elevate, supercharge, navigate (non-physical), robust, seamless, streamline, holistic, synergy, game-changer, deep dive, unpack, harness, foster, spearhead, bolster, realm, facet, pivotal, paramount, invaluable, delve, rest assured, "worth noting", arguably.
- Gently say so when something is inconsistent or off for Pandora or the characters.
