## The story's spine (November 2026)

- Sänrri, the Lost Cohort and Txafwe are captured by the Mangkwan. That captivity is the "Scaled and Icy" era. This replaces the older idea that Sänrri led the Cohort's rescue.
- In hiding, Sänrri makes tsaheylu in a place where she can reach Eywa and meets the Second and Third Toruk Makto: Tunskxe (Simon, *Gurren Lagann*) and Yoko'fya (Madoka Kaname, *Madoka Magica*).
- They teach her to resist the Mangkwan's forced tsaheylu and then master it from inside, by recovering "ambition" and "hope." The Astikasya never start a forced tsaheylu themselves. Kiri is the exception to the rule.
- Millie's theory, from *Fire and Ash*: tsaheylu is a consenting bond; the Mangkwan force it to dominate through pain; Na'vi lack the "ambition" to resist it, while avatars and Recoms have it.
- Someone inside the Mangkwan helped Sänrri, and she never learned who. It was Roneyu, born Mangkwan, who escaped unnoticed. Roneyu is AITM's "Keons."
- Kaymi and Tsu'lan go together to bring Sänrri home. It is those two, not the full Nine.
- Her return to the Falls is AITM's "Overcompensate." She tells the clan about the two Toruk Makto and they take it as a good omen. She teaches the lesson to the Nine, then to the whole clan, before the Siege.
- Tìmeyo and Kerevi: their system makes their will stronger. Kerevi knows the Mangkwan technique and would never use it on the clan, because of Syayvi.
- The Siege: see [[Siege of the Falls]], which carries every Siege ruling.
- Luka survives the Siege and dies at the end of [[The Wild Hunt]]. The version where Luka is re-forged at Bridgehead to impersonate Tsu'lan is discarded.
- Chuchu is the clan's "Trudy" and she survives. The Falls are unobtanium; their magnetism shuts down RDA machines and Earth House technology inside.
- Tamtey Kataru'ite, the protagonist of *Frontiers of Pandora*, is olo'eykte of the Sarentu. News of the Nine Spears spreads through the Sarentu.
- Tsu'lan is the physically strongest of the Nine. Tsu'ong, Kaymi or Tsa'rawm could give him a fight.
- The clan's color is yellow. See [[Astikasya Yellow]].
- The southern water is an inland lake, called "the Lake." Sänrri keeps a private name for it. See [[The Lake]].

## Twenty One Pilots overlay

- Fixed parallels: Sänrri is Clancy; Kaymi is the Torchbearer; the RDA is Dema; the Astikasya are the Banditos; ANAKT are the Bishops; Luka is Nico (Blurryface); forced tsaheylu is seizing; the Mangkwan fire doctrine is Vialism; Tunskxe and Yoko'fya are the Neds of Voldsøy.
- The Nine Spears are Sänrri's Banditos: they protect her and she protects them.
- Songs: "Overcompensate" is Sänrri's, and its "we both" is Sänrri and Kaymi. "Paladin Strait" is the Battle over the Lake. "My Clematis" (RUBYEYE & C!naH) is the reveal and destruction of ANAKT. "City Walls" is the final battle. "The Contract" is Sänrri after the Siege. "The Line" is human Tomori and the consciousness transfer. "Drag Path" (original version) is tentatively the song of the whole AU.

## Ruled on 2026-11-11 and 2026-11-12, while building this vault

- Sänrri's ikran is Towa. Ketxe's ikran is Kxäng.
- Lì'may, Miri Sugo and the children Anake, Kxayì, Mìyo, Mìrì, Netezu, Lawarey, Nìla, Mawpay and Oarä are Millie's original characters. Rawìl, Notä, Tulìpey and Eantu are original characters by @lindholm_lizzie.
- Yueyla is an outsider like Tìfyaw and keeps the name te Mayfam.
- Tìmeyo and Kerevi are a DID system and share one note. 'Awll took Nì'syu's name and knows who she is. Txewì is a nickname for Txewan.
- When a character has two profiles, the long Master List version is the profile.
- The nine arcs, in order: [[A Torchlight]], [[The Great Games]], [[The Thanator and the Slinger]], [[Scattered Ashes, Burning Hearts]] (a flashback arc), [[The Reckoning]], [[Ambition and Hope — The Ones Who Know All]] (working title), [[Prelude to Fire]], [[Siege of the Falls]], [[The Wild Hunt]].
- [[Welcome Back to Trench]] is Overcompensate and belongs to Prelude to Fire. [[The Great Games Legacy]] belongs to The Great Games. [[Kava with the Nine]] and [[The Night the Warriors Lost Their Dignity]] are side stories.
- Ivan is dead; the two Spears against him were Rewon and Tsu'ong. Till is dead, killed by Tsa'rawm and Ketxe with the AMP suit chainsaw Tsa'rawm stole and made his own (ceramic, non-magnetic).
- Mizi: burned, blinded by shrapnel and fallen from a cliff, all canon. She lives, almost a ghost.
- Siege: casualties are heavy but not among named characters; whether a main character dies is undecided. The Occupation lasts days to weeks. The fighting lasts two to four hours over the Lake, maybe more inside. Chuchu's moment is outside the Falls.
- Wild Hunt: Sänrri's catatonic silence falls between the Siege and the Hunt. "The lost ANAKT fireteam" is a saying; the target is Luka. Roneyu rides, "to mend a life lost to ash."
- The Haykxa siblings are adopted, and so close it is basically blood.
- Luka never impersonates Tsu'lan; every line saying so is removed.
- Notä's minigun is gone. Miri Sugo being "ambushed by Nine Spears" is not canon.
- 'Awll took Nì'syu's name herself; she was not forced.
- Yawyay's home is the Ta'unui reef; she leaves for it with Tìmeyo at the end of the Siege.
- The Ashen Spear was a spear anyone could use, taken and reborn in the fires of the Siege.
- Sänrri is part of the Lost Cohort.
- Tsu'ong's challenge to Atanvi for Mìreya happened before he left to explore the world. Out there he met Ketxe; they come to the Falls together in arc 3.
- Haykxa adoptions: Kayan adopted Tsa'rawm and Lamaytxa; Sänume was adopted by Tsa'rawm. They all live together and are basically siblings.
- Luey, the twins' mother, dies.
- Earth House has ten members.
- Succession: when Kaymi becomes olo'eykte, Rawìl takes her role as eyktanay.
- Kaymi and Tsu'lan come for Sänrri and the rest; all the captives fly home with them.
- "They are coming" is delivered twice: once to 'Ipxerey through Sänrri's voice in the Cave of Truth, once to the whole clan.
- The will is the force itself, like a soul. Ambition and hope are how the will is projected.
- 2154: Tomori was an RDA Sci-Ops scientist in the Western Frontier with Nika during Jake's rebellion and the Battle of the Hallelujah Mountains. She escaped in the middle of it; her workplace exploded in an attack ("Heavydirtysoul").
- The Mangkwan kidnap Sänrri, the Cohort and Txafwe. The Reckoning's cameos are shots from *Avatar: Fire and Ash*, showing AITM and the films share one world.
- The Thanator and the Slinger are Tsu'ong and Ketxe. The arc is their arrival at the Falls and the drama of exes returning with a new family.
