## The story's spine (November 2026)

- Sänrri, the Lost Cohort and Txafwe are captured by the Mangkwan. That captivity is the "Scaled and Icy" era. This replaces the older idea that Sänrri led the Cohort's rescue.
- In hiding, Sänrri makes tsaheylu in a place where she can reach Eywa and meets the Second and Third Toruk Makto: Tunskxe (Simon, *Gurren Lagann*) and Yoko'fya (Madoka Kaname, *Madoka Magica*).
- They teach her to resist the Mangkwan's forced tsaheylu and then master it from inside, by recovering "ambition" and "hope." The Astikasya never start a forced tsaheylu themselves. Kiri is the exception to the rule.
- Millie's theory, from *Fire and Ash*: tsaheylu is a consenting bond; the Mangkwan force it to dominate through pain; Na'vi lack the "ambition" to resist it, while avatars and Recoms have it.
- Someone inside the Mangkwan helped Sänrri, and she never learned who. It was Roneyu, born Mangkwan, who escaped unnoticed. Roneyu is AITM's "Keons."
- Kaymi and Tsu'lan go together to bring Sänrri home. It is those two, not the full Nine.
- Her return to the Falls is AITM's "Overcompensate." She tells the clan about the two Toruk Makto and they take it as a good omen. She teaches the lesson to the Nine, then to the whole clan, before the Siege.
- Tìmeyo and Kerevi: their system makes their will stronger. Kerevi knows the Mangkwan technique and would never use it on the clan, because of Syayvi.
- The Siege: see [[Siege of the Falls]], which carries every Siege ruling.
- Luka survives the Siege and dies at the end of [[The Wild Hunt]]. The version where Luka is re-forged at Bridgehead to impersonate Tsu'lan is discarded.
- Chuchu is the clan's "Trudy" and she survives. The Falls are unobtanium; their magnetism shuts down RDA machines and Earth House technology inside.
- Tamtey Kataru'ite, the protagonist of *Frontiers of Pandora*, is olo'eykte of the Sarentu. News of the Nine Spears spreads through the Sarentu.
- Tsu'lan is the physically strongest of the Nine. Tsu'ong, Kaymi or Tsa'rawm could give him a fight.
- The clan's color is yellow. See [[Astikasya Yellow]].
- The southern water is an inland lake, called "the Lake." Sänrri keeps a private name for it. See [[The Lake]].

## Ruled on 2026-11-12: the ancient Toruk Makto

Full detail, with the proposals and open questions kept apart, is on [[Ambition and Hope — The Ones Who Know All]].

- Tunskxe, the Second Toruk Makto (Simon the Digger, *Tengen Toppa Gurren Lagann*), founded the Astikasya thousands of years before the story. The clan is that old.
- The present Samaya family descends directly from Tunskxe, through Eyfuwen. The descent is history and symbol. It carries no inherited power, and the Astikasya are not born superior to other Na'vi.
- Yoko'fya, the Third Toruk Makto, is Madoka Kaname's Na'vi counterpart (*Puella Magi Madoka Magica*).
- Both lived many thousands of years before Sänrri. They are never contemporaries of Jake Sully, Sänrri or the present clan.
- Tunskxe teaches ambition and Yoko'fya teaches hope. Will is the force; ambition and hope are how it is projected (unchanged).
- What Sänrri receives is old knowledge the Na'vi have largely forgotten, restored, and it is no new power. Living in balance with nature, they had little reason to keep a way of asserting their will against another mind inside the bond. In times of great sorrow that capacity matters again.
- Limits: the teaching takes effort and understanding. It gives no one dominion over Eywa, no limitless reality-changing power, and no answer to every conflict. The Astikasya still never start a forced tsaheylu. Kiri's exception stands.
- The ancient heroes do not return to fight their descendants' battles. They give the knowledge and the living do the rest. Nobody at the Siege shows Spiral Power or any inherited ability.
- These identities and the founding belong to AITM, not to the official *Avatar* canon.
- Not ruled, and not to be invented: founding dates, the generations in between, the circumstances of the founding, their looks as living Na'vi, and whether the two knew each other in life.

## Ruled on 2026-11-12: gender and pronouns

From Millie's brief for the gender and pronoun metadata. The working list of open questions is `60 Meta/_admin/gender-pronoun-review-20261112.md`.

- Rängay (Taki Shiina) is a trans woman. She transitioned through the Eye of Eywa.
- Txafwe (Umiri Yahata) is a trans man. He transitioned through the Eye of Eywa.
- Rängay and Txafwe are mates, and their relationship is ST4T.
- Character notes carry three separate properties: `gender`, `pronouns` (a list) and `transition`. They are independent of one another.
- A blank value means "not established." It never means cisgender. Gender is never inferred from pronouns, appearance, anatomy, the source franchise or a Na'vi name, and a source character's identity does not decide the AITM character's.
- Not every trans character transitioned through the Eye of Eywa; `transition` records only what the files or Millie say.
