# Avatar: Into the Mists — Character Profiles: Samaya & Haykxa Families

**By Millie Caicedo**

> *This file contains full character profiles for the Samaya and Haykxa families.*

## Samaya Family

### Atanvi (Suletta Samaya-Rembran)
**Ikran**: Payri
**Birthday**: April 9


**Personality:** A naturally shy but deeply determined Na'vi who often stutters when nervous but shows remarkable strength when protecting others. Despite her timidity in social situations, she's one of the clan's finest warriors \- though she'd blush to hear it said. She lives by the family motto "if you move forward you gain two, if you run you gain one," using it to push through her own doubts and help others face theirs. As the youngest daughter of the Olo'eyktan and Tsahìk, she carries both leadership and spiritual responsibilities, though she often doubts herself until Mìreya or her siblings remind her of her worth. Her connection to Eywa is profound but endearingly awkward \- she's been known to apologize to plants for startling them. She underwent her gender transition through the Eye of Eywa alongside Txewan, maintaining her new form through sacred herbs and medicines, an experience that deepened both their bond and their understanding of themselves. She leads the parent group with a mixture of stuttered instructions and unwavering determination, especially when the children need protecting. Most comfortable with her chosen family \- blood-oath siblings Tsa'rawm and Txewan, beloved mate Mìreya, and all their children \- she nonetheless pushes herself to help anyone in need, even if she has to stammer through it. Her skill in combat and ikran flying is remarkable, though she'd rather teach younglings about forest patterns than fight. Despite her anxieties, she's the first to stand against injustice, often surprising everyone (including herself) with her fierceness when her loved ones are threatened. This duality \- the shy teacher who can become a warrior in an instant \- makes her a unique and beloved member of the clan. As of now, and as an honor she has as the younger daughter of the olo’eyktan, she serves as **the Merciful** among the Nine Spears, carrying the **Tender Spear** as a reminder that compassion and combat can coexist.


### Mìreya (Miorine Samaya-Rembran)
**Ikran**: Tsawla
**Birthday**: May 7


**Personality:** Serious and professional with an unwavering determination to assert her independence, Mìreya maintains a carefully controlled exterior that masks a deeply tender and vulnerable core, channeling her brilliant strategic mind into her work as a talented tiretu and botanist who constantly seeks innovative ways to use Pandora's flora to benefit her family and clan. Her knowledge of plant medicine rivals that of the most experienced healers, a skill she developed partly to honor her late mother's botanical expertise, which she continues through maintaining extensive greenhouses throughout the settlement. Beneath her composed facade lies someone who struggled with trust and connection before finding her place among the Astikasya, her sharp intellect and natural leadership abilities making her an indispensable strategist alongside Rewon, though she tends to make quick decisions based on what she believes is best for others, sometimes without fully considering their feelings. This protective instinct extends especially to her wife Atanvi, whom she adores completely and views as her greatest source of strength and support, while in private revealing a remarkably affectionate and emotionally needy side that only her closest family witnesses. She has cultivated deep bonds with Syulang and Rewon, whom she considers sisters, maintains a particularly strong connection with her eldest son Rawìl, and despite being offered roles as tsakarem alongside Atanvi's potential eyktanay position, she respectfully declined, preferring to work behind the scenes while subtly positioning Rawìl for future leadership responsibilities. Her complicated relationship with her estranged father, whom she refuses to acknowledge, has left her fiercely independent but deeply committed to being the parent she never had, while her exceptional command of English, second only to Sänrri in the clan, and her quick wit make her both respected and beloved, though she's often lovingly teased by family members for her intensity and tendency to become surprisingly protective and clingy when truly comfortable.


### Rawìl (Ravell Samaya-Rembran)
**Ikran**: Telisi
**Birthday**: November 16


**Personality:** A quietly intense teenager who carries himself with an almost unsettling calmness, Rawìl has inherited his mother Mìreya's strategic mind and his aunt Kaymi's warrior instincts, creating a unique blend of calculated precision and raw talent. His dedication to becoming a respected warrior borders on single-minded, often training until exhaustion with his ikran Telisi or practicing combat forms alone under moonlight. Despite this intensity, he possesses an unexpected gentleness that emerges in his intricate weaving \- creating pieces so detailed they seem to capture living memories within their threads. The large scar on his back from protecting his cousins speaks to his defining trait: absolute, unquestioning loyalty to those he considers family. While others might see him as cold or detached, this emotional distance is selective \- he simply sees no point in wasting words or energy on those outside his inner circle. His friendship with Anake brings out a more competitive side, their bond forged through countless hunts where words became unnecessary between them. With his sister Notä, he shows a different face entirely \- the protective older brother who secretly enables her chaotic inventions while maintaining plausible deniability. His tactical mind constantly analyzes situations, seeing angles others miss, making him formidable despite his youth. Though being groomed for future leadership as potential eyktanay, Rawìl seems more comfortable in action than ceremony, his connection to Eywa expressed through perfect shots and silent hunts rather than elaborate rituals.


### Notä (Notrette Samaya-Rembran)
**Ikran**: Temek
**Birthday**: June 1


**Personality:** Notä is a clever and innovative thirteen-year-old who approaches life with equal parts strategic thinking and chaotic energy. Known for her smug demeanor, she masks deeper vulnerabilities with quick wit and daring hunting techniques that often give her older brother Rawìl grey hairs. While she deeply admires her brother and secretly seeks his approval, she expresses this through playful rebellion and pushing boundaries. Her closest relationship outside her immediate family is with her cousin Kxayì, their rivalry driving both to excel while strengthening their bond. After experiencing her first heartbreak and moving past her crush on Nì'syu, she channeled her energy into becoming an exceptional young hunter, developing unconventional methods that blend traditional skills with creative innovation, mostly through her inventions through woodshaping. Named after her grandmother (Mìreya's mother), whom she only ever met through visions at the Tree of Souls, Notä carries a deep curiosity about her legacy. This manifests in her determination to forge her own path while honoring her family's traditions. Her initial interest in The Scattered Ashes has evolved into a broader fascination with finding unique ways to serve her clan, even if her methods sometimes cause concern among the adults. This fascination also led her to seek apprenticeship under **Ketxe, the Unbound**, where she is currently learning the unconventional art of applied botanics, ranging from traditional healing salves to volatile, experimental compounds and even explosions, a pursuit that perfectly blends her scientific curiosity with her chaotic, innovative nature. Despite her outward confidence, she harbors private doubts and dreams, expressing these mainly through her innovative hunting approaches and close relationships with her family, growing into her own person while maintaining that characteristic smug smile.


### Kaymi (Ericht Samaya-Takamatsu)
**Ikran**: Rahaylo
**Birthday**: July 14


**Personality:** Brave and fierce warrior with unmatched vigilance over those she protects. As *eyktanay* (olo'eyktan-in-waiting), Kaymi balances clan leadership responsibilities with her protective nature, often shadowing her family from hidden vantage points throughout Sheltering Falls. Her bond with her ikran, Rahaylo, is legendary, enabling aerial maneuvers that make even experienced hunters marvel. With her sister Atanvi, she maintains a silent, supportive presence, respecting her transition while leaving small tokens of shared history. Her relationship with Sänrri represents a perfect balance \- the analytical former scientist complementing Kaymi's instinctive approach to Pandora's challenges. As an aunt, she trains Rawìl with stern perfectionism while secretly supporting Notä's inventive spirit, testing her creations on actual hunts. The adoption of Lì'may revealed her gentler side \- the fierce warrior who once tracked palulukan solo now crafts elaborate tales of "The Strongest Warrior on Pandora" for her daughter's ears alone. Since the Scattered Ashes' arrival, Kaymi established careful surveillance of the exiles, particularly Syayvi, whom she both respects and considers a potential threat. This vigilance has softened only with the news of Syayvi's pregnancy. As she prepares for eventual leadership, Kaymi has begun balancing her protective instincts with a broader vision for the clan's future, gradually transforming from individual protector to guardian of the Astikasya's unique balance of tradition and innovation.​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​ As *eyktanay*, she currently serves as the **Brazen** among the Nine Spears, she leads the elite warrior cadre that protects the Astikasya, wielding the **Crimson Spear** as both symbol and weapon of her commitment to defend their gentle kingdom.


### Sänrri (Tomori Samaya-Takamatsu)
**Ikran**: Towa
**Birthday**: November 22


**Personality:** In her life as Na’vi, Sänrri evolved from her former self as Dr. Tomori Takamatsu, retaining core traits while growing into new strengths. Once a quiet, introverted scientist who struggled with self-confidence and social interactions, she transformed after defecting from the RDA Sci-Ops to join the Na’vi, following the path of Jake Sully, the Sixth Toruk Makto. Since childhood, she had never quite felt “human” \- a disconnection that finally found resolution when she fully connected with Eywa. As a human, she found comfort in collecting specific items \- band-aids with animal designs, slippery objects, perfectly-sized things, and natural specimens. She would seek solace in planetariums and aquariums when anxious, a habit that translated into taking night flights with her protective ikran, Zìsya. Though her speaking voice was soft and often accompanied by a stutter, she possessed a remarkably powerful singing voice and expressed herself through poetry and musical compositions. Now as Sänrri, she has grown into a more confident and flirtatious personality while maintaining her gentle nature. Her autistic traits express themselves through her continued love of collecting, now focused on leaves, rocks, and natural specimens which she weaves into her clothes. Upon experiencing true communion with Eywa, she completely abandoned her scientific worldview, embracing pure spiritual faith and intuitive connection to Pandora’s living network in her role as tsakarem (tsahìk-in-waiting) under ‘Ipxerey’s guidance. Her powerful voice, once used for analytical poetry, now leads clan ceremonies with joyful songs celebrating Eywa and her chosen family. She is deeply protective of her family, especially her mate Kaymi and their adopted daughter Lì’may, channeling her former tendency to blame herself into a fierce dedication to those she loves.​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​


### 'Ipxerey (Elnora Samaya/Prospera Mercury)
**Ikran**: Txalmä
**Birthday**: November 1


**Personality:** Wise and experienced, always offering advice and guidance to those around her, 'Ipxerey embodies the profound transformation from a woman driven by loss and vengeance into a spiritual leader whose depths of understanding come from having walked through darkness and emerged into light. As the de facto spiritual leader of the clan alongside her mate and olo'eyktan Eyfuwen, she guides the clan in connecting deeply with Eywa, her own journey from the calculating, manipulative strategist she once was into a compassionate teacher making her uniquely qualified to help others find their way through their own spiritual growth. Her vast knowledge of Na'vi history and traditions is complemented by hard-won wisdom about the dangers of letting grief and anger consume one's purpose, often acting as the living memory of the clan while ensuring that past mistakes are never repeated. 'Ipxerey possesses the unique ability to communicate with ancestors through the roots of the Tree of Souls, providing guidance in times of crisis, her own experience with loss and the weight of difficult choices lending gravity and authenticity to her counsel. She maintains the keen intellect and strategic mind that once made her formidable, but now channels these gifts toward building and protecting rather than destroying, her devotion to her family, especially her grandchildren Rawìl, Notä, and Lì'may, serving as both her anchor and her greatest source of joy. Having trained both her daughters-in-law Mìreya and Sänrri as tsakarem, she made the wise decision to delegate the role to Sänrri due to her position as mate to the eyktanay and eldest daughter Kaymi, demonstrating the same calculated precision she once used for darker purposes now applied to ensuring the clan's spiritual future. Beneath her serene exterior lies someone who understands the true cost of power and vengeance, making her guidance all the more valuable to those who seek to avoid the paths that once nearly consumed her, her transformation from one who manipulated others to one who nurtures their authentic growth representing the very best of what redemption and spiritual awakening can achieve.


### Lì'may (Miyako Samaya-Takamatsu)
**Birthday**: September 9


**Personality:** Lì'may is a precocious three-year-old who channels her parents' contrasting energies \- her mother Kaymi's warrior spirit and Sänrri's curious nature. When first rescued from an old RDA facility, she was non-verbal and extremely attached to Sänrri, only gradually emerging from her shell through patient care and love. Now she's a constant whirlwind of activity, declaring herself the "strongest warrior" in all Pandora while collecting "very important specimens" that suspiciously resemble her mother's rock collection. Her transition from silent, traumatized child to boisterous warrior-in-training showcases her resilience and the healing power of family. While she adores playing with the Ngotxì twins, she's especially drawn to her older cousins Notä and Kxayì, whose influence has contributed significantly to her impetuous personality. She often mimics Notä's smug confidence and Kxayì's dramatic flair, creating her own unique blend of chaos that both amuses and concerns her family. While fierce in her proclamations of strength, she maintains an innocence that endears her to even the most serious clan members, turning hardened warriors into indulgent caretakers with her genuine charm and determination to prove herself the "strongest."


### Eyfuwen (Nadim Samaya)
**Ikran**: Torya
**Birthday**: March 13


**Personality:** Charismatic, jovial, and open-hearted, Eyfuwen possesses a magnetic presence that attracts everyone around him, embodying the warmth and steadfast devotion that defined his original role as a loving father and dedicated family man. As the clan's Olo'eyktan, he's a natural storyteller able to captivate his audience with stories that interweave tradition and humor, his resonant laughter serving as a beacon of joy throughout the settlement during difficult times. Deeply connected to Eywa, he sees life as a precious gift to be celebrated and shared, approaching his leadership with the same protective instincts and selfless dedication that once drove him to sacrifice everything for his family's safety. His skills as a mediator reflect his background as a development manager who understood the importance of balancing different perspectives and finding solutions that honor both innovation and tradition, using his charisma and wisdom to resolve conflicts with grace and compassion. As a father, he's extraordinarily loving and encouraging, always pushing Atanvi and Kaymi to pursue their dreams while maintaining the kind of patient, nurturing presence that turns even ordinary moments into celebrations. His relationship with 'Ipxerey is one of deep respect and mutual love, complementing each other perfectly in their clan leadership roles, with his practical wisdom balancing her spiritual insight in ways that strengthen the entire community. Eyfuwen is renowned for organizing large feasts and celebrations, believing that unity is best forged through shared joy and understanding that bringing people together around food and stories creates bonds that transcend individual differences. Despite his naturally extroverted nature and gift for bringing out the best in others, he also values moments of quiet reflection, often seeking Eywa's guidance in the tranquility of the forest where his contemplative side emerges, revealing the thoughtful strategist beneath his jovial exterior who carries the weight of leadership with both humility and unwavering determination to protect those he loves.

## Haykxa Family

### Tsa'rawm (Denji Hayakawa)
**Ikran**: Eykran
**Birthday**: September 12


**Personality:** Energetic and optimistic, a warrior to the core, always ready to help and protect his own. Tsa'rawm is an expert direhorse rider, demonstrating great skill and dexterity in races and handling these majestic animals. His connection with direhorses is almost mystical, able to communicate with them in a way that amazes even the most experienced Na'vi. His bravery in battle is matched only by his compassion outside of it, always willing to help any clan member in need. Crass and loud in his way of being and talking, Tsa'rawm is the most boisterous and loud of the bond siblings alongside Atanvi and Txewan, him being the reason that they have become more outgoing and extroverted. He was adopted as a brother by Kayan, taking his surname and becoming brothers in all but blood, this being repeated also with his sisters Lamaytxa and Sänume. Despite his rough exterior and tendency toward blunt honesty that sometimes creates unnecessary conflicts, he has a good sense of empathy and is willing to save those in danger as much as he can, showing his kind-hearted nature beneath his shortcomings. His personality comes off as rude and harsh in an almost childish way, but this masks a deeper wisdom born from hardship. As someone who lived in severe poverty before joining the clan, he doesn't take anything for granted and approaches life with infectious enthusiasm. His adventurous spirit means he's always willing to try new experiences and isn't afraid of the unknown, often being the first to volunteer for dangerous missions. Though he can be impatient and reckless, he's oddly patient when there's no immediate action needed, content to relax and enjoy peaceful moments with his family. His loyalty to his chosen siblings is absolute, and while he loves to tease them mercilessly, he would give his life to protect any of them without hesitation. As a gift for his many merits in service for the clan, he now stands as **the Thunderous** among the Nine Spears, his **Scarred Spear** bearing witness to countless battles fought to protect his family and clan, while also wielding a fearsome **chainsaw** he found in the fight against the ANAKT Fireteam and their Mangkwan raiding army.


### Rewon (Asa Mitaka)
**Ikran**: Räyä
**Birthday**: July 13


**Personality:** Calm and reflective, Rewon serves as the voice of reason in difficult situations, though this serenity was hard-won through years of personal growth. As a skilled artisan, she channels her intensity into creating beautiful ornaments and tools for her clan, actively participating in weapon and utensil creation ceremonies. Her work is so precise and beautiful that some of her creations are considered sacred by the clan, each piece reflecting both technical mastery and deep understanding. She has a particular gift for mediation, often resolving conflicts with her innate calm and wisdom, drawing from her own experience with internal struggles. However, when mediation fails, Rewon proves herself an adept warrior, standing as the best archer in the Haykxa family alongside her mate Tsa'rawm and brother-in-law Kayan, as well as being christened **the Unerring** among the Nine Spears, she carries **the Balanced Spear**, her precision and calm making her an essential voice of reason for the clan. The scar on her face tells the story of her first encounter with both a thanator and Tsa'rawm \- a moment that taught her to trust despite her natural inclination toward caution. Her close friendship with Mìreya and Syulang shows how far she's come from her once-solitary nature, though she maintains a certain reserved dignity that balances her mate's boisterous energy. Those closest to her occasionally glimpse moments when her composure briefly shifts to reveal a more passionate, almost fierce personality that she's learned to integrate rather than suppress \- these moments of intensity, particularly during hunts or when protecting her family, hint at her complex inner life and the duality she's learned to balance within herself. Her understanding of both restraint and passion makes her uniquely qualified to guide younger Na'vi through their own emotional struggles, particularly Notä and Kxayì, who benefit from her steady guidance without feeling judged for their more impulsive natures.​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​


### Anake (Shū Hayakawa)
**Ikran**: Faylu
**Birthday**: November 23


**Personality:** Anake is the clan's undisputed class clown, channeling his boundless energy into elaborate pranks and schemes that either delight or exhaust those around him. While his mischievous nature and silver tongue (inherited from both parents) make him seem carefree, he harbors serious ambitions \- to one day become Olo'eyktan of his own great clan. Unlike others who might resent Rawìl's inevitable leadership of the Astikasya, Anake sees it as motivation to forge his own path, setting his sights on either founding a new clan or earning leadership of the Zeswa through merit. This seemingly impossible dream only fuels his determination; when Anake sets a goal, he pursues it with unstoppable resolve hidden beneath layers of jokes and laughter. His supernatural archery skills aren't just talent but the result of secret late-night practice sessions where he drops the clown act and pushes himself to exhaustion. He genuinely believes that making people laugh makes them stronger, often using humor to defuse his mother Rewon's intensity or help Rawìl relax when his cousin gets too caught up in responsibilities. His deepest fear is being seen as "just" the funny one, driving him to perfect his warrior skills in private while maintaining his lighthearted facade in public.


### Kxayì (Natsumi Hayakawa)
**Ikran**: Nimun
**Birthday**: October 18


**Personality:** A pure concentration of gremlin energy in Na'vi form, Kxayì inherited her father's loud personality and her aunt Lamaytxa's chaos, creating a whirlwind of noise and unpredictability wherever she goes. Her dream to become a legendary warrior like Tsa'rawm and Kaymi drives every impulsive decision, though her path toward this goal is anything but traditional. She approaches hunting with chaotic innovation, creating techniques so unorthodox they either fail spectacularly or succeed beyond anyone's imagination. Her rivalry with Notä pushes both girls to excellence, though Kxayì's competitive nature means she'll challenge literally anyone to anything \- including challenging Kaymi to sparring matches (she loses every time but keeps trying). Despite being genuinely annoying at times with her inability to control her volume or energy, her fierce loyalty to family is absolute; she'd fight Toruk itself for any of them. She hero-worships Kaymi to an embarrassing degree, often mimicking the eyktanay's mannerisms (poorly) and declaring herself "future protector of the clan" to anyone who'll listen. Her unpredictability is her greatest strength and weakness \- enemies can't anticipate her moves because even SHE doesn't know what she'll do next. Beneath the noise and chaos, she's desperately trying to prove she's more than just "Tsa'rawm's loud daughter," though she'd die before admitting this vulnerability.

### Lamaytxa (Power Hayakawa)
**Ikran**: Fenwì
**Birthday**: January 29


**Personality:** Extroverted and energetic, always looking for ways to have fun and make others laugh. Lamaytxa is a skilled hunter, known for her audacity and ability to face challenges with a smile. Her sense of humor is legendary in the clan, capable of easing tensions even in the most difficult situations. Despite her playful nature, she's incredibly astute in hunting, using innovative tactics that often surprise both her prey and companions alike. Her name means "flood," as she was rescued from one after the RDA flooded her home, and was adopted by Kayan and Tsa'rawm as their sister. She speaks in a very archaic manner and tends to lie and think herself very important. Adept in using weapons like the bow and arrow, she mostly prefers the spear-thrower, and knives of her own making. She’s also adept in the art of blood-letting and blood in general, though it’s unknown where she has learnt this. She recently has taken an interest in a certain Sky Person of the Resistance \- Kobeni Higashiyama, as she thinks she’s “funny for a pinkskin.”, often scaring the girl to oblivion, much to her laughter. She doesn’t trust humans like her younger sister Sänume, aside from three people: Kobeni, Chuchu, and ‘Silver-hand’ Nika.

### Sänume (Nayuta Hayakawa)
**Ikran**: Payarä
**Birthday**: December 14


**Personality:** Intelligent and observant, always analyzing everything happening around her. Sänume is a young apprentice healer, fascinated by plants and their healing properties, and dreams of following in the footsteps of her clan's great healers. Her sharp mind allows her to see connections that others overlook, which has led her to discover new medicinal properties in common plants. Her rivalry (and romantic love) with 'Eweyn drives her to constantly improve. She has a hatred towards humans, except for Sänrri. She's very protective of her family, especially Tsa'rawm and her niece and nephew. Has a troubled relationship and traumatic experiences with her defunct mother, Txalay, who she has omissive trauma. She's ‘Ipxerey's finest student, and is often with her as her right hand woman alongside Mìreya and Sänrri. Kind of bratty. She currently is followed by a pack of *nantang* (viperwolves) that like her and play with her off their own whims, but she does not determine them as pets, but as a part of a whole.


### Kayan (Aki Hayakawa)
**Ikran**: Tswalay
**Birthday**: November 11


**Personality:** Firm, stoic and determined, with a strong sense of duty and justice. Kayan is a natural leader who guides his family with wisdom and determination, though his cold exterior masks a deeply emotional and caring nature. His imposing presence and calm voice have the power to appease conflicts with just a few words, though he can be blunt to the point of rudeness when dealing with those who don't take their responsibilities seriously. Despite his serious exterior, he has a profound love for his family and clan, expressed through small gestures of protection and unwavering loyalty to those he considers his own. His tendency to get attached to others easily makes him fiercely protective, hating to see people get hurt, especially those under his care. As Eyfuwen's right hand and the clan's *mowarsiyu* (advisor to the Olo’eyktan), he takes his role with absolute seriousness, believing that leadership requires sacrifice and constant vigilance. His relationship with his adopted siblings Tsa'rawm, Lamaytxa, and Sänume reveals his capacity for deep familial bonds, while his relationship with Txolaw provides the perfect balance between his driven nature and his mate's peaceful approach to life. Though he maintains professional distance during clan duties, those closest to him know he would give everything to protect his family, even at the cost of his own well-being. He was initially considered to bear the weight of the Balanced Spear, but he relinquished the honor due to him finding someone more worthy: his sister-in-law Rewon.

### Txolaw (Angel Hayakawa)
**Ikran**: Keyna
**Birthday**: August 9


**Personality:** Txolaw embodies a fascinating duality – appearing stoic and serious but harboring a surprisingly gentle spirit within. His legendary laziness is a constant source of amusement and exasperation for the clan, often claiming he’d “rather sleep than hunt” and frequently found napping in the warmest spots of Sheltering Falls. As one who underwent gender transition through Eywa’s eye, he carries a profound spiritual connection that manifests in his unique ability to commune with Pandora’s spirits. This spiritual gift extends to an extraordinary and sacred role: he is the craftsman of the Nine Spears, forging their ceremonial weapons through ritual fire, chants, and calls to Eywa. Each spear is blessed and shaped according to the warrior who will carry it, and Txolaw feels the weight of every battle through their blades, connected to the Nine through the weapons he creates. Despite his apparent apathy, his spiritual sensitivity makes him essential during ceremonies, though he approaches his duties with visible reluctance and dramatic sighs. His aversion to physical touch (except with Kayan) stems from his overwhelming empathy – he “feels” others’ emotions too easily, making casual contact uncomfortable. This creates a complex relationship with clan life, as his gentle nature conflicts with his preference for solitude. His relationship with Kayan provides perfect balance, Kayan’s driven nature complementing Txolaw’s peaceful approach, though his laziness remains a source of fond teasing. For Kayan, who declined the title of the Tenth Spear to serve the Olo’eyktan as *mowarisyu* (councilor), Txolaw created a spear unlike any other. Not through public ritual or ceremony, but through their tsaheylu bond alone – their queues intertwined as he poured intention directly into the wood, the bone and the stone, their souls merged in the act of creation. This spear needed no blessing from the tsahìk, no oath before Eywa, because their mating bond was already sacred, already witnessed by the Great Mother herself. It remains known only to them, a private vow made manifest. When protecting his family, glimpses of fierce determination emerge, though he invariably returns to his preferred state of peaceful inactivity as soon as possible. Yet the Nine Spears hanging in the sacred spaces of Sheltering Falls are testament to a love so profound it shapes the very weapons that keep his people alive.
