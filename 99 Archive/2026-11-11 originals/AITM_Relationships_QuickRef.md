## Samaya Family

### Eyfuwen
- **Mate:** 'Ipxerey
- **Children:** Kaymi, Atanvi
- **Grandchildren:** Rawìl, Notä, Lì'may (adoptive)
- **Close bonds:** Tse'hayu (old war-companion)

### 'Ipxerey
- **Mate:** Eyfuwen
- **Children:** Kaymi, Atanvi
- **Grandchildren:** Rawìl, Notä, Lì'may (adoptive)
- **Students (primary):** Sänrri (tsakarem apprentice), Sänume (healer apprentice), Mìreya (strategic advisor)

### Kaymi
- **Parents:** Eyfuwen & 'Ipxerey
- **Sibling:** Atanvi
- **Mate:** Sänrri
- **Adoptive daughter:** Lì'may
- **Complicated/resolved:** Tìmeyo (once destined mates before exile severed it)
- **Rivalry → respect:** Rängay (the Kaymi/Rängay pact — see Lore doc)

### Atanvi
- **Parents:** Eyfuwen & 'Ipxerey
- **Sibling:** Kaymi
- **Mate:** Mìreya
- **Children:** Rawìl, Notä
- **Blood-oath siblings:** Tsa'rawm, Txewan
- **Gender transition companion:** Txewan (underwent the Eye of Eywa together)

### Mìreya
- **Mate:** Atanvi
- **Children:** Rawìl, Notä
- **Close as sisters:** Syulang, Rewon
- **Student of:** 'Ipxerey (strategic and botanical)

### Sänrri
- **Mate:** Kaymi
- **Adoptive daughter:** Lì'may
- **Mentor:** 'Ipxerey
- **Cohort:** Rängay, Pxayu, Apayì, Pamyaw (the Lost Cohort — her original spiritual training circle)
- **Old human bond:** Kobeni (first person to speak Japanese to her on Pandora)

### Rawìl
- **Parents:** Atanvi & Mìreya
- **Sibling:** Notä
- **Best friend:** Anake
- **Courting:** Tulìpey

### Notä
- **Parents:** Atanvi & Mìreya
- **Sibling:** Rawìl
- **Rival:** Eantu (volatile, mirrors parents' old dynamic)
- **Former crush:** Nì'syu (resolved)

### Lì'may
- **Adoptive parents:** Kaymi & Sänrri
- **Adored by/influenced by:** Notä, Kxayì (chaos influences)
- **Playmates:** Mìyo, Mìrì
