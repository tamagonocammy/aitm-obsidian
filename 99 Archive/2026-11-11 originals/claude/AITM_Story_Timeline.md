# Avatar: Into the Mists — Story Timeline

**By Millie Caicedo** · built with Claude, November 2026

This is the master order of events for AITM, arranged on the Twenty One Pilots era framework. It pulls from the Siege of the Falls Arc, the Wild Hunt Arc, the Factions Profiles, the Flying Troupe, the Great Games Legacy, the Worldbuilding Reference, and the canon decisions settled on 1 November 2026. Where the documents leave a gap, it is marked **OPEN** rather than filled in.

Tentative song for the whole AU: **"Drag Path"** (original version, *Breach: Digital Remains*).

---

## Prologue — The Forest and the Falls
*Pre-lore (self-titled / Vessel)*

The Astikasya live as herbalists in their ancestral forests. A tragedy forces them to migrate into the Spires of the Clouded Forest. **OPEN: the tragedy is never named in the documents.**

The Samaya, Haykxa and Ngotxì families settle Sheltering Falls. They find the Cave of Truth and the submerged Tree of Souls and develop woodshaping.

Deep backstory that sits here, before the main story:

**The twins' exile.** Their father's neglect leads to their mother Luey's death. Syayvi and Tìmeyo kill him and are exiled. Syayvi is renamed Tswa'avä and cut off from Eywa. Tìmeyo loses the tsakarem path and the promised mating with Kaymi.

**The blood oath.** As children, Atanvi, Tsa'rawm and Txewan swear siblinghood over stolen zangke. Kaymi teases them about it. Kayan first thinks it is a game, then asks the two girls to look after her brother. 'Eweyn is still a baby.

**Other roots.** Atanvi and Txewan make their journey to the Eye of Eywa together. Txafwe flies in from the Eastern Sea and stays for Rängay.

## The Dreamwalker Years

Dr. Tomori Takamatsu arrives on Pandora with RDA Sci-Ops. Nika helps her into her avatar body. She defects and follows Jake Sully's path.

She trains as tsakarem under 'Ipxerey. The Lost Cohort (Rängay, Pxayu, Apayì, Pamyaw) forms around her. She clashes with Rängay and falls for Kaymi.

Under the Tree of Souls, she undergoes the consciousness transfer and becomes Sänrri for good, with "The Line" as its anchor. The scene is planned but not yet written.

**~15 years before the Siege — the Great Games.** Sänrri is already bonded with Kaymi. On the same night at the Heart of the Plains, Rawìl (Atanvi and Mìreya) and Anake (Tsa'rawm and Rewon) are conceived.

Lì'may is rescued from an RDA facility and adopted by Kaymi and Sänrri.

**OPEN:** whether the bonding with Kaymi came before or after the transfer, and exactly when Lì'may was adopted.

## Blurryface — The Varang Years
*Song: "Paladin Strait" belongs to the clan as a whole, with Sänrri as its throughline.*

**~6 months before the Siege: the events of Frontiers of Pandora.**

Varang's corruption spreads among the Mangkwan, and she allies with Quaritch.

**Bridgehead.** The Mangkwan occupy Bridgehead. Cpl. Miri Sugo defects, flees through the jungle, and survives an ambush by saying Nika's name. He is sent on to the Resistance.

**Delta-7.** The Astikasya raid Mining Outpost Delta-7. Kobeni hides behind the air filters, hears Japanese from Sänrri, and defects.

The ikran-mounted crossbow is developed.

**Within the Mangkwan:** Syayvi and Tìmeyo, sheltering with the Mangkwan, witness the heresy and begin gathering the Scattered Ashes from inside.

**OPEN:** Miri's profile says he was ambushed by "the Nine Spears," but Syayvi only becomes the Ninth during the Siege. Either the name existed earlier with a different ninth, or Miri names them in hindsight.

## Scaled and Icy — The Captivity
*The bright surface hiding "Clancy is dead."*

Sänrri, the Lost Cohort and Txafwe (Umiri) are captured by the Mangkwan and held for months. They watch sacred rhythms twisted into heresy. Txafwe sees Varang working with Quaritch.

**Roneyu, the Keons of AITM.** Roneyu (Crona), Mangkwan-born and invisible, helps Sänrri unseen. They clear a path, perhaps through something that never looks like a person. Then they slip away that same night, unnoticed as always. To the Mangkwan, the Roneyu who vanished simply stopped existing. **Sänrri never learns who helped her**, just as Clancy never knew Keons saved him in "Saturday" by seizing Trash.

**The Toruk Makto.** Hiding and desperate, Sänrri reaches a place where she can make tsaheylu with Eywa. There she meets the **Second and Third Toruk Makto, Tunskxe (Simon) and Yoko'fya (Madoka)**. They are her Neds on her Voldsøy.

**The lesson.** Tsaheylu is a consented bond. The Mangkwan force it to dominate through pain, and pure Na'vi, who never needed to defend themselves inside the network, have no resistance to it. Tunskxe teaches will and Yoko'fya teaches hope. Together they teach Sänrri to **resist a forced bond and then overpower it from the inside**, the way Quaritch, paralyzed, held on through will alone. The Astikasya never initiate a forced bond themselves. Kiri remains the one true exception: she holds "sudo" over the whole Eywa network.

**The escape.** Apayì poses as Mangkwan and gets a message to Tsu'lan, while the Ashes work from inside. **Kaymi (Eri) and Tsu'lan (Yuji) go together** to bring Sänrri out. This is the Torchbearer with one Bandito at her side, and the start of Tsu'lan's personal bond with Sänrri.

The Ashes and the Cohort come home with Sänrri, carrying the warning: **"They are coming."**

## Overcompensate — The Homecoming
*Sänrri's song; the "we both" is Sänrri and Kaymi.*

Sänrri returns to the Falls changed. She tells the clan about Tunskxe and Yoko'fya, and they take it as a **good omen**: Toruk Makto only rise in times of great sorrow, and two of them chose to speak to the clan's tsakarem.

## Clancy — The Teaching, the Occupation and the Siege

**The teaching.** Sänrri teaches the lesson to the Nine first, then to the whole clan before the Siege, preparing them for what is coming.

**Tìmeyo and Kerevi.** Their system makes their will stronger, because they already live the inner battle of front and alter every day. Kerevi knows the Mangkwan technique itself, but **would never use it on clanmates, because of Syayvi.**

**Fire and Ash.** Weeks before the Siege, the clan leaves to fight beside Jake Sully. Those left behind include 'Ipxerey, Nika and Earth House, Tsu'lan, Syayvi and the Scattered Ashes, the elders, and the children.

**The Occupation (short).** The Mangkwan and the ANAKT Fireteam overrun the Falls by numbers and by force. Rawìl, Anake and Tulìpey run Firelight-style operations through the hidden passages. Notä picks up a minigun and the Third Law breaks. War paint is painted for survival, not ceremony. Syayvi, pregnant, fights beside Tsu'lan to protect the children. **OPEN: exact length. The Siege doc still says "weeks."**

**The return.** The adults come home victorious to a stolen home. Notä screams, "WHERE WERE YOU?!"

**The Siege (long).** The ANAKT gauntlet:

**Ivan** is brought down by Mìreya, Rewon and the children, with Rewon's arrow at the end. **OPEN: the Character Database says both "killed" and "vanished."**

**Sua** threatens Syulang and the children. Txewan kills her in silence and never speaks of it.

**Mizi** finds Sua's body and is caught in Till's blast. She loses her sight to shrapnel from a forbidden human explosive during the nursery defense, falls in her fight with Atanvi (*La Piadosa y la Bruja*), survives blind, and defects.

**Till** is cornered in the workshops by Tsa'rawm and Ketxe. His kuru is severed, and he drowns in the aquifer.

**Hyuna** fights Sänrri and Kaymi, then Kaymi alone. She dies laughing and receives a warrior's burial.

Chuchu: "THEY'RE CHILDREN YOU FUCKIN' BASTARD!"

Syayvi earns **the Ashen Spear** and becomes the Ninth Spear, **the Penitent**.

## Breach — The Final Battle and the Wild Hunt
*"City Walls" opens it.*

**The final battle of the Siege: the Nine against Luka**, in the Cave of Truth. Luka takes Sänrri: "Hello, Dr. Takamatsu." This is AITM's "Nico and the Niners," and the first time all nine fight as nine. It reconciles the three older versions into one three-stage fight: Tsu'lan's "I'm you" moment; the full Nine striking together with the Scattered Ashes; and Kaymi and Sänrri at the center with "City Walls." The sacred pond runs red. Tsu'lan lets Luka go, and Luka escapes wounded: "There are many of us."

**Aftermath.** The clan rebuilds and argues over keeping the human weapons. Tìmeyo and Yawyay leave for the reef. Syayvi and Nì'syu stay, and Sìlpey is coming. Mizi wanders the mist guiding lost children home.

**The Flying Troupe** arrives on Tsu'lan's word. Roneyu comes with them and faces the tsakarem they saved, who does not know them. Tsu'lan tells them, "You are here now. That is what matters." Syayvi sits beside them at the fire. **OPEN: whether Roneyu and Syayvi knew each other inside the Mangkwan.**

**The Wild Hunt.** Tsu'lan's vow gathers Yukeyo, the Flying Troupe, and Tsyokanì, the Ghost of the Northern Ravines. **Luka dies at the end of the Hunt**, warning them again. 'Ipxerey welcomes Tsyokanì home: "You do not travel alone."

The war is not over.
