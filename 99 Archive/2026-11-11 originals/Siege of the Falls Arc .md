The Siege of the Falls Arc - Summary
Setup & Context
Timeline Placement:
	•	Takes place weeks after the events of Avatar: Fire and Ash
	•	Approximately 6 months after Frontiers of Pandora and its DLCs
	•	The “Shibuya Incident” of the AU - everything goes wrong
The Warning:
The Scattered Ashes (Syayvi, Tìmeyo, Nì’syu, Txafwe, Yawyay) and the Lost Cohort return to Sheltering Falls after escaping Mangkwan captivity. Their message: “They are coming.”
The Main Conflict
Fire and Ash Events:
The Astikasya clan leaves to help Jake Sully in the main Fire and Ash conflict (the “IW/Empire Strikes Back/Marineford” level disaster of Avatar). While they’re away fighting that war, the Western Frontier is left vulnerable.
The Occupation:
The clan returns home victorious, expecting safety, only to find Sheltering Falls has been occupied by:
	•	Mangkwan clan forces (regular soldiers/warriors)
	•	ANAKT Fireteam (elite recombinant squad under Quaritch’s command)
	•	Varang’s corrupted regime working with the RDA
What Happened During Occupation:
The people left behind (elderly, injured, very young children with caretakers) suffered under Mangkwan rule for weeks. But the teenage warriors - led by Rawìl (15), Anake (15), and Tulìpey (15) - formed a guerrilla resistance like the Firelights from Arcane, running black ops to protect survivors and harass occupying forces.
The ANAKT Fireteam
Elite recombinant soldiers, each with unique fight mechanics and locations (Destiny 2 Forsaken-like structure):
	1.	Ivan: The Scolopendra - Strategic chess match (vs Mìreya, with Rawìl & Notä intervening)
	2.	Sua: The Clematis - Silent knife battle (vs Txewan)
	3.	Mizi: The Witch - Nursery protection (vs Atanvi, defects after learning Sua died)
	4.	Till: The Thornbriar - Chaotic workshop destruction (vs Tsa’rawm, kuru severed, drowned in aquifer)
	5.	Hyuna: The Nightshade - Training grounds death match (vs Kaymi, killed but Kaymi wounded)
	6.	Luka: The Strangler Fig - Final boss in Cave of Truth (vs Tsu’lan + Scattered Ashes, escapes wounded)
Key Character Moments
The Teenagers:
	•	Ran guerrilla operations for weeks during occupation
	•	Used human weapons (breaking the Third Law about metal)
	•	Notä with minigun like Jinx from Arcane Season 2
	•	Became hardened warriors protecting younger kids
	•	Firelights/Lost Boys energy - living in hidden passages, stealing supplies, hit-and-run tactics
The Parents’ Return:
Devastating reunion where parents realize their children had to grow up too fast:
	•	Rawìl giving tactical reports like a commander
	•	Anake trying to joke but voice cracking from trauma
	•	Notä angry: “WHERE WERE YOU?!” but also clinging to Mìreya
	•	Kxayì furious and sobbing simultaneously
	•	Younger kids traumatized, some not recognizing parents until later
	•	War paint recognition - same symbols but applied in different contexts of survival vs ceremony
A core fighting group led by Eyktanay Kaymi:
	1.  	Kaymi - The leader 
	2.	Atanvi - The gentle heart
	3.	Txewan - The quiet strategist
	4.	Tsa’rawm - The chaotic berserker (uses AMP suit chainsaw)
	5.	Tsu’lan - The moral powerhouse
	6.	Syayvi - The redemption seeker
	7.	Rewon - The perfect archer
Brutal Moments:
	•	Tsa’rawm severing Till’s kuru - the most dishonorable death for Na’vi (spiritual execution)
	•	Tsu’lan’s “I’m you” moment with Luka, gaining facial scars
	•	Kaymi killing Hyuna but getting seriously wounded
	•	Txewan’s silent knife fight with Sua (Mizi never learns Txewan killed her beloved)
	•	Hours of non-stop fighting showing mountain warrior endurance
The Resistance Role:
	•	Nika, Chuchu, Kobeni, Aliya providing human tech support and guerrilla tactics
	•	Chuchu’s badass moment: Shotgun blast protecting Na’vi kids - “THEY’RE CHILDREN YOU FUCKIN’ BASTARD!”
	•	Chuchu teaching Tsa’rawm human curse words (he uses them on Till during their fight)
	•	Chuchu teaching Tsu’lan earthian boxing (he uses it against Luka)
Aftermath & Consequences
Casualties:
Unknown - originally planned to kill characters who died in canon (Eyfuwen/Nadim, Kayan/Aki, Lamaytxa/Power, Tìfyaw/Kikuri, plus the three dead recoms) but this wasn’t finalized
Survivors:
	•	Luka - Escaped to RDA, wounded and planning revenge
	•	Mizi - Defected, now wandering alone unable to join either side
	•	Till - MIA/presumed dead in aquifer after kuru severing
Psychological Impact:
	•	Entire clan dealing with massive trauma
	•	Identity crisis over using human weapons (breaking sacred traditions)
	•	Kids forced to become soldiers
	•	Trust issues with how vulnerable they were
	•	Question of whether to keep weapons or return to old ways
The Scattered Ashes:
	•	Proved themselves during the siege despite being exiles
	•	Insane aura - fighting people they used to know, coordinating attacks, leading charges
	•	Eventually scatter to different locations:
	•	Syayvi & Nì’syu stay (pregnant with Sìlpey)
	•	Tìmeyo & Yawyay leave for the reef (back to Ta’unui clan)
	•	Others finding their own paths to healing
Themes & Parallels
Primary Inspiration: TYBW/Shibuya Incident
	•	Home invasion while protectors away
	•	Sacred spaces corrupted
	•	Children becoming soldiers
	•	Special War Powers (Sänrri, Kaymi, Rawìl, Rewon, Nika)
	•	Systematic hunting of targets
	•	Everything changes after this arc
Secondary Parallels:
	•	Seven Samurai - defending community against overwhelming force
	•	Destiny 2 Forsaken - hunting named bosses, Luka as The Fanatic
	•	Kill la Kill - retaking the academy from corrupt forces
	•	Historical “don’t fuck with mountain people” - mountain tribes are built different
Central Message:
“Everything is allowed in war” but at what cost to your soul? The Astikasya survive but lose parts of their identity and innocence in the process.
