## The Astikasya Clan

The **Astikasya Clan** is renowned for their spiritual depth, innovative spirit, and sacred connection to Eywa. Once known primarily as skilled herbalists, a tragic event reshaped their collective identity, forcing them from ancestral forests to the Spires of the Clouded Forest where they evolved into formidable hunters, visionary artisans, and fearless defenders. Their strength lies not in brute force, but in their ability to adapt without forgetting who they are. Under the guidance of **Olo’eyktan Eyfuwen** and **Tsahìk ‘Ipxerey**, the Astikasya cultivate this unique blend of tradition and progress, embodying a shared belief: that true wisdom lies in honoring the past while embracing change as a living force. In this way, they have preserved their spiritual practices, song traditions, and rituals, while simultaneously developing technologies and methods that protect both their people and their way of life.

Their home, the **Sheltering Falls**, also known as the Falls, is a breathtaking refuge carved deep within the mist-shrouded Spires. Hidden among towering cliffs and veiled in perpetual fog, the settlement is a harmony of stone, water, and living wood; using intricate woodshaping techniques, the Astikasya mold nearby trees into bridges, walls, sanctuaries, and communal platforms that blend naturally into the mountainside, a song of resilience carved within the mountain. At the heart of their territory lies the **Cave of Truth** (Na’vi: **Tìngayä Slär**), their most sacred site. Inside this vast cavern, waterfalls cascade into glowing, bioluminescent pools beneath which rests the submerged Tree of Souls, a rare and powerful place where the clan communes directly with Eywa through spiritual ceremonies like soul transfers, naming rites, healing rituals, and ancestral gatherings. The waters carry memory, and the mists above seem to whisper old stories to those willing to listen.

This spirit of adaptation is further exemplified by their remarkable ingenuity, especially in aerial combat; their most celebrated creation: the **ikran-mounted crossbow**, which has changed the course of their defense efforts, its swift, accurate design making even RDA aircraft wary. Yet every tool they forge carries meaning, created with intention and weighed against its harmony with Eywa. Much of this rapid advancement can be attributed to **Sänrri te Tsawtral Tanhì'ite**, formerly known as **Dr. Tomori Takamatsu**, a former human avatar driver ("dreamwalker/uniltìranyu" to the Na'vi) and now the clan's tsakarem. Once a scientist, she chose a new path guided by Eywa's sacred rhythms, her unique knowledge bridging human insight and Na’vi wisdom. Under ‘Ipxerey’s mentorship, Sänrri has helped develop tools that enhance, not disrupt, their communion with Eywa. Though forged in hardship, the Astikasya are not defined by grief but by growth. Every mist surrounding their home carries memory, and every woven thread or carved staff bears witness to their story: a people who remember, who dream forward, and whose songs and inventions echo the belief that adaptation is not betrayal, but rather, it is reverence in motion.
