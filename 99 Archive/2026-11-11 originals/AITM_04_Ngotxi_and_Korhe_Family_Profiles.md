# Avatar: Into the Mists — Chunk 4: Ngotxì & Korhe Family Character Profiles
## Ngotxì Family

### Txewan/Txewì (Hitori Gotou)
**Ikran**: Kxeyo
**Birthday**: February 21


**Personality:** There are two voices that live inside Txewan te Ngotxì Mitsìyo'ite, and they are always at war. The first is the stutter: sharp, involuntary, a betrayal of her body when the clan gathers and all those eyes turn toward her. It fractures her sentences, makes her shrink inward, makes her wish she could disappear into the mist like the phantom they named her. The second voice is the one that emerges when she sings. It does not stutter. It does not falter. It rises clear and unbidden, weaving the stories and sorrows of her people into melodies that make even the sternest elders pause and listen. This is the voice that earned her **the Singing Spear**, the one wrapped in songcords that hum when she moves, the one that coordinates warriors through sound and shadow during battle. This is the voice that made her one of the Nine, despite carrying the weight of feeling perpetually inadequate next to warriors who seem so certain of themselves. But long before the spear, before the title, before she was ever called **the Phantom**, there was the **Woven Harmony.** Txewan, Syulang, Katirì, and Tswesya: four souls bound not by exile or survival, but by the quiet, radical act of choosing each other. They are a refuge built in trust, a small circle where music flows as naturally as breath and silence is just as sacred. It was within this circle that Txewan first learned her voice (both of them) mattered. That her catastrophic spirals, her worst-case-scenario mind that spins and spins until she can barely breathe, could be held gently by people who understood. That Tswesya's dry humor could make her laugh mid-panic. That Katirì's steady rhythms could ground her when the world felt too loud. That Syulang's warmth could remind her she was loved even when she felt like a burden. They rehearse in quiet moments, tucked away in corners of Sheltering Falls where the bioluminescent vines glow soft and the sound of waterfalls drowns out the rest of the world. Txewan composes, her fingers trembling slightly as she works through a new melody, and Tswesya listens with that unreadable expression that somehow still feels like approval. Katirì's drums provide the heartbeat, the foundation, while Syulang hums along, her voice weaving effortlessly with Txewan's in a way that makes the anxious composer believe (just for a moment) that harmony is possible. These are the moments where Txewan becomes just **Txewì**, the nickname Tswesya gave her, the one that feels like home. She is deeply devoted to her family: Syulang, her beloved wife, whose cheerful presence keeps her tethered when anxiety threatens to pull her under; her bond siblings Atanvi and Tsa'rawm, who helped her become brave enough to transition through the Eye of Eywa alongside Atanvi, an experience that reshaped her understanding of herself; and her children. Mìyo and Mìrì, the twins whose matching voices make her heart ache with pride, and baby Netezu, whose quiet fascination with sound already mirrors her own. Motherhood terrified her. Still does, sometimes. The fear that she will never be enough, that her anxiety will somehow damage them, haunts her in the dark hours. But Syulang is patient. The Harmony is patient. And slowly, Txewan is learning to be patient with herself. Her music chronicles the clan's emotional life: the victories, the losses, the small joys that might otherwise be forgotten. She composes constantly, her mind always half-lost in melody, and when she takes a breath from creating she often looks to the stars, talking to Sänrri about the vastness of space and Eywa's infinite presence within it. When nervous, she fidgets. Kuru flailing in the wind, tail swishing and lashing out, fingers tapping out rhythms only she can hear. And sometimes, late at night when the clan sleeps and the mist curls thick around Sheltering Falls, she hums. Not for anyone else. Just for herself. Just to remember that her voice, in all its forms, still exists.




### 'Eweyn (Futari Gotou)
**Ikran**: Sìltu
**Birthday**: July 7


**Personality:** Young, curious, and energetic to the core, 'Eweyn is a born explorer and a whirlwind of enthusiasm, always seeking new adventures and experiences in Pandora's vast mysteries alongside her inseparable ikran, Sìltu. As one of the youngest adults, often alongside her mate Sänume with whom she shares a spirited rivalry bordering on love and an insatiable thirst for knowledge, 'Eweyn is a free soul, frequently seen gathering fruits or soaring through the skies. She adores her young nieces Mìyo and Mìrì and nephew Netezu, greatly enjoying telling them stories of her explorations, showing them new things she has found in the wild, and matching their playful energy. Her relationship with her older sister Txewan is good and affectionate, though she loves to tease her a lot, often making her sister embarrassed but also helping to lighten Txewan's anxious moods. Unlike many Na'vi, 'Eweyn is unafraid of Sky People technology; her innate curiosity leads her to constantly ask Sänrri and the Resistance about Earth and her life there, giving her an exceptionally broad perspective and a future ripe with discovery. She is often seen with the Resistance’s HQ deep within the Clouded Forest, sharing findings and information with Nika.

### Syulang (Ikuyo Gotou)
**Ikran**: Kxarì
**Birthday**: April 21


**Personality:** Syulang te Ayfomä Kxurryo'ite has never met a gathering she couldn't improve or a conflict she couldn't gently defuse. She moves through the clan like sunlight through mist: warm, pervasive, impossible to ignore. She organizes ceremonies, makes sure the elders have comfortable seating, checks that the children aren't getting into too much trouble, and ensures everyone feels valued and seen. She's the shortest of the parent generation, a fact that delights the younglings and occasionally frustrates her, but what she lacks in height she more than makes up for in sheer force of care. The clan still talks about how she delivered Mìyo and Mìrì naturally, without remedies or assistance, her stubbornness and optimism carrying her through. It became a story they tell to remind themselves that sometimes strength looks like endurance, like trusting your body even when it hurts. But beneath that sunny exterior, Syulang wrestles with her own quiet anxieties. She sees the unique talents around her (Txewan's compositions that make people weep, Katirì's rhythms that connect souls to Eywa, Tswesya's instruments that are works of art in themselves) and sometimes she wonders what she brings besides organization and enthusiasm. The voice is small, easily drowned out by her natural optimism, but it exists. And so she channels that uncertainty into becoming the best supporter she can be, the one who makes space for others to shine. She organizes the Woven Harmony's rehearsals not because she leads them, but because she genuinely loves seeing her chosen family come together. She smooths over Tswesya and Katirì's occasional creative disagreements with patience and humor. She sits with Txewan during anxious spirals, not trying to fix them, just being present. She turns every practice session into something more: a space where vulnerability is safe, where mistakes are learned, where the four of them are held by something bigger than any one of them could create alone. The Harmony is Syulang's heart made manifest. They laugh together, bicker gently, share comfortable silences, push each other to be better. Syulang thrives in this space. Here, her scattered energy becomes enthusiasm. Her tendency to talk too much becomes the bridge that helps Tswesya and Txewan feel less alone in their quietness. Her optimism, which could feel exhausting in larger settings, becomes exactly what the circle needs when creative frustration sets in. She works alongside Katirì to help Txewan overcome her social anxiety, and alongside Atanvi and Tsa'rawm when more serious intervention is needed. Her emotional intelligence is her greatest gift. She reads people instinctively, knows when to step in and when to step back, understands that sometimes the most loving thing you can do is simply show up. She mediates conflicts not by imposing solutions but by helping people see the good in each other, by creating space for reconciliation. Her gatherings and celebrations aren't frivolous; they're the threads that weave the clan together, reminders that even in the shadow of war and loss, there is still joy worth protecting. And at home, with Txewan and their children, Syulang allows herself to be imperfect. To admit she's tired. To let her mate's quiet strength hold her for once. To watch the twins finish each other's sentences and baby Netezu reach for sounds with his small hands, and feel, despite everything, that this (this messy, beautiful, chosen family) is more than enough.


### Mitsìyo (Michiyo Gotou)
**Ikran**: Moey
**Birthday**: March 3


**Personality:** Gentle and nurturing with an unshakeable inner strength, Mitsìyo serves as the clan's emotional anchor and one of its most respected elders. Her warm presence and natural empathy make her the go-to confidante for clan members of all ages, from worried parents seeking advice to youngsters needing comfort after difficult days. As a master weaver and keeper of domestic traditions, she creates beautiful ceremonial garments and teaches younger Na'vi the intricate patterns that tell their clan's history through textile art. Her patient teaching style and infinite capacity for understanding helped shape both Txewan's gentle nature and 'Eweyn's confidence, though she never plays favorites among her beloved grandchildren. Despite her soft-spoken demeanor, Mitsìyo possesses the quiet authority that comes from decades of wise counsel, and even the most stubborn clan members find themselves listening when she speaks. Her bond with Tse'hayu spans many seasons of shared music-making and child-rearing, their partnership serving as a model of enduring love for the younger generations. She has an uncanny ability to sense when someone is struggling emotionally, often appearing with warm food and gentle words exactly when they're needed most. Her greatest joy comes from watching her family thrive, especially seeing how Txewan's musical gifts and 'Eweyn's adventurous spirit carry forward the best traditions of their lineage while creating new ones.

### Tse'hayu (Naoki Gotou)
**Ikran**: Ìkìtä’
**Birthday**: March 3


**Personality:** A distinguished elder who easily blends the roles of warrior, artist, and nurturer, Tse'hayu serves as both the clan's master cook and keeper of musical traditions. Once a formidable warrior-bard who fought alongside Eyfuwen in their younger days, he now channels his passion into two distinct but equally important crafts: creating exquisite traditional instruments and preparing the communal meals that bring the clan together each evening. His approach to both music and cooking reflects his perfectionist nature, every dish must be seasoned precisely, every instrument tuned to spiritual as well as auditory perfection. As the clan's head cook, he takes immense pride in ensuring everyone is well-fed, often seen before dawn preparing elaborate feasts and teaching younger clan members the sacred recipes passed down through generations. His direct, sometimes blunt communication style masks a deeply caring nature, he believes in pushing others to reach their potential through honest feedback rather than empty praise, whether they're learning to play an instrument or properly season a stew. His mentorship of Txewan nurtured her natural musical gifts while his patient cooking lessons taught 'Eweyn that even adventurous spirits need grounding in traditional skills. Despite his serious demeanor during instruction, he transforms completely when either performing music or presenting a particularly successful meal, his joy infectious enough to get even the most reserved clan members celebrating around the evening fires. His partnership with Mitsìyo demonstrates how strength and gentleness complement each other perfectly: where she provides emotional support through weaving and counsel, he nourishes both body and soul through music and food. Though he rarely speaks of his warrior past, his strategic mind makes him a valued advisor to the clan leadership, and his experience in battle, art, and sustenance gives him unique insight into the balance between protecting tradition and embracing necessary change.

### Mìyo (Miyo Gotou)
**Ikran**: Säno
**Birthday**: February 1


**Personality:** Mìyo is a cheerful and playful girl, full of energy and curiosity, already showing signs of being a talented dancer who enjoys participating in ritual dances with her family. She has a special childish accent shared with her twin sister Mìrì, and they often finish each other’s sentences and speak in unison, which sometimes bewilders adults but delights other children. Mìyo and Mìrì love exploring together, maintaining a collection of small treasures they’ve found during their adventures around Pandora \- everything from unusually shaped rocks to brilliantly colored feathers to seed pods that make interesting sounds. She’s endearingly scatterbrained, often forgetting where she put things or getting distracted mid-sentence by something shiny, but her enthusiasm is so infectious that no one has the heart to scold her. She spends considerable time with her older cousins Notä and Kxayì, unconsciously adopting their mannerisms and speech patterns, which sometimes results in an eight-year-old using surprisingly sophisticated vocabulary or attempting “mature” expressions that come out adorably wrong. Mìyo’s spontaneity, excitability, and mischievousness make her a perfect foil to her twin \- while Mìrì provides the calm planning, Mìyo supplies the chaotic energy that turns simple games into grand adventures. Her light-hearted teasing of her sister often involves gentle pranks or silly faces, and she has an innate talent for making anyone laugh, even during the most serious moments.

### Mìrì (Muri Gotou)
**Ikran**: Kxarä
**Birthday**: February 1


**Personality:** Similar to her sister in her zest for life and exploration, Mìrì shares the special childish accent with twin Mìyo, often finishing each other’s sentences in that bewildering way that makes adults smile and shake their heads. However, where Mìyo is scattered sunshine, Mìrì is focused moonlight \- she’s the spitting image of her mother Txewan in both appearance and temperament, naturally more taciturn with strangers but surprisingly wise when she chooses to speak. Despite being only eight years old, when Mìrì does talk, she often says things that are startlingly mature for her age, causing adults to do double-takes and wonder where such insight comes from. She’s always seen carrying her small carved ikran toy, a gift that has become her constant companion and security object, which she’s named and treats as if it were alive. As the older twin (by mere minutes, but she takes this responsibility seriously), Mìrì often takes the conversational lead despite her generally quieter nature, her calmer and more collected demeanor providing stability when Mìyo’s enthusiasm threatens to overwhelm a situation. Paradoxically, while she appears more mature and thoughtful, she’s actually the clumsier of the two \- frequently tripping over her own feet or accidentally knocking things over, though she handles these mishaps with such dignified resignation that it only adds to her charm. Her treasure collection tends toward more practical or beautiful items rather than random curiosities, showing an early aesthetic sense that mirrors her musical family’s artistic nature.

### Netezu (Ryūnosuke Gotou)
**Birthday**: September 7


**Personality:** Netezu is a 1 year old Na'vi baby, full of curiosity about the world around him. His large golden eyes observe everything with fascination, especially the lights and colors of bioluminescent plants. He has a strong attachment to his mothers, Txewan and Syulang, calming immediately upon hearing their voices or music. Netezu is surprisingly quiet for his age, but has a cheerful gurgle he uses to express happiness. He shows an early affinity for sounds, turning his head towards the source of any melody. His older sisters, Mìyo and Mìrì, adore him and he responds with smiles and excited movements whenever he sees them. Although still very young, Netezu already shows signs of being an observant and sensitive baby. He recently began to walk on his own, making sounds that are like the words taught by everyone in the clan.

## Korhe Family

### Tsu’ong (Guel Jeturk)
**Ikran**: Nìwak
**Birthday**: October 2

*Personality:*  Once defined by arrogance and a fiery temper, Tsu'ong has undergone a profound transformation that shapes his role as a father, clan member, and warrior. His rough temperament and quick anger once made him an intimidating presence, with absolute confidence in his skills that bordered on narcissism. In his youth, he challenged Atanvi for Mìreya's hand, treating her like a prize to be won, and suffered a humiliating defeat that became the first crack in his pride. Personal losses, particularly the death of his father and the necessity of fleeing his homeland with his mate and children to seek uturu from the very clan he once scorned, reshaped his worldview entirely, tempering his impulsiveness with hard-won empathy and a strong moral compass. Now a staunch advocate for wisdom over violence, he views combat as a last resort, preferring to resolve conflicts through counsel gained from painful experience. His relationship with the Samaya family, especially Atanvi and Mìreya, transformed from rivalry to respect—a journey that began with shame and ended in genuine admiration. This healing accelerated when his daughter Tulìpey fell in love with Rawìl, creating a bond between families that once stood opposed. Tsu'ong's relationship with his mate Ketxe is built on mutual redemption: two broken people choosing to be better together, finding peace far from the darkness of their pasts. During the Siege of the Falls, Tsu'ong proved his transformation complete, fighting not for glory but for the protection of his adopted home and the gentle kingdom that had given his family sanctuary. His Tipani combat techniques, once used in service of pride, now served the defense of others. In recognition of his journey from arrogance to humility, his wisdom in counsel, and his unwavering commitment to peace through strength, he was honored as **the Wise** among the Nine Spears, bearing the **Tempered Spear**, a weapon that symbolizes how pride, when cooled by experience and hardship, becomes unbreakable wisdom.


### Ketxe (Reze)
**Ikran**: Tìrol
**Birthday**: September 30

**Personality:**  Ketxe's true nature is layered and complex, belying her initially perceived gentleness. Born to the renowned **Tawkami clan**, known for their mastery of botanical alchemy, Ketxe diverged from her peers' healing focus to pursue a more... explosive path. While others in her birth clan cultivated medicines and salves, she became fascinated with the volatile compounds hidden within Pandora's flora, discovering which seed pods could create concussive blasts, which saps burned with colors that could blind, and which spores could create devastating chain reactions. Her "disappearance" from both her clan and from Tsa'rawm, her first love, was not the accident most believed. The truth, which she guards closely, is far darker: she was captured and controlled by Varang and the Mangkwan clan, her explosive expertise twisted into a weapon for their corrupted cause. Under Varang's manipulation, Ketxe conducted operations that still haunt her, her gentle nature warped by someone who saw her only as a tool. Her escape came at great cost: she fled into the wilderness, severing all connections to avoid putting anyone she loved in danger, carrying the shame of what she'd been forced to become. It was in this self-imposed exile that she found Tsu'ong, another soul broken by pride and loss. Together, they became each other's redemption: two people choosing to heal rather than destroy, to build rather than burn. Their bond is forged in mutual understanding: both know what it means to be someone you're ashamed of, and both chose to become better. When they sought uturu at Sheltering Falls with their children Ean'tu and Tulìpey, Ketxe had to face the ghosts of her past, including Tsa'rawm, the man whose heart she'd broken to protect him from Varang's reach, and the clan that had every reason to reject her.  Her past connection with Tsa'rawm creates underlying tension, particularly with Rewon and Sänume of the Haykxa family, though time and honest conversation have begun to heal those wounds. During their brief time together, she taught him some of her explosive techniques, knowledge he's sworn never to reveal, a secret that binds them in complicated ways even now. Her relationship with the Haykxa family remains careful, built on mutual respect and the shared understanding that the past cannot be changed, only learned from. Her mysterious interest in old RDA explosives adds an intriguing element to her character, sparking both curiosity and concern. She studies human demolitions not to copy but to improve upon them using Pandora's natural chemistry, believing the Sky People's crude methods pale compared to what Eywa's own garden can produce. Her gentle demeanor masks a mind constantly calculating blast radii and chemical reactions, seeing potential weapons where others see beauty, a duality that once made her valuable to Varang and now makes her indispensable to the Astikasya's defense. During the Siege of the Falls, Ketxe unleashed her full potential in defense of the home that had given her family sanctuary. Her explosive traps decimated Mangkwan forces, her knowledge of volatile compounds turning the battlefield into a maze of controlled chaos. She faced soldiers she once knew, setting aside guilt to protect the children playing in Sheltering Falls' mists, including her own. In recognition of her breaking free from Varang's control, her choice to use her dangerous gifts for protection rather than destruction, and her unwavering commitment to the clan that accepted her despite everything, she was honored as **the Unbound** among the Nine Spears, wielding the **Unleashed Spear**: a weapon that represents freedom from chains and the choice to fight on her own terms. Despite her past and her reserved nature, Ketxe's love for her children and her mate drives her to seek harmony within the broader community, showcasing the softer side beneath her enigmatic exterior. She channels her dangerous knowledge into protective measures, secretly establishing explosive defenses around the clan's perimeter that only she can navigate safely, her own twisted way of showing love. Her journey from controlled weapon to free warrior serves as proof that even those used as tools of destruction can choose to become guardians of life. Young warriors who train with Ketxe learn not just the chemistry of explosives, but the philosophy of controlled power, that true strength lies in choosing when to unleash devastation and when to hold back, that the most dangerous person is the one who fights by choice rather than compulsion. She remains vigilant, knowing that Varang's reach is long and her memory longer. But now, surrounded by family and protected by the Nine Spears, Ketxe finally allows herself to believe in safety. She is no longer the weapon someone else wields, she is Ketxe te Korhe Kilve'ite, the Unbound, and she fights for love.


### Tulìpey (Alhena Jeturk)
**Ikran**: Tsyìra
**Birthday**: March 15


**Personality:** Tulìpey presents a fascinating blend of tomboy toughness and hidden sensitivity, inheriting her father's fierce pride and her mother's calculated nature. Normally crass and direct like her twin brother, she transforms into a flustered, lovey-dovey mess around Rawìl, her longtime crush turned partner \- though this softness belies a sharp mind for strategy and clan politics that often catches others off guard. This duality in her personality often amuses and confuses those around her, though she carries herself with an underlying dignity that betrays her complex upbringing. Despite \- or perhaps because of \- the history between their families, she approaches her relationship with Rawìl with both fierce determination and tender vulnerability. Her extensive knowledge of clan politics and traditions belies her young age, making her a valuable asset in inter-clan relations \- a skill born from years of managing her own family's complicated dynamics. She's particularly protective of Notä, forming a strong bond with her despite their different backgrounds, often acting as a bridge between the younger girl's innovative spirit and more traditional clan expectations. While her father's warrior blood runs strong in her veins, she channels it differently \- using precision and knowledge as her weapons of choice, particularly in her study of healing arts under Sänrri's guidance. This tutelage under the current tsakarem is no mere apprenticeship; she is being formally groomed as Sänrri's successor, absorbing the spiritual arts and traditions to eventually step into the role of **tsakarem** (tsahìk-in-waiting) once Sänrri is elevated to **tsahìk**. Her skills in weaving and potion-making suggest a future role that bridges traditional warrior culture with more diverse clan needs, maintaining her family's fierce pride while seeking new ways to honor and evolve their traditions, much like her relationship with Rawìl represents a hope for unity between their families.

### Eantu (Alzir Jeturk)
**Ikran**: Pxayvi
**Birthday**: March 15

**Personality:** Eantu is a proud and adventurous young Na'vi, embodying the spirit of his Tipani heritage while maintaining that characteristic arrogance that makes him both endearing and infuriating to his peers. He's developed a complex dynamic with Rawìl and Anake \- deep respect mixed with constant competition, their trio infamous for missions that combine Tipani stealth with increasingly wild schemes. His relationship with Notä is particularly volatile, their bickering sessions legendary within the clan (much to everyone's amusement, as it mirrors their parents' dynamic perfectly). Despite his often jerky exterior, Ean'tu's loyalty to his friends is absolute, even if he shows it through challenges and competitive spirit rather than open affection. His tall stature and natural agility make him a promising hunter, though his perpetual need to prove himself often leads to increasingly reckless decisions. Like his father, he carries himself with perhaps too much pride, but unlike Tsu'ong, he channels it into a fierce protectiveness of his chosen circle \- even if he'd rather eat a banana fruit than admit it. His relationship with his twin sister Tulìpey is especially complex \- fiercely protective while also being the first to tease her about her obvious feelings for his best friend.
