The **Astikasya Clan** is renowned for their spiritual depth, innovative spirit, and sacred connection to Eywa. Once primarily skilled herbalists, a tragic event reshaped their collective identity, forcing them from ancestral forests to the Spires of the Clouded Forest where they evolved into formidable hunters, visionary artisans, and fearless defenders.

## Cultural Practices

### Laysrew (Free Dance)

**Translation:** "Free dance"

**Description:** Traditional ecstatic movement during celebrations, rites of passage, moments of collective release.

**Characteristics:**
- Full-body expression of energy, joy, communion
- Resembles human "mosh pit"
- No steps, no leaders
- Each dancer moves to own heartbeat and collective rhythm
- Accompanied by hand drums and bone flutes

**Types:**

**Regular Laysrew:**
- During bonding ceremonies
- Victory feasts
- After long mourning periods
- Sacred but unrestrained
- Safe outlet for wild emotion, grief, exuberance

**LaysrewtsyÃ¬p (Child-Safe Version):**
- Gentler form maintaining core values
- Free expression and rhythmic release
- Guided, playful environment
- Often first communal ritual for children
- Builds confidence and cultural belonging

**Philosophy:** 
- Participation always optional
- Few choose to stay still when drums begin
- Physical manifestation of connection to Eywa
- Release of emotion through movement
